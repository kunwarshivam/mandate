- **E2-10 Bound how far the news reader's receipt time may run ahead (DEC-2281 item 3).** The reader's host clock stamps each receipt, and a receipt stamped far in the future sets the news stream's last `knowledge_at` there; rule 285 then holds every later poll from it until the clock catches up. Compare each round's receipt with a trusted time (the scheduler's clock stream, or the previous receipt plus the time elapsed), never with the vendor's item times, which are legitimately old on a first poll, a quiet day, or a late-indexed item; refuse to journal and count a round past the bound, and test it with an injected clock.
