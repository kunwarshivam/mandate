- **E2-10 Bound the cell source's news tail, and notice a news stream shorter than its head (hardening, DEC-2291; #2945's review notes).** `CellSource` keeps every `NewsItemStored` row's outcome for the life of the process (`NewsTail::items`), so its memory grows with the news stream. Keep only the items a pass can still offer: those whose `knowledge_at` falls inside the research run's lookback. Drop older items from the map, while still reading each record at most once. Today, a store that answers no rows past the tail's head reads as "nothing new", even when the stream now ends before that head (a replaced or truncated stream). Instead, the source checks that the head's seq and digest are still on the stream. If they are not, it refuses the pass as `news_unreadable` and raises the mismatch alert, and never re-offers from the start. Items refused as Missing or Corrupt stay refused until restart, as now: that is the safe default (`AGENTS.md` rule 3), and the row does not change it. *Accepted when:* a test drives the tail through more items than the lookback holds and asserts that the map's size stays bounded and that no record is read twice. A test that shortens the stream below the head asserts the refusal and a single alert. Mutants on the diff show 0 missed.
