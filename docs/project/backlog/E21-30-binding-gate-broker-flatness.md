- **E21-30 X: the binding gate reads the broker's open orders and positions, not only the journal's** (lane X, DEC-2802 item 6). An untraded account's opening reads flatness from the account stream alone: an order or a position the broker holds that the platform never journaled is unseen until the next reconciliation stops the process being ready. Once the brokers serve their facts (G2b-2, #2561), read the broker's open orders and positions at the binding clock and hold the opening unless both are empty. Tests first: an external open order or position holds the opening.
