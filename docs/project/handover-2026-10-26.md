## The founder's goal

The whole product, built properly, no shortcuts or one-off "proofs":

1. The owner creates an agent in the web app (mandate envelope, confirm, deploy).
2. The agent works autonomously. The research agent uses the models via the model gateway and the
   agentic harness to find ideas inside the envelope.
3. It keeps deciding until it buys: signals, then the deterministic builder, then the gate, then
   autonomy (ASK/AUTO), then the executor, then the broker.
4. It monitors the position: marks, protection, risk.
5. It acts on news and events.
6. It decides to sell: signal, goal or thesis exits, and risk exits.
7. The owner sees everything in the web app and can approve, intervene or kill at any point.

The work runs on the managed cell (k8s: runtime, executor and manager as separate processes). The
founder chose this over a single-process shortcut, and infrastructure is part of the product.
Brokers: Alpaca paper, and Robinhood (the founder's own account, via the Robinhood connector,
tested by agents against `mandate-rh-sim` only).

Current target: a real end-to-end trade loop driven from the web app, in the local cell, proven by
the cell-demo-web browser test, then the rest of the lifecycle. Plan by dependency order, not by
calendar.
