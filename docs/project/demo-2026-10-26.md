# The demo, 2026-10-26: the local cell, from web/

Lane DR, from the cell runs on main through 2026-10-26 (the last at a107b02, ~13:20 UTC). Everything runs on the
local cell with paper, fixture brokers and recorded data only: no live credential, no real order
(`AGENTS.md` rule 8). The cell's own report (`cargo xtask cell demo --scenario demo`) is the
up-to-date list; this page says what to run, what you will see, and what is not yet real.

**Rehearse at 13:35Z, demo after it is green.** Lane DR runs the whole cell demo at 13:35 UTC on
Tuesday 2026-10-27, five minutes after the window opens, and reports its first stop; the founder's
demo follows once that rehearsal is green, later in the same window. Nothing about the loop can be
proved outside the window, so the rehearsal is where a failure is found and fixed, not the demo.
The in-session path has a test of its own that does not wait for the window:
`mandate-daemon`'s `the_demo_envelope_decides_asks_and_proposes_once_granted_in_session` runs the
deployed runtime's wiring over this envelope at a Tuesday-session clock and proves decide, ask,
grant and the proposal to the executor.

**The trade loop's window: Tuesday 2026-10-27, 13:30 to 20:00 UTC** (9:30 to 16:00 in New York).
The crossover decides only on a fresh output, and its output from a daily close stays fresh for
one day; openings trade only in the regular session. Tuesday's session is the first in which both
hold: Monday's close, the day before, is still fresh, and the market is open ("When the trade loop
can run" below). Outside the window the demo shows steps 1 to 3, 5 and 7, and step 4 up to the
model: each agent's stream holds its `ObservationRecorded` and its `ModelOutputRecorded`, whose
`expires_at` is already past, and no `DecisionMade` follows, since the runtime decides on no stale
output. The runtime records no separate "stale" event; the expired `expires_at` is the reason.

## Bring it up

On x86_64 Linux with docker, and the tools `bash .cursor/install.sh` installs (k3d, helm,
kubeconform), plus Node.js at `web/.nvmrc` and `npm ci` in `web/` for the browser part. Keep
ample disk free: in lane DR's sandbox runs on 2026-10-26, the kubelet evicted the cell's pods for
disk pressure with about 13.5 GB free (an observation of that machine, not a documented limit).

```bash
cargo xtask cell up                        # build the images, start k3d, install the journal, vault and workspace
cargo xtask cell demo --scenario demo      # the story's steps in the cell, then web/ at https://localhost:4317
cargo xtask cell down                      # when done
```

- Behind a TLS-inspecting proxy, set `MANDATE_CELL_CA_BUNDLE` to the proxy's CA bundle first.
- On a sandboxed VM that refuses a negative `oom_score_adj`, set `MANDATE_CELL_RESTRICT_OOM=1`
  before the cluster is created.
- `cargo xtask ci cell-demo` is the same scenario without the browser, as CI runs it.
- `cell demo` forwards the API to http://127.0.0.1:8787 and the stub sign-in issuer to
  127.0.0.1:8090, and signs `web/` in as the demo owner (`demo-owner`).

## When the trade loop can run

The crossover reads daily closes, and an output is fresh for at most one day (its horizon is the
pinned `max_output_age_s`, which the mandate schema caps at 86400; mandate spec §8.2). Openings
trade only in the regular session. So the runtime can decide and the order can fill only in a
regular session whose previous trading day closed within the last 24 hours: not on a Monday, whose
latest close is Friday's and already stale, nor on the day after a market holiday. While the US is
on daylight time, until 2026-11-01, that is 13:30 to 20:00 UTC, Tuesday to Friday; from then, 14:30
to 21:00 UTC; an early close shortens it.

The demo takes the conservative reading and shows the trade loop in such a session, tomorrow's.
Reading freshness in trading sessions instead would loosen a freshness rule, which only the founder
can decide (DEC-79).
