## Decision

1. **V-044:** tripwire ids are sorted and unique; a counted threshold is a whole number from 1 to
   1,000; a `realized_loss_usd` threshold is in whole cents and at most `capital.allocation_usd`.
2. **§9.2:** tripwires are classified on their own row, matched by `id`. Adding one, lowering a
   threshold, or `end_delegations` → `exits_only` is risk-reducing; removing one, raising a threshold,
   softening an action, or changing a metric is risk-increasing. The autonomy row leaves the path out.
3. **V-042 stands as written:** a version that removes or loosens a tripwire is risk-increasing, so it
   carries no delegation over.
4. **§7:** the compiler and templates may propose tripwires (`platform_proposed`, confirmed like any
   envelope field, V-020), as DEC-187 allows.

## Rationale

Every reducing change can only fire a tripwire sooner or hold more, which MI-31's fuzz checks against
the previous version on the same history. A tripwire's metric change restarts its count, so it is
treated as a removal plus an addition, and the removal is increasing.

## Alternatives considered

- Neutral for every tripwire change, as "just a safety net": a removal would then take effect without
  step-up.
