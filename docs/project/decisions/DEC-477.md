| **Story or claim** | W4 web redesign (claim #297), with [DEC-476](DEC-476.md) |

## Decision

The approved v2 mockup shows three things the brief does not yet allow. Until the founder decides,
the app builds the conservative reading of each.

1. **Approve and Skip on a request card in a thread or the copilot.** Proposed: the card carries
   both, with the same passkey, deadline and figures as D6, and the journal records the same
   events. Built meanwhile: the card links to the request, where the owner approves or skips with
   every line shown. Skip lowers risk and could come first on its own.
2. **Replies in the agent's voice** ("I'd like to buy 6 MSFT"). Proposed: a model's reply may be
   shown, quoted and named as model output, never with a button (PX-18). Built meanwhile: answers
   come from the record, written by code and labelled "From the record".
3. **Creating an agent inside the copilot.** Proposed: setup's conversation runs in the panel and
   ends at the same summary and passkey. Built meanwhile: the copilot hands the owner's words to
   setup (A0), where they wait unsent.

An owner request card from a message (D14: the order the builder would propose, the gate's verdict
and the autonomy outcome, before anything is sent) is also not built. Asking for an order gets a
refusal that says orders are not placed from a message. That is narrower than D14, and it adds no
risk.

## Rationale

Approving from a chat card puts an action that adds risk one tap from a conversation, away from
the screen built to show every line before it. Voice and in-panel setup change who appears to
speak and where an envelope is confirmed. Each is the founder's call under DEC-79. Each built
fallback lowers risk or adds none.

## Alternatives considered

- **Build the mockup as drawn:** rejected until the founder decides, for the reasons above.
- **Skip only, without Approve:** a possible first step for item 1, left to the founder.
