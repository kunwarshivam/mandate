## Decision

#363 cited DEC-217 in the code it merged, but no record of the decision was ever written. This
file records what that code does, as it stands on `main` after #364's corrections. Each item
names the files that cite DEC-217. Where this file and the code disagree, the code is what the
founder merged. Hex values are the sRGB values of the OKLCH steps in `web/src/lib/palette.ts`.

1. **Azure and Sun replaces Ink and Volt** ([DEC-214](../04-decision-log.md#decisions); `palette.ts`
   says "superseding DEC-214's volt").
   - **Hues.** Paper and ink stay at hue 255. Azure (258) is the brand: primary actions, links, the
     focus ring, your mandate's field, rules, markers and labels, the account's line, and the first
     asset series. Sun (90) is the highlight, always under ink type, and in light mode the account's
     pale field (`lapis-soft`, an approval card or the current tab).
   - **The lightness curve.** All ramps keep thirteen steps on one shared curve. The curve moves at
     six steps: L at 400 goes from 0.76 to 0.78, at 600 from 0.52 to 0.48, at 850 from 0.31 to 0.29,
     at 900 from 0.24 to 0.215, at 950 from 0.2 to 0.175, and at 975 from 0.16 to 0.135.
   - **Light key values.** The primary action and links, and the mandate's labels and focus ring,
     are azure-800 #043E89. The rules, the markers and the account's line are azure-600 #0858BC.
     The mandate's field and the selection are azure-200 #D0E3FE. The highlight is sun-300 #FED254,
     under ink-950 #0F1113.
   - **Dark key values.** The primary action, links and labels are azure-300 #C1D9FE, and the rules,
     markers and line are azure-400 #8BB9FD. The selection is azure-800 #043E89. The highlight is
     the same sun-300.
   - **The dark theme is near black.** Ink's dark steps are near neutral: chroma 0.008 or less at
     850 to 975, falling to 0.005. The card is ink-950 #0F1113 and the page is ink-975 #07080A. The
     mandate's field and the account's field are a raised ink-850 charcoal #292C2F, not navy, and
     azure stays in their bars, rules and labels.
   - **Tests.** `palette.test.ts` holds the shape:
     - azure, gain and loss form a near-triad, each pair 100 to 140 degrees apart;
     - sun sits within 20 degrees of azure's complement;
     - azure appears only in the azure tokens, and sun only in the sun tokens;
     - no second blue appears;
     - saturated azure is never a surface;
     - only ink type sits on the sun, at 7:1 or more.
   - `globals.css`, `tokens.ts` and `brand-palette.ts` carry the same values (`tokens.test.ts`).
2. **Gains and losses stay green and red**, with red moved from hue 12 to 36. Green (150) and red
   share lightness and chroma at every step. The default gain and loss move from step 700 to
   green-600 and red-600 in light, and from step 300 to green-400 and red-400 in dark.
   - **Colour-blind alternates.** These are unchanged: teal for a gain, raspberry for a loss in light
     and orange in dark.
   - **Tritanopia.** Tritanopia merges teal and blue at any depth. So the colour-blind gain against
     azure marks and the account's azure line is required to stay apart under deuteranopia and
     protanopia only, and is information only under tritanopia (`contrast-pairs.ts`,
     `palette.test.ts`).
   - **The `figure` pair kind.** A gain or a loss is a signed figure of weight 500 or more, with its
     sign and word beside it. It is measured as a new pair kind, `figure`: WCAG 4.5:1, APCA Lc 60
     (`contrast-pairs.ts`).
3. **Five asset series** (`series-1` to `series-5`): azure, sun, teal (192), sky (232) and the
   neutral grey.
   - Teal and sky come from azure's cool side, and no series uses the gain or loss hue.
   - In dark, `series-1` is azure-200, a step off the mandate's rules (#364).
4. **The hero line follows its change** (`time-chart.tsx`, `charts/options.ts`).
   - The line is green when the range is up, red when it is down, and ink when it is flat. It uses
     the colour-blind alternates when that preference is on.
   - It is smooth, 3 px wide, with no fill, read against a dotted rule at the range's opening value.
5. **An Assets section on Home, in place of Positions** (`holdings.ts`). It shows the account as
   each held instrument, the agents' cash, and the part no agent manages.
   - An instrument keeps one series colour on every chart of the dashboard, chosen by its share of
     the account: the largest is `series-1`, and so on up to `series-4`.
   - Cash is `series-5`, and the unmanaged part is `muted`.
6. **Each agent is a pixel owl** (`owl.tsx`, `owl-sprite.ts`, `globals.css`, the `/design` page).
   - **The sprite.** A 16 × 16 sprite whose ears, markings, feathers and blink rhythm come from the
     agent's ID (an FNV-1a hash), so an agent keeps its face on every screen.
   - **Feathers.** Feathers take `series-1` to `series-4`, never the gain or loss colour.
   - **Eyes.** The eyes show the agent's mode and nothing else: open when it trades, lidded on exits
     only, asleep when paused, shut when stopped.
   - **Motion.** An awake owl blinks on its own rhythm and its pupils follow the pointer a whole
     pixel at a time. A sleeping owl breathes, and a stopped owl is still. Every owl is still under
     reduced motion.
   - **No reaction to P&L.** Nothing about an owl follows P&L. The owls were later redrawn in the
     same grid ([DEC-452](DEC-452.md) item 4) and given fixed sizes (DEC-514).
7. **Sample news** (`fixtures/news.ts`). There is no news feed, so a News section shows sample
   headlines about the fixture instruments. They are labelled as sample text and are never a link.
   They report events and never say where a price is going.
8. **The brand's colours come from the palette** (`brand-palette.ts`):
   - Ink #0F1113;
   - Off-white #FBFDFE;
   - Azure #0858BC;
   - Deep azure #043E89;
   - Sun #FED254;
   - Night #07080A.
9. **#364's corrections**, part of what is recorded here.
   - In light, `primary` moved from azure-600 to azure-800. So no primary text sits in the mark
     colour of the mandate's rules.
   - In dark, `series-1` moved from azure-400 to azure-200. So a holdings bar never paints a block
     in the mandate's own azure.

Unchanged by this decision:
- The kill switch is still crimson and nothing else. Its ramp's hue moves from 27 to 20, so
  crimson-700 is now #9B0A28.
- The warning amber stays off every screen.
- The method stands: OKLCH, WCAG 2.2 AA with APCA in the tests, and simulated colour vision.
- [DEC-205](../04-decision-log.md#decisions) and [DEC-204](../04-decision-log.md#decisions)
  stand where neither DEC-214 nor this decision replaced them. `palette.ts` names only DEC-214 as
  superseded, so DEC-205's status is not changed here, although some of the values it listed
  (ink #14161A, crimson #9C0C12) now differ in the code.

`web/COLOR.md` holds the ramps, the tokens, the usage rules and the contrast and colour-vision
results.

## Rationale

The founder's reasons were not recorded. #363's description is a list of commit subjects, and the
one for the palette reads: "the Azure and Sun palette replaces volt: azure for actions and your
mandate, sun for the highlight under ink type, green and red for gains and losses, five asset
series, and a midnight dark mode". The only stated reasoning is the comment #363 put at the top of
`palette.ts`, written by the authoring session, not the founder:
- azure is the brand, with gain green and loss red "roughly a third of the wheel either side of
  it, a near-triad, so the three read as a set and never as each other";
- sun, "near azure's complement, is the one warm pop";
- the asset series come from azure's cool side "so a chart of holdings never borrows the gain or
  loss hue".

The founder may want to confirm that this record matches their intent.
