## Context

`mandate_executor::risk_state::AccountRiskFold` now reads marks, ticks, the agent's own fills and
fees (DEC-2393), and replays its own records (DEC-2399). Nothing deployed calls it, which leaves
three gaps:
- The cell's executor process (`mandate-executord`) never builds the fold.
- The executor's own fold refuses the records the risk fold returns (`RiskDayStarted` as a later
  slice, the rest as `E6-4`), so the first one appended would stop the process.
- The binding gate (lane X, DEC-2380 item 2) has no risk snapshot to read, so it holds every opening
  of an agent that has ordered.

## Invariants first

- The binding gate never judges an opening on a risk state the journal does not support: an
  unread or unreadable view holds the opening (`AGENTS.md` rule 3).
- No reading here holds an exit, a protective order, a risk exit, or a kill switch (rule 13).
- Every risk record is journaled before the next input is handed (rule 5, DEC-2307).
- A replay of the account stream reaches the view the running process had (DEC-2399).

## Decision

1. **The executor fold reads the risk fold's records as records** (C0). `RiskDayStarted`,
   `RiskLimitTriggered`, `RiskLimitLifted`, `HighWaterMarkReset`, `InstrumentRestrictionChanged`
   and `GoalCompleted` fold as records that move nothing in `ExecutorState`; the risk fold is
   their reader. An `AgentModeApplied` the risk fold returns is the executor's own originated mode
   record, which the executor fold already reads. `MandateVersionApplied` and `UniverseChanged`
   keep their stories (R2b, E17-3).
2. **One fold per deployed agent, opened flat at its deployment.** For each agent whose
   `AgentDeployed` the process follows and which is not stopped, the process opens an
   `AccountRiskFold` and folds the account stream into it from seq 1:
   - flat, at the `AgentDeployed`'s `event_time` floored to the whole second (`Opening.at`);
   - bound to the deployment's reviewed instrument (DEC-2380 item 5), and with its asset class;
   - on the confirmed version, validated on the latest `AccountSnapshotRecorded`'s equity
     (`mandate_gate_inputs::control`), with `inherited_loss_usd` the connection's loss carry from
     that same context (mandate spec §5.7);
   - with `mark_max_age_s` the reviewed quote staleness, in whole seconds.

   An agent whose fold cannot open (no confirmed version, no snapshot, a version the validation
   rejects) has an unreadable view (the #2120 review's follow-up): its openings are held, and its
   exits are not. No fold is opened for a stopped agent.
3. **Rows before the deployment are not the agent's.** A tick or a mark whose clock precedes the
   opening instant steps nothing. An own fill or fee there cannot be (an order names its agent
   only after the deployment), so it is a refusal to decide, and the view fails closed.
4. **The executor meets DEC-2399 item 3's contract.** After every committed batch, and after the
   start's replay before any other write, the process folds the batch's stored rows into every
   agent's fold and appends each fold's `owed()` as the next batch. An owed batch in doubt is
   offered again first, with the same event ids.
5. **The account stream's tick** (DEC-2307 item 5, DEC-2399 item 5). On each scheduler tick the
   process steps a copy of each fold. When any copy returns a record, or the tick crosses New York
   midnight, the process journals a `ClockAdvanced` copy on the account stream, its `clock` the
   tick's and its `causation_id` the scheduler's event, and then the records owed. A tick that
   returns nothing is not journaled, and changes no result (MI-13).
6. **The calendar** is `mandate-time`'s US-equities exchange calendar. For an equity it counts
   regular-session seconds and answers each instant's session. For crypto it counts every second
   and answers `crypto` (mandate spec §5.2).
7. **The binding gate's risk snapshot** (C1). `mandate_executor::risk_state::risk_snapshot(view)` is
   `Some((RiskSnapshot, AgentMode))` for a readable view, with E, H, E₀, C, L, the latched limits,
   the active rungs, the size factor and the mode copied from the risk state's snapshot. It is
   `None` for an unreadable view, or for a latched limit the gate's `LimitKey` cannot name. The
   gate then holds the opening, and an exit never reads it (DEC-2380 item 2's `input` answers
   `None` for every purpose that does not add risk).

## Consequences

- R4a, in `mandate-executor`: item 1, the opening and pre-deployment readings (items 2 and 3) on
  `AccountRiskFold`, the calendar (item 6), and `risk_snapshot` (item 7), tests first.
- R4b, in `mandate-executord`: the `RiskBook` (items 2, 4 and 5), with lane X wiring
  `risk_snapshot` into `AgentRisk`. It is tested on the executor process's fixture journal: the
  replay, crash between a batch and its records, midnight, and a fill tripping a rung.
- Rung actions (the flatten a `flatten_and_pause` asks for, the trim, cancelling openings on
  `exits_only`) are R5. Owner acknowledgments and versions are R2b; until they land, either
  makes the view unreadable, holding openings only.

## Alternatives considered

- **Open the fold at the account's head when the process first sees the deployment.** Refused: the
  head depends on when the process ran, so a replay would open it elsewhere. The deployment's
  instant is in the journal.
- **Journal every scheduler tick on the account stream.** Refused: one row a second per account
  adds nothing a replay needs (MI-13).
