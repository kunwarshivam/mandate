## Context

The mandate-version backtest has to apply splits and dividends (DEC-2245). The account fold
already books them (trading spec §8.5). The risk state, though, keeps its own quantity, cost basis,
realized P&L and mark (mandate spec §5), and neither `ref.py` nor `mandate-spec`'s `risk::Input`
folds a corporate action. If only the account applied a 2:1 split, the risk state would read the
next mark against the old quantity, halve E, and trip the drawdown ladder on nothing. Mandate spec
§5.2 already names the inputs; what it does not say is what folding them does.

## Invariants first

- A split moves E only by the residual's cash in lieu less the residual's value at the adjusted
  mark, within trading spec I3's mark-rounding bound. With no residual it moves E only by that
  rounding.
- A cash dividend moves E by exactly its receivable, round(Q × d, 2).
- No action moves E₀, C, N (dollars contributed) or L.
- Posting a receivable moves no figure.
- A split with no residual triggers or lifts only what a clock tick at the same instant would.

## Decision

1. **A split** folds as §8.5 books it, at §8.5's instant: Q', the residual basis R removed,
   cash in lieu round(f × p, 2) added to realized less R, and every mark replaced by
   round(mark × old ÷ new, 12, half_even). Cash in lieu is a receivable, so it is in E from
   application.
2. **A cash dividend** adds round(Q × d, 2) to realized on application, Q being the position
   then, which is the position after every fill traded before the ex-date. It is income on the
   ex-date (§8.5) and a receivable.
3. **`CashInLieuPosted` and `DividendPaid`** move a receivable to cash, so they change nothing the
   risk state holds.
4. **An action is not a quote.** §5.6's hard trigger reads only quotes, so an action never starts
   or completes a hard-trigger wait.
5. **H follows E**, as after every input (§5.2's evaluation order). So a dividend booked at 20:00
   before the ex-date, while the last mark is still the cum-dividend close, raises H by the
   dividend. The ex-date's lower mark then shows a drawdown of about the dividend's share of H.
   This only tightens, and MC-R29 shows it.
6. **Everything else stays out of scope** (§8.5: stock dividends, spin-offs, mergers, symbol
   changes, delistings). It is never folded.
7. **What it adds:** mandate spec v0.8 (§5.2's bullet, MI-33), `ref.py`'s three kinds, MC-R27 to
   MC-R29 with their title assertions, the fuzz's splits, dividends and postings with an
   independent oracle, and four seeded bugs in `mutants.py`. `mandate-spec` follows in a DEC-77
   pair (DEC-2245 item 6, slice 2b).

## Rationale

The risk state is a second ledger of the same position. Folding the action the way the account
does keeps the two in step, which is what §5.2's list of inputs already implies. Defining the change
in E from the action alone gives each limit a precise, testable reason to move: real cash only.

## Alternatives considered

- **Hold E, H and every mark still across an action, and re-base the position silently.** This
  hides real cash: a residual sold for less than it was worth would vanish from every limit.
- **Exclude corporate-action inputs from H.** It would loosen the drawdown by the dividend in the
  window between the receivable and the ex-date's mark. That is a loosening, so it is the founder's.
- **Leave the risk state unfolded and keep refusing actions in a backtest.** This is today's
  behavior; it leaves E4-4 slice 2 unbuildable.
