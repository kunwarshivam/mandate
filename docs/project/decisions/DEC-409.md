# DEC-409: A sequence's ladder that no longer climbs when its step is confirmed ends there

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-10-02 |
| **Decided by** | The stream K builder (`claude-code`) under DEC-79 and DEC-176: it makes the code do what `steps`' contract already says, and adds no risk |
| **Story or claim** | E7-4, issue [#485](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/485) |

## Decision

1. **The rule.** When the cancel of a sequence's stepped rung is confirmed while its ladder does
   not climb (its agent is paused or stopped, or its interval has reached its bound and
   alerted), the ladder ends at that confirmation. Its `stepping` is cleared in the fold. The
   unsold remainder is never sent as a next rung, and protection returns for what is left once
   no other exit there is working or waiting (§5.4, `settle`).
2. **Why the fold.** The agent's mode and the interval's bound are both folded from the
   journal, so the fold reads them at the confirmation exactly as the live executor did. A
   restart ends the same ladders, and no new event is needed.
3. **Lone ladders are unchanged** (DEC-260 (14)). A lone ladder's step was asked while it
   climbed, and no protection returns for its remainder, so a pause since then does not end it.
   A parked lone ladder still waits for the next open, and for its agent to resume. DEC-410
   counts its remainder.

## Rationale

`steps`' contract already says the confirmed cancel ends the sequence after a pause or a stop:
"the rung is not replaced, and protection returns for what is left". The code re-read that
contract at every later step, so a sequence that lingered (protection held back by other exits
still selling) resumed its ladder the moment the agent was unpaused. A new exit allowed in
between was sized against the whole position: #485's 11 against 10.

## Alternatives considered

- **Let the paused sequence resume, and count its remainder in the gate (DEC-410's rule).**
  Rejected for sequences. A stopped agent may never resume, and its phantom remainder would
  shrink an owner's exit for as long as it stayed stopped. Ending the ladder matches the
  existing contract and frees the room at once.
- **Journal a new "ladder ended" record.** Not needed: the fold has what the decision reads.
