## Context

Two gate holds stop new openings in one instrument while exits go on:
- `protection_unattributed`: a broker-created protective leg rests that no agent can be named for
  (trading spec §5.4, DEC-160 3(c)).
- `attribution_deficit`: the agents' lots exceed the position (§8.1, DEC-2849 I6).

Trading spec §9.1 left registering both codes "Proposed (founder)", and DEC-2849 I6 asks that the
owner be alerted once per deficit episode. Today `protection_unattributed` raises only an operator
alert (`mandate-alerts`), and `attribution_deficit` raises none, so an owner whose openings are held
is not told.

## Decision

1. **Founder ruling: register both.** Each is an owner alert kind of the notifications spec's
   closed set, class `safety`, push category `attention_needed`, recipients the owners. Generic
   text and opaque ids only (`AGENTS.md` rule 6): the `OwnerAlertSent` carries its `subject`, its
   `kind` and a `null` `owner_command`, as every other does; the push names no instrument, quantity,
   price, agent or account. The alert's subject names the hold; showing its instrument in the
   workspace app is backlog row E8-4h.
2. **The subject** is the `GateDecided` that holds an opening (`verdict` `hold`, `reason_code` the
   kind) and begins an episode. The executor writes the `OwnerAlertSent` in that record's batch
   (DEC-2514), never for a later hold of the same episode.
3. **An episode**, for one kind and one instrument, is a stretch in which at least one opening in
   the instrument is held for that reason: its last journaled `GateDecided` is a hold with that
   `reason_code`, and it is still held (not since allowed, denied or abandoned). It begins when a
   hold of that reason finds no other opening of the instrument held for it, and ends when none is.
   Everything it reads is journaled, so a replay finds the same episodes and owes no second alert.
   A held opening's reason is its last journaled hold: a re-check at a tick is not journaled, so an
   opening that stays held for another reason keeps its episode open, and a cause that appears
   only at such a re-check is alerted at the next opening held for it. Both err towards fewer
   alerts and never change a hold.
4. **Unchanged:** the holds themselves, their order in §9.1 step 4, exits (`AGENTS.md` rule 13),
   and the operator alert `mandate-alerts` raises for `protection_unattributed`.
5. **This reads DEC-2849 I6 two ways, both narrowing the alert and neither touching the gate.**
   I6's "alerted once per deficit episode" becomes once per episode of openings held for the
   deficit (item 3), since the fold cannot judge D against the increment (I8); and its "the alert
   names the instrument" becomes the alert's subject naming the hold, since rule 6 keeps the
   instrument out of every push. These replace I6's wording on those two points.

## Consequences

- Notifications spec v0.12 adds the two §3.2 rows; journal spec v0.111 grows §9.15's closed kind
  set from 36 to 38; trading spec v0.25's §9.1 says both codes are registered; the alerts read
  model's schema serves the two kinds.
- A code change follows, landed right after this one: the kinds in `mandate-notify`, the journal's
  notice classes, the alerts read and the web's table, and the executor's once-per-episode
  `OwnerAlertSent`, with a test that the payload carries only the subject, the kind and `null`.

## Alternatives considered

- **One alert per held intent.** Refused: a deficit can hold every opening of every agent in the
  instrument for days, and the founder asked for one per episode.
- **End an episode when D falls below the tolerance.** Refused: the executor's fold does not hold
  the instrument's increment, so the episode would not be a function of the journal alone (I8).
