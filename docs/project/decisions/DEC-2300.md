# DEC-2300: A Robinhood sell's fill is booked only at a price the owner attests from Robinhood's own confirmation; until then the agent stays paused

On Robinhood, a sell's fill has no published price. The order record carries its filled quantity
alone, the position is gone or reads zero, and no activity feed exists (connections spec U-R8;
the contract's `orders[]` fields are unpublished). DEC-1151 books an entry's fill from the record
and the position's average buy price. That works only for a buy on a flat ledger, and DEC-1151
item 4 forbids inventing a price. So the first sell, R0's protective stop or an owner exit, is
never booked. The ledger still holds the share the broker no longer does. The reconciliation pauses
the agent, and the next start fails closed (DEC-1151, DEC-1152). That is safe, but the
platform's books stay wrong, and nothing returns the agent to a correct state.

## Decision (proposed)

1. **The quantity from the broker, the price from the owner.** A sell's fill is booked only when
   all of these hold:
   - The order is ours, a sell that reduces risk (`protective`, `risk_exit`, `owner_exit` or
     `discretionary_exit`). "Ours" is DEC-1150 item 7's naming: a record carrying our `ref_id`,
     or carrying no `ref_id` and the broker order id the Robinhood connector holds for our key
     (the id the place answered, which it cancels by and restores from the journal).
   - It is a limit or stop-limit order, so it has a limit price (item 2).
   - Its broker record reports a filled quantity above zero and no more than its own quantity.
   - The ledger holds the instrument long at least that quantity, with no unattributed fill.
   - The broker lists the position reduced by exactly that quantity. A sold-out position may be
     absent or a zero row (DEC-902 item 3).

   The price is one the owner attests from Robinhood's own trade confirmation, in a stepped-up
   CLI gesture over the control stream. The gesture binds the client order id, the quantity, the
   price and the confirmation's own reference under a confirmation code (DEC-279 item 1).
2. **A sell with no limit is refused attestation.** A market sell, a stop-market, or any exit
   whose request carries no limit price is refused `attestation_unbounded`. Nothing is booked,
   and the agent stays paused as on `main`. On Robinhood this does not arise: its profile's only
   forms are limit openings and the one stop-limit protection (trading spec §5.2, DEC-529 item
   2), and every exit, an owner exit included, is a limit on the exit price ladder (§5.6). The
   refusal keeps the rule closed if a later profile adds an unlimited form.
3. **The attested price is bounded on both sides, never trusted.**
   - **Below: the order's limit.** A sell limit or stop-limit cannot fill below its limit, so a
     lower attested price is refused `attestation_below_limit`.
   - **Above: the unsafe side.** A too-high price would overstate proceeds and cash and flatter
     every loss measure. The ceiling is the collar's passive side on the sell (trading spec §9.6:
     the passive band, 20%) over the highest risk-mark-quality quote journaled for the
     instrument between the order's submission and the record that shows it filled. A
     risk-mark-quality quote is one that passed trading spec §8.2's checks when the run read it:
     0 < bid ≤ ask, spread within the profile's limit, fresher than the profile's staleness
     threshold. On a Robinhood run it is the run's own quote, Robinhood's `get_equity_quotes`,
     which DEC-529 item 12 checks against the IEX quote; Robinhood publishes no IEX or SIP feed
     of its own. The ceiling is that quote's ask × (1 + the passive band). With no such quote in
     the window, the ceiling is the order's limit, so the only price that can be attested is the
     limit itself. A price above the ceiling is refused `attestation_above_ceiling`.
   - **The cash read, as a second ceiling only.** When the account snapshot read after the fill
     shows the sale credited, the attested proceeds (price × quantity, less the configured fees)
     may not exceed the change in cash between the snapshots before and after it. Above that it
     is refused `attestation_above_cash`. The cash change never sets the price: settlement timing
     and other cash movements are unpublished (DEC-1151, U-R8), so it can only cap.

   Fees are the fee configuration's (DEC-958, DEC-960), never attested.
4. **Invariant: an attestation never improves a limit's measures.** The drawdown ladder, the
   daily loss and the lifetime loss floor (mandate spec §5.4, §5.5 and §5.7) read an attested sale at
   min(attested, limit). For a limit sell that is the limit, the worst price the order allowed.
   The cash ledger, realized P&L and the report book the attested price; the difference is
   reported as an unverified improvement. An attestation, however wrong within its bounds, can
   therefore never lift a limit, release a rung, or delay a trigger beyond what the limit alone
   would. A property test asserts it: over generated attested prices inside the bounds, every
   limit measure equals the measure computed with the sale at its limit.
5. **Until it is attested, nothing is booked and the agent stays paused.** That is the behavior
   on `main` today. The owner is alerted with generic text and opaque ids only (rule 6). Exits,
   protective orders and the kill switch are held only as rule 13 already allows. This adds no
   new hold.
6. **Booked once; a second attestation is refused.** The attestation is an
   `OwnerCommandIssued`-style control record whose `event_id` derives from the client order id
   and the filled quantity (DEC-290). The journal's append protocol books it once: a retry of the
   same draft returns `AlreadyCommitted`, and a draft with the same id and any other content is
   an `IdempotencyConflict` (journal spec §5.1 item 1). So once a sale is attested and booked, an
   attestation of the same fill at another price is refused `attestation_conflict` and nothing
   is appended. The booked fill's id is `attested:<client order id>:<filled quantity>`, which the
   executor's fold books once, as DEC-1151 item 2 does for `record:` ids. The reconciliation
   books it before it compares positions, as DEC-1151 item 3 does.
7. **What this needs, in order:**
   - a journal spec PR (protected, ES-22) for the attestation's record and its rule;
   - then DEC-77 pairs in `mandate-executor` (the booking) and `mandate-cli` (the gesture), with
     zero missed mutants;
   - and a line in the R0 runbook: after a sell, the founder attests the price from the
     confirmation before the next run.

## Rationale

Robinhood's confirmation is the only published source for a sale's price, and the owner already
holds it. Taking the quantity from the broker and only the price from the owner keeps the
broker's word wherever it exists. The bounds in item 3 mean a typo or a careless attestation
cannot record a fill the order could not have had, and item 4 means even an attestation inside
them moves no limit. Until the attestation, nothing changes from
`main`: the agent stays paused, so this decision cannot add risk before it is built, and once
built it only lets an attested, bounded fill through.

## Alternatives considered

- **Book at the order's limit as a worst-case price, flagged provisional** (the next most
  conservative). It understates proceeds and cash, so it never overstates buying power, and it
  needs no owner step. But it records a price nobody reported, against DEC-1151 item 4. Realized
  P&L would be wrong by the fill's improvement over the limit. A later true-up needs a correcting
  event the journal does not have. Recommended second: if the founder prefers no manual step, take
  this with item 2's bound and an owner-visible "provisional" mark.
- **Derive the price from the change in `cash`.** The platform's model credits a sale's proceeds
  at once. Whether Robinhood's `cash` does so before settlement is unknown (DEC-1151, "A sale's
  cash on Robinhood"), and dividends and deposits have no feed (U-R8). That makes the arithmetic
  ambiguous, and rule 3 refuses ambiguity.
- **Add `get_equity_tax_lots` or a P&L tool to the allowlist.** Their answer fields are
  unpublished. Adding a tool changes the pinned contract (M2) and the allowlist (CN-8's
  reasoning), and it waits for Robinhood's written answer to U-R8.
- **Keep failing closed with no way back** (status quo). It is safe, but the books stay wrong and
  the agent can never resume without a hand edit to the journal, which nothing allows.
