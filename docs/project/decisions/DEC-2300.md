# DEC-2300: A Robinhood sell's fill is booked only at a price the owner attests from Robinhood's own confirmation; until then the agent stays paused

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Proposed (founder-reserved, DEC-79: live-money accounting. Until the founder rules, the code stays as it is on `main`: a Robinhood sell's fill is never booked, the reconciliation finds the position unexplained, and the agent stays paused, which adds no risk, `AGENTS.md` rule 3) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-18 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | Proposed by lane LV (Claude Code) for the founder |
| **Story or claim** | E7-26, E7-6; DEC-1152's "Not closed here"; the [R0 rehearsal](../tasks/r0-rehearsal.md)'s step 2t; claim #1371 |

On Robinhood, a sell's fill has no published price. The order record carries its filled quantity
alone, the position is gone or reads zero, and no activity feed exists (connections spec U-R8;
the contract's `orders[]` fields are unpublished). DEC-1151 books an entry's fill from the record
and the position's average buy price. That works only for a buy on a flat ledger, and DEC-1151
item 4 forbids inventing a price. So the first sell, R0's protective stop or an owner exit, is
never booked. The ledger still holds the share the broker no longer does. The reconciliation pauses
the agent, and the next start fails closed (DEC-1151, DEC-1152). That is safe, but the
platform's books stay wrong, and nothing returns the agent to a correct state.

## Decision (proposed)

### What is booked, and from what

1. **The quantity from the broker, the price from the owner.** One attestation books one sale.
   It is accepted only when all of these hold:
   - The order is ours: a record carrying our `ref_id`, or carrying no `ref_id` and the broker
     order id the Robinhood connector holds for our key, the id the place answered, which it
     cancels by and restores from the journal (DEC-1150 item 7).
   - It is a sell that reduces risk: `protective`, `risk_exit`, `owner_exit` or
     `discretionary_exit`.
   - It has a limit price: a limit or a stop-limit (item 2).
   - Its broker record is terminal (`filled`, or `cancelled` or `expired` with a filled quantity)
     and reports a filled quantity above zero and no more than the order's quantity. The
     attestation names that final cumulative quantity. A partial fill is attested once, when its
     order ends, never step by step, so one order has one attestation.
   - The ledger holds the instrument long at least that quantity, with no unattributed fill in it.
   - The broker lists the position reduced by exactly that quantity. A sold-out position may be
     absent or a zero row (DEC-902 item 3).

   The price is one the owner attests from Robinhood's own trade confirmation, in a stepped-up
   CLI gesture over the control stream. The gesture binds the client order id, the quantity, the
   price and the confirmation's own reference under a confirmation code (DEC-279 item 1).
2. **A sell with no limit is never booked.** A market sell, a stop-market, or any exit whose
   request carries no limit price is refused `attestation_unbounded`. On Robinhood this does not
   arise: its profile's only forms are limit openings and the one stop-limit protection (trading
   spec §5.2, DEC-529 item 2), and every exit, an owner exit included, is a limit on the exit
   price ladder (§5.6). The refusal keeps the rule closed if a later profile adds an unlimited
   form.

### The bounds

3. **Below: the order's limit price.** A sell limit or stop-limit cannot fill below its limit.
   For a stop-limit the bound is its limit price, never its stop trigger. A lower attested price
   is refused `attestation_below_limit`.
4. **Above: the unsafe side.** A too-high price overstates proceeds and cash. The ceiling reads
   the quotes the run journaled for the instrument, each carried in the account stream's
   `GateDecided.quotes_used` (`bid`, `ask`, `as_of`, `feed`; journal spec §9.5), never
   `MarkUpdated`, which holds no bid and ask.
   - **The window.** From the order's `OrderSubmitted` to the first broker record that shows any
     of it filled. The window is cut there: a quote after the fill cannot have priced it.
   - **Which quotes count.** Only one that was risk-mark quality when the run read it (trading
     spec §8.2): 0 < bid ≤ ask, spread within the profile's limit, fresher than the profile's
     staleness threshold at its `as_of`. A crossed, wide, stale or last-trade-only quote does not
     count.
   - **The ceiling, the founder's choice (question 2 below).**
     - (a) *Recommended:* the highest bid among those quotes. A long's risk mark is the bid
       (§8.2), and a sell that fills above the best bid seen while it worked is not credible.
     - (b) The highest ask among them, with no band. It is looser, and it admits a fill inside
       the spread the bid would refuse.

     §9.6's 20% passive band is not used: it prices resting limit orders, not fills. With no
     counting quote in the window the ceiling is the order's limit, so only the limit can be
     attested. Above the ceiling is refused `attestation_above_ceiling`.
5. **The cash read: a second ceiling, never the price.** When an account snapshot read after the
   fill shows the sale credited, the attested proceeds (price × quantity, less the configured
   fees) may not exceed the change in cash between the last snapshot before the fill and that
   one; above it is refused `attestation_above_cash`. With no such snapshot there is no cap:
   Robinhood credits settled cash at T+1, and its timing and other cash movements are unpublished
   (DEC-1151, U-R8). A later reconciliation whose ledger cash and broker cash disagree pauses the
   agent through the existing cash band (DEC-146), whatever an attestation said.

   Fees are the fee configuration's (DEC-958, DEC-960), never attested.

### Nothing a risk read sees moves

6. **One shadow figure, `E_lim`.** Each booked attestation carries an unverified improvement
   u = (attested − limit) × quantity, never negative (item 3). U is the sum of the unverified
   improvements, and E_lim = E − U; cash_lim = cash − U likewise. **Every read that bounds risk
   reads E_lim**; only the presentation of cash and P&L reads the booked figure. The closed list
   of readers, each pinned by item 9's property test:

   | Reader | Reads |
   |---|---|
   | Drawdown ladder and high-water mark (mandate spec §5.5) | E_lim |
   | Daily loss (§5.4) and breach confirmation (§5.6) | E_lim |
   | Lifetime loss floor (§5.7) | E_lim |
   | Loss carry at retirement, max(0, N − E) (§3.1, `on_complete: release`) | E_lim |
   | Goal completion: `accumulate` and `profit_stop`, E − C ≥ level × C (§3.1) | E_lim |
   | Position sizing, `scale_sizes`, order size and exposure (§5.3) | E_lim |
   | Capital base and allocation changes (§5.1) | E_lim |
   | The gate's buying power and cash checks, after T+1 settlement included (trading spec §9) | cash_lim |
   | The surveillance and aggregate-flow reports (E17-6) | E_lim |
   | The cash ledger as shown, realized and total P&L as shown, statements (trading spec §8.2) | booked |

   A reader not on the list reads E_lim until a decision moves it. So an attestation, however
   wrong within its bounds, can never lift a limit, release a rung, delay a trigger, enlarge an
   order, complete a goal, lighten a carry, or add buying power beyond what the sale at its limit
   would.
7. **When an improvement stops being unverified.** Only one thing clears it: a later account
   snapshot whose settled cash shows the sale's booked proceeds (the cash increase across the
   settlement date at least the booked proceeds less fees, with no other cash movement the
   ledger cannot explain). The reconciliation then journals the improvement verified, and it
   leaves U. Time alone never clears it. Under the cases the review named:
   - **Partial fills:** one attestation per order, at its end (item 1), so there is one u.
   - **A later buy:** U is unchanged; the buy books at its own fill.
   - **Retirement:** the carry reads E_lim (item 6), so an unverified improvement makes the carry
     larger, never smaller. It is not carried to the next agent as a gain.
   - **Withdrawals:** they read cash_lim, so unverified cash cannot be withdrawn.
   - **Replay:** U is the fold of the attestation records less the verification records, both
     journaled, so it is derived from the journal alone.

   Whether it clears at all, or stays unverified for good, is the founder's (question 3).

### Refusals, idempotency, resumption

8. **Every refusal appends nothing, and the agent stays paused** (or `exits_only` where it
   already was). The closed list:

   | Code | When |
   |---|---|
   | `attestation_not_ours` | No record, or a record that is not ours (item 1) |
   | `attestation_not_reducing` | The order's purpose does not reduce risk |
   | `attestation_unbounded` | The order has no limit price (item 2) |
   | `order_not_terminal` | The broker record has not ended |
   | `attestation_quantity` | Quantity zero, above the order's, or not the record's final cumulative quantity |
   | `ledger_not_long` | The ledger is not long that quantity, or holds an unattributed fill |
   | `position_not_reduced` | The broker position is not reduced by exactly that quantity |
   | `attestation_below_limit` | Below the limit price (item 3) |
   | `attestation_above_ceiling` | Above the ceiling (item 4) |
   | `attestation_above_cash` | Above the cash cap (item 5) |
   | `step_up_missing` | The gesture's step-up is missing or stale |
   | `code_invalid` | The confirmation code is wrong or past its deadline |
   | `attestation_conflict` | The order is already attested at another price (item 9) |

   The refusal itself changes no mode. After a booking the reconciliation runs clean, and the
   agent resumes only by the owner's `resume`, an owner command as today; a booking never
   resumes it.
9. **A new event type, booked once.** The attestation is `SaleAttested`, a new control-stream
   event type: DEC-290 item 1 closes `OwnerCommandIssued` to its listed commands. Its event id
   departs from DEC-290 item 4's derivation, recorded here: it is derived from the control
   stream, the type and the client order id alone, not the head or the payload, so every
   attestation of one order is one event id. Journal spec §5.1 item 1 then books it once:
   - The CLI's retries inside one invocation send identical bytes, and get `AlreadyCommitted`.
   - A fresh re-run carries a new `submitted_at` and step-up, so its bytes differ and the append
     answers `IdempotencyConflict` even at the same price. The CLI then reads the stored event:
     the same quantity and price is reported as already booked, with no error; any other price
     is refused `attestation_conflict`.

   The booked fill's id is `attested:<client order id>:<quantity>`, which the executor's fold
   books once, as DEC-1151 item 2 does for `record:` ids, and the reconciliation books it before
   it compares positions (DEC-1151 item 3).

### Tests, and what this needs

10. **Every "never" and "always" has a named test with an independent oracle, each shown to
    catch a seeded bug:**
    - `an_attested_price_outside_its_bounds_is_refused`: the oracle recomputes the floor, the
      window and the ceiling from the journaled quotes itself. Seeded bugs: the stop as the
      floor, a post-fill quote counted, the ask used where the bid is chosen.
    - `a_sale_without_a_limit_is_never_booked`.
    - `one_order_is_booked_once_under_any_retry_order`: generated orders of retries, re-runs
      and conflicting prices; a separate accumulator counts the bookings.
    - `every_refusal_appends_nothing_and_leaves_the_agent_paused`: over the closed list.
    - `ours_is_the_ref_id_or_the_held_broker_id`: the DEC-1150 item 7 cases, with a lookalike id.
    - `every_risk_reader_reads_e_lim`: over item 6's closed list. The oracle computes each
      measure with the sale at its limit and asserts equality. Seeded bug: the carry reading E.
    - The fuzz varies stale, crossed, wide, last-trade-only and missing quotes, cash snapshots
      present or absent, early or late against settlement, and partial versus whole fills.
11. **What this needs, in order:**
    - a journal spec PR (protected, ES-22): `SaleAttested` and its verification record, their
      rules, and the reconciliation's verification;
    - a mandate spec PR (protected): E_lim and item 6's readers;
    - then DEC-77 pairs in `mandate-executor` (the booking and U), `mandate-spec` (E_lim's
      readers) and `mandate-cli` (the gesture), with zero missed mutants;
    - and a line in the R0 runbook: after a sell, the founder attests the price from the
      confirmation before the next run.

### The founder's three choices

- **The price.** Book a Robinhood sale at the price you attest from Robinhood's confirmation,
  bounded and kept out of every risk figure until verified (recommended). Or book it at the
  order's limit, the worst price it allowed, with no owner step and no attested figure at all.
- **The ceiling.** Cap the attested price at the highest bid seen while the order worked
  (recommended), or at the highest ask, which is looser.
- **Clearing "unverified".** Let a later settled-cash read verify the improvement (recommended).
  Or keep every attested improvement unverified for good, so risk figures always use the limit
  price.

## Rationale

Robinhood's confirmation is the only published source for a sale's price, and the owner already
holds it. Taking the quantity from the broker and only the price from the owner keeps the
broker's word wherever it exists. The bounds in items 3 to 5 mean a typo or a careless attestation
cannot record a fill the order could not have had, and item 6 means even an attestation inside
them moves no figure a risk read sees. Until the attestation, nothing changes from
`main`: the agent stays paused, so this decision cannot add risk before it is built, and once
built it only lets an attested, bounded fill through.

## Alternatives considered

- **Book at the order's limit as a worst-case price, flagged provisional** (the next most
  conservative). It understates proceeds and cash, so it never overstates buying power, and it
  needs no owner step. But it records a price nobody reported, against DEC-1151 item 4. Realized
  P&L would be wrong by the fill's improvement over the limit. A later true-up needs a correcting
  event the journal does not have. Recommended second: if the founder prefers no manual step, take
  this with an owner-visible "provisional" mark; it is the first choice's alternative above.
- **Derive the price from the change in `cash`.** The platform's model credits a sale's proceeds
  at once. Whether Robinhood's `cash` does so before settlement is unknown (DEC-1151, "A sale's
  cash on Robinhood"), and dividends and deposits have no feed (U-R8). That makes the arithmetic
  ambiguous, and rule 3 refuses ambiguity.
- **Add `get_equity_tax_lots` or a P&L tool to the allowlist.** Their answer fields are
  unpublished. Adding a tool changes the pinned contract (M2) and the allowlist (CN-8's
  reasoning), and it waits for Robinhood's written answer to U-R8.
- **Keep failing closed with no way back** (status quo). It is safe, but the books stay wrong and
  the agent can never resume without a hand edit to the journal, which nothing allows.
