# DEC-2300: A Robinhood sell's fill is booked only at a price the owner attests from Robinhood's own confirmation; until then the agent stays paused

On Robinhood, a sell's fill has no published price. The order record carries its filled quantity
alone, the position is gone or reads zero, and no activity feed exists (connections spec U-R8;
the contract's `orders[]` fields are unpublished). DEC-1151 books an entry's fill from the record
and the position's average buy price. That works only for a buy on a flat ledger, and DEC-1151
item 4 forbids inventing a price. So the first sell, R0's protective stop or an owner exit, is
never booked. The ledger still holds the share the broker no longer does. The reconciliation pauses
the agent, and the next start fails closed (DEC-1151, DEC-1152). That is safe, but the
platform's books stay wrong, and nothing returns the agent to a correct state.

## Decision (proposed)

1. **The quantity from the broker, the price from the owner.** A sell's fill is booked only when
   all of these hold:
   - The order is ours (DEC-1150 item 7's naming), a sell that reduces risk (`protective`,
     `risk_exit`, `owner_exit` or `discretionary_exit`).
   - Its broker record reports a filled quantity above zero and no more than its own quantity.
   - The ledger holds the instrument long at least that quantity, with no unattributed fill.
   - The broker lists the position reduced by exactly that quantity. A sold-out position may be
     absent or a zero row (DEC-902 item 3).

   The price is one the owner attests from Robinhood's own trade confirmation, in a stepped-up
   CLI gesture over the control stream. The gesture binds the client order id, the quantity, the
   price and the confirmation's own reference under a confirmation code (DEC-279 item 1).
2. **The attested price is bounded, never trusted.** A sell limit or stop-limit cannot fill
   below its limit. So an attested price below the order's limit is refused before anything is
   appended. A price more than the rule set's collar above the order's last quote is refused
   too. Fees are the fee configuration's (DEC-958, DEC-960), never attested.
3. **Until it is attested, nothing is booked and the agent stays paused.** That is the behavior
   on `main` today. The owner is alerted with generic text and opaque ids only (rule 6). Exits,
   protective orders and the kill switch are held only as rule 13 already allows. This adds no
   new hold.
4. **The booked fill's id** is `attested:<client order id>:<filled quantity>`. It is booked once
   however often it is read (journal spec §5.2). The reconciliation books it before it compares
   positions, as DEC-1151 item 3 does.
5. **What this needs, in order:**
   - a journal spec PR (protected, ES-22) for the attestation's record and its rule;
   - then DEC-77 pairs in `mandate-executor` (the booking) and `mandate-cli` (the gesture), with
     zero missed mutants;
   - and a line in the R0 runbook: after a sell, the founder attests the price from the
     confirmation before the next run.

## Rationale

Robinhood's confirmation is the only published source for a sale's price, and the owner already
holds it. Taking the quantity from the broker and only the price from the owner keeps the
broker's word wherever it exists. The bound in item 2 means a typo or a careless attestation
cannot record a fill the order could not have had. Until the attestation, nothing changes from
`main`: the agent stays paused, so this decision cannot add risk before it is built, and once
built it only lets an attested, bounded fill through.

## Alternatives considered

- **Book at the order's limit as a worst-case price, flagged provisional** (the next most
  conservative). It understates proceeds and cash, so it never overstates buying power, and it
  needs no owner step. But it records a price nobody reported, against DEC-1151 item 4. Realized
  P&L would be wrong by the fill's improvement over the limit. A later true-up needs a correcting
  event the journal does not have. Recommended second: if the founder prefers no manual step, take
  this with item 2's bound and an owner-visible "provisional" mark.
- **Derive the price from the change in `cash`.** The platform's model credits a sale's proceeds
  at once. Whether Robinhood's `cash` does so before settlement is unknown (DEC-1151, "A sale's
  cash on Robinhood"), and dividends and deposits have no feed (U-R8). That makes the arithmetic
  ambiguous, and rule 3 refuses ambiguity.
- **Add `get_equity_tax_lots` or a P&L tool to the allowlist.** Their answer fields are
  unpublished. Adding a tool changes the pinned contract (M2) and the allowlist (CN-8's
  reasoning), and it waits for Robinhood's written answer to U-R8.
- **Keep failing closed with no way back** (status quo). It is safe, but the books stay wrong and
  the agent can never resume without a hand edit to the journal, which nothing allows.
