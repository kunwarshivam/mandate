## Decision

1. **The account and the agents lead Home's main column** (supersedes DEC-467 item 1). First the
   account figure, then the agents (a band each on desktop with its paper P&L and disclosure, a
   row with its headroom on a phone), then Assets on desktop. Needs you stays the first thing in
   the document and on a phone's screen.
2. **The latest decisions sit beside the money, not ahead of it** (supersedes DEC-467 item 2). On
   desktop the 20rem rail holds Needs you, then the latest four decisions with their tally ("The
   latest 4 decisions: …") and All decisions. A phone's Home reads Needs you, Account equity,
   Agents, then ends on the latest three decisions with their tally and All decisions. The
   timeline is narrowed to fit a rail: the agent's owl on a hairline, the action, the verdict in
   words, the agent, the purpose and the time on one line, and one line of why (the gate's rule
   when it held the action, otherwise what happened next). The audit keeps every decision.
3. **The account figure is the page's hero again** (supersedes DEC-467 item 3). Its chart is
   260px on desktop and 180px on a phone (unchanged, so the range picker clears the tab bar), and
   it opens on today (1D), the question an owner checks first. The Home balance is the 76px display
   figure. Both hero lines stay 3px.

DEC-467 items 4 to 10 are unchanged.

## Rationale

A decision has already been taken by the time Home shows it; what an owner opens Home to learn is
whether anything needs them and how their money is doing. DEC-467 put the record ahead of the
result. The record still matters, so it keeps a place on Home, but beside the money in the rail
on desktop and after it on a phone, one link from the full audit.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the decisions first and enlarge the account figure in the rail.** Rejected: the money
  would still be second in the reading order.
- **Drop the decisions from Home.** Rejected: what the agents did and whether it was allowed is
  what only this product shows, and four lines in the rail cost the money nothing.
- **Open on the week (DEC-467's 1W).** Rejected: today's change is the first question; the week
  is one press away.
