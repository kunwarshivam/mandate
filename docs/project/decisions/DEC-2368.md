## Context

[DEC-2362](DEC-2362.md) sizes the protection the executor places again after an owner's release
(`protection.rs`'s `protectable`): the long position less the released shares the owner still
holds, read as `held − min(Σ released, held − Σ agents' attributed lots)`. Its item 5 named an edge
and left it to the founder. The owner's holding is read as the long position less the agents' own
lots, so an agent's fill the fold could not attribute reads as the owner's. Once the owner sells
released shares, that fill can fall out of the agent's protection. With 10 bought, 2 more filled
unattributed, 4 released and 3 of those sold by the owner, the executor covers 6 of the agent's 8.
No sizing rule removes the edge without the opposite error: covering released shares the owner
still holds whenever the attributed lots under-count.

## Invariants first

1. The agent's protection never covers shares the owner released and still holds (DEC-2362).
2. Outside the edge, the sizing is unchanged: every share the executor can read as the agent's is
   covered.
3. An under-cover the edge causes is never silent: it is journaled, and the owner is alerted once
   per episode.
4. A replay journals and alerts exactly as an unbroken executor would.

## Decision

1. **Under-cover in the edge (the founder's option A).** Protection stays sized as DEC-2362 items
   1 to 4 say: what is protectable, never the owner's released shares. The strict reading, sizing
   to the agents' attributed lots alone, is not taken: it would leave an agent's unattributed fill
   uncovered even with no owner sale, which adds risk.
2. **Journal the gap.** Each time the executor sizes protection for an instrument (§5.4's sequence
   end, `re_cover`, and the buffer day's re-placement), it computes the gap: the shares that may be
   the agents' and are not covered. That is the agents' attributed lots (net of their releases, as
   `protectable` reads them) plus the agents' unattributed bound, less what the placement covers,
   floored at zero and capped at the shares it leaves uncovered (`held − protectable`). The
   unattributed bound is, summed over each agent buy in the instrument in a state other than
   `Rejected` or `Abandoned`, the fill the broker reported for it (`OrderStateChanged`'s
   `filled_qty`, the fold's `reported_filled`) less the fill applied to it, floored at zero: the
   shares the broker says the agent bought that no fill update attributed to it. A buy still
   working, or ended with nothing reported filled, adds nothing, so a working entry never reads as
   a gap. It is narrower than the bound `ExecutorState::holds_lots` reads for DEC-2347 item 3.6,
   which counts every buy not wholly applied at its whole quantity and is right for an exit that
   may only wait longer, not for a record that alerts. In the context's example the gap is 6 + 2 − 6 = 2; with the same unattributed 2 and
   no owner sale, the placement covers 8, and the gap is 6 + 2 − 8 = 0. A gap above zero is
   journaled, after the
   placement's own records, as `ProtectionChanged` with the new action `protection_gap`, its
   `instrument_id`, and `qty` the gap. A placement of nothing (all protectable shares released)
   journals its gap the same way.
3. **Alert once per episode (the agent's choice, DEC-176).** The owner is alerted with the executor
   key `protection_gap`, under the notifications spec's `protection` kind (`attention_needed`), the
   first time a placement finds a gap for an instrument. The alert names the record only (rule 6).
   The fold notes the instrument on `protection_gap` and clears the note on the instrument's next
   `placed`. A placement that finds a gap while the note stands journals it without alerting again.
   The episode ends when a later placement covers the shares, that is, finds no gap.
4. **Nothing else changes.** No order, size, price, hold or pacing changes. The record and alert
   are disclosure only.

## Landing

The journal spec (v0.83) and the notifications spec change first, alone (the spec guard). Then a
tests PR (`mandate-journal`'s rules for the new action, and the executor's tests, pending E6-4)
and an implementation PR, each with zero missed mutants and an independent review.

## Alternatives considered

- **Size strictly to the agents' attributed lots.** Rejected (item 1): it under-covers outside the
  edge.
- **Cover the owner's released shares in the edge.** Rejected by the founder: rule 13 and DEC-2354
  invariant 1 keep the agent's orders off the owner's shares.
- **Alert on every placement with a gap.** Rejected: the same fact repeats, as DEC-2355 found for
  the kept OCO.
- **Journal only, no alert.** Rejected: an under-protected position is something the owner can act
  on, and a once-per-episode alert only adds visibility.

## Consequences

- Journal spec v0.83: `ProtectionChanged`'s `action` gains `protection_gap`; rule 42 makes `qty`
  non-null on it.
- Notifications spec: the `protection` kind gains the executor key `protection_gap`.
- `mandate-journal`: the action and rule 42's new arm.
- `mandate-executor`: the gap's computation and record wherever protection is sized; the fold's
  note; the alert.
- DEC-2362 item 5 is decided by this record.
