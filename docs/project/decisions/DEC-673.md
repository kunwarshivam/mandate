# DEC-673: held_mismatch takes an explicit anchor and carries the expected hold

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-10-08 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | The L2 builder agent, under DEC-176 on the coordinator's design ruling of the #829 review: each item only refuses more, except item 4, which keeps today's answer for streams written before §9.11 and is the coordinator's to confirm |
| **Story or claim** | E10-15, claim #749; DEC-672; journal spec §9.11, §11 |

## Decision

1. **The anchor is an input.** `held_mismatch` is given the stored chain's state before the range
   by its caller: no version-2 `AgentModeChanged` before it, the `held` the last one carried (a
   hold, a lift, or a carried copy), or unknown. The check never takes a range's own first record
   as its anchor.
2. **Unknown fails closed for a copy.** With no anchor, the range's first version-2
   `AgentModeChanged` that is not a hold or a lift is reported as having no anchor.
3. **The carried hold is the expected one,** derived from the reasons: a hold sets it, a lift clears
   it, and every other record keeps it whatever it wrote. A hold or lift whose `held` contradicts
   its reason is reported, and a wrong `held` on a copy does not move what the next copy is judged
   against.
4. **An unknown anchor does not judge version-1 records before the range's first version 2.** Every
   stream written before §9.11 is all version 1, so failing them closed would report every tail
   verification of an existing stream, a SEV-1 that pauses its agents (§11) and holds their exits.
   A version 1 after an unseen version 2 is caught by the full-chain run, which is always anchored,
   and by any anchored range.

## Rationale

The second review of #804 showed a range that trusted its first record could launder a dropped
hold; the review of #829 showed an anchor given as a leading row degenerates to "the rows begin
with a hold or a lift". An explicit anchor makes the caller say what the chain held, and the
expected-hold reading is independent of what the records wrote.

## Alternatives considered

- **Fail a version 1 closed under an unknown anchor** (the coordinator's first proposal): it
  reports every legacy stream's tail as a SEV-1; three existing `mandate-journal` tests show it.
- **Carry the written `held`:** one wrong copy would then make the next wrong copy pass.
