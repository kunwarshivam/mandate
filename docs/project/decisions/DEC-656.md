4. **Scope.** The waiver covers only #789 and #812, and is no precedent for any other PR's size.

## Rationale

The reference generator is a single source. A §9.12 change is the spec section, its rules 96 to
106, the validator that implements them in `control.py`, the vector builder, and the generated
vectors, and `reference/journal/generate.py --check` and the reference-case drift check
(`cargo xtask refcases`) must pass at every commit on `main`. A split leaves one of two broken
states on `main`:

- **Rules without vectors.** The spec states rules 96 to 106, or the validator implements them,
  with no `membership` vectors: the identity crate (E9-7) has nothing to be tested against, and the
  validator's seeded bugs have no vector to catch them, so `generate.py --check` fails.
- **Vectors without rules.** `journal.yaml` and the fixtures hold a `membership` section that cites
  §9.12's rules before the spec states them.

#812 is bound the same way: §9.12's fold paragraph, the reference fold, and its history vectors
check one another.

Most of the lines are vectors written as data: `membership.py` holds 170 invalid and 18 valid
drafts, one per member type and rule clause, and `membership_fold.py` 37 hand-written histories.
Cutting them would cut the evidence the review needs, not the surface it reviews.

## Alternatives considered

- **Land the spec text first and the reference after.** Rejected: one of the two broken states
  above sits on `main` in between.
- **Split the vectors by record across PRs.** Rejected: each PR lands rules some of whose clauses
  no vector yet exercises, so a validator mutant escapes on `main` in between.
- **Trim the vectors to fit.** Rejected: every case pins a rule clause or catches a mutant, and
  item 3's condition needs more of them, not fewer.
