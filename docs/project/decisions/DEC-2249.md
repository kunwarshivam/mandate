## Context

The cell's research source (`crates/mandate-daemon/src/source.rs`, DEC-2214) gives the core no
context while the agent stream holds a record it cannot fold: any thesis record, and any
`ModelInvocationRecorded` that carries a reservation. So research pauses after its first reserved
call or proposal. E21-59 asks the source to fold those facts through the functions the core judges
with. Mapping the core shows three ways the story's wording does not fit the code:

- `fold_theses` re-judges whole proposals through `admit`, and a journaled thesis record lacks the
  leveraged-ETP flag a `ProposedThesis` needs. Replaying the journal through it would re-decide
  past admissions, not reproduce them. The per-decision lineage update, `LineageState::step`, is
  private.
- `UniverseChanged` is never on the agent stream: the core writes it to the account stream
  (`mandate-journal`'s catalogue), so UNFOLDED's `UniverseChanged` entry is dead.
- The reservation ceiling's fold, `mandate_gateway::meter::spend`, reads the meter stream, and the
  cell has none: its worker keeps the meter in memory (`crates/mandate-daemon/src/worker.rs`). The
  worker's `Caps` set no research daily cap, so in the cell the daily research cap is enforced only
  by the core, from the source's `research_spend_usd_today`. The workspace and deployment caps the
  in-memory meter does hold are emptied by a restart.

## Decision

1. **Lineages replay the recorded decisions through the core's one implementation.**
   `mandate-research` gains `LineageState::replay(lineage, revision, instrument, decision)`, and
   the fold's private step calls it, so there is one implementation of §8.6's lineage update.
   Replay applies a recorded decision and never judges: no check runs, and nothing but those four
   inputs is read. The cell's source replays each journaled `ThesisProposed` and `ThesisRevised`
   (its `admitted` and `reason`, read with `mandate_domain::ThesisRefusal::parse`) in stream order.
2. **The working universe is derived as the core derives it.** The source builds one
   `UniverseEntry` per instrument from the admitted thesis records, marks those a
   `ThesisInvalidated` names, and runs `expire_theses(now, entries, lineages)`. `pinned` comes from
   the confirmed envelope. The surviving entries are also the pass's active theses.
   `UniverseChanged` leaves UNFOLDED, with a test that it never reaches the agent stream.
3. **Spend and the last reservation come from the worker's own meter, and a restart fails
   closed.**
   - The worker's in-process meter is shared with the source, through one meter store both read.
     `research_spend_usd_today` is `mandate_gateway::meter::spend` over its records for the agent
     and the risk day, open reservations at their maximum included.
   - `last_reservation` stays `None`. A meter record carries only its reservation's risk day, not
     an instant, and the cell journals no `MeterReserved` whose time could give one. Within one
     process every reservation is this core's own run, which `due()` already anchors through the
     last attempt and the last call; after a restart the rule below holds the day. E21-61 gives it
     back from the journaled meter stream.
   - After a restart the meter is empty. While the agent stream holds a reserved call on the
     current risk day that the meter does not hold, or a call record whose reservation or time
     cannot be read, the store answers unavailable. So the gateway refuses every new research call
     as `meter_unavailable`, journaled as a refused call, and the source gives no context. Both hold
     until the next risk day.
   - Journaling the cell's meter stream is the faithful fix. It is the gateway lane's change, and
     it is recorded as E21-60's sibling, E21-61. Its own decision lifts this rule.
4. **Other agents' claims stay out of E21-59.** DEC-2214 item 1 stays in force: no context while
   another agent is deployed on the control stream. Folding other agents' claims from their own
   streams is E21-60.
5. **Correction to DEC-2247.** DEC-2247's context says DEC-2214 item 1 holds "until claims are
   folded (E21-59)". The story that folds other agents' claims is E21-60, not E21-59. DEC-2247
   itself stays unedited.

## Rationale

One implementation of the lineage update means the source cannot drift from the core. Deriving the
universe through `expire_theses` keeps the same expiry and invalidation rule. Counting from the
worker's meter counts what the gateway has reserved, open reservations included, which a fold of
the agent stream alone would miss. Failing closed after a restart keeps the daily cap a cap until
the meter is journaled.

## Alternatives considered

- **Copy `step`'s rules into the daemon.** Rejected: a second implementation that can drift.
- **Count settled costs from the agent stream with reservations allowed.** Rejected: it under-counts
  an open reservation, and after a restart the gateway's in-memory caps are empty too.
- **Journal the meter stream now.** Deferred to E21-61: it is the gateway lane's writer.
