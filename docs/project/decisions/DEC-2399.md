## Context

The executor restarts. Its account risk fold must then be rebuilt from seq 1, and the stream it
reads back already holds the records the fold returned before the restart. DEC-2301 item 2 says a
journaled record the fold would not have produced makes the view unreadable. DEC-2304 item 4 says a
failed append leaves the view unreadable on restart too. Neither says how the fold matches a record
to the input that produced it. Nor do they say how the executor avoids appending a record twice, or
what it does when a crash fell between a cause and its records.

## Invariants first

- The executor appends each record the fold returns exactly once.
- A replay of the journal from seq 1 ends at the view the live fold had.
- Any record on the stream the fold would not have produced, in that place, makes the view
  unreadable: openings wait, and exits, protective orders, risk exits and the kill switch go on
  (DEC-2301 item 3).
- The fold stays pure: it returns records and never appends them (DEC-2304).

## Decision

1. **The fold owes what it returns.** Every record the fold returns for a row joins a queue it
   owes. The next risk records on the stream must be exactly these, in order: the same type, schema
   version, `causation_id` and payload. The risk records are `RiskDayStarted`, `RiskLimitTriggered`,
   `RiskLimitLifted`, `HighWaterMarkReset`, `PositionReleased`, `InstrumentRestrictionChanged`,
   `GoalCompleted` and `AgentModeApplied`. A matching record is consumed and steps nothing. Any
   mismatch is `not_reproduced`.
2. **The records follow their cause.** The executor is the account stream's only writer under its
   epoch. It appends the records the fold owes in the batch right after the rows that caused them,
   before any other append. So any row that is not an owed record, arriving while records are owed,
   is `not_reproduced`. So is a risk record arriving while none is owed, except as item 4 says.
3. **The tail append.** When the fold has read the stream to its head and still owes records, a
   crash fell between their cause and their append. By item 2 they can only be owed this way when
   nothing but those records could follow the cause. Any other row after the cause has already
   made the view unreadable. The executor then appends exactly the owed records (the fold's
   `owed()`), and nothing else, in their order. This is the record the fold produces from the
   journal alone, so it adds no risk. It refines DEC-2304 item 4, which would have left the view
   unreadable for good after such a crash. A failed append while running is still
   `append_failed`, as DEC-2304 item 4 says.
4. **Another writer's mode record.** An `AgentModeApplied` the fold does not owe is the executor's
   own for another cause (an owner's pause, a kill switch, a reconciliation). It moves nothing in
   the fold, whose mode is the risk state's.
5. **The account stream's tick** (DEC-2307 item 5, built with the wiring). The executor folds each
   scheduler tick on a copy of the fold. It journals the `ClockAdvanced` copy, and then the records
   owed, only when that step returns a record or crosses New York midnight. A tick that returns
   nothing changes no result (mandate spec §5.2, MI-13), so leaving it unjournaled loses nothing a
   replay needs.

## Consequences

- `AccountRiskFold::owed()` names the records owed. The executor appends `owed()` at the head, both
  while running and after a replay, and never the return of a replayed row.
- The tests replay journals the executor writes and compare against the live view. A property test
  cuts each journal at every point, as a crash would. Its oracle is the records the live fold
  returned, journaled apart from the fold's own queue. A tampered type, version, cause, payload,
  order, or a forged record is each `not_reproduced`.

## Alternatives considered

- **Leave the view unreadable after any crash between a cause and its records** (DEC-2304 item 4 as
  written). Refused: it holds every opening for good after a crash the journal itself can repair,
  with no gain in safety. The records appended are the ones the fold derives from the journal.
- **Match records by `causation_id` alone, anywhere later on the stream.** Refused: a record could
  then sit after inputs it should have preceded, and a replay would read a state the live fold never
  had.
