# DEC-945: The asked opening reads Robinhood's own ask at the cross-check, holds it to the opening's quote age bound, and compares it with the IEX ask

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (agent: reversible engineering readings under DEC-79, each under DEC-176 — every reading refuses where the code before it refused too, and none loosens a bound) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-16 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | `claude-code` (builder, the O2 broker-ask lane), under DEC-79, DEC-176 and DEC-921 |
| **Story or claim** | E7-26; O2 and G1b of the [first live trade brief](../tasks/first-live-trade.md); claim [#1373](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/1373); DEC-529 item 12, DEC-902 item 7, DEC-933 item 2, DEC-936, DEC-940 item 4 |

Before this, `Session::broker_ask` was an `Option<Price>` taken at the preflight, and a Robinhood
session answered `None`, so every live asked opening refused `quote_absent` (DEC-940 item 4).
The connector's `get_equity_quotes` read existed only inside the preflight
(`RobinhoodConnector::preflight_facts`), and the preflight's quote would be minutes old by the time
a grant arrives.

## Decision

1. **The read.** `RobinhoodConnector::equity_quote` (mandate-robinhood) is the preflight's own
   quote read made public, unchanged: `get_equity_quotes` on the `Ordinary` budget with exactly
   `{"symbols": [symbol]}`, no account, one uncrossed record whose `symbol` is byte-equal, exact
   decimal text, no other member (DEC-902 item 7). `RobinhoodBridge::equity_quote` (mandate-shell)
   drives it on the bridge's runtime and answers any refusal as `Cause::Absent`
   (`QUOTE_UNREAD`). `BrokerAsk::robinhood(bridge)` (mandate-paper) wraps it as the session's
   broker ask. `mandate-rh-sim` already served the tool; its tests drive the bridge's read
   (`mandate-shell-rh-sim`).
2. **The staleness check.** Robinhood's quote carries no time (DEC-902 item 7), so its age is
   bounded by the run's own clock: the clock is read just before the call (`sent`) and just after
   it (`answered`). The answer reflects the broker no earlier than `sent`, so at `answered` the
   quote is at most `answered - sent` old. That age must not exceed the bound, or the read refuses
   `quote_stale` (`QuoteRefusal::Stale`, a new reason code). A clock that runs backwards across the
   read leaves the age unknown and refuses `quote_stale` too (rule 3). The bound is the rule set's
   `iex_quote_max_age_s` (DEC-470 item 2, 10 s in the fixture): neither DEC-529 nor the brief
   names a separate bound for Robinhood's quote, and this is the one the opening's other quote is
   held to. **No skew tolerance:** DEC-936's 250 ms applies to the IEX quote's own stamp at the
   preflight only, and Robinhood's quote has no stamp to be ahead of the clock. Measuring from
   `sent` rather than `answered` (DEC-902 item 7 reads "the runner's clock read after the call")
   only tightens: it counts the call's own latency against the bound.
3. **The read happens at the check.** `Session::broker_ask` now returns a `BrokerAsk` taken
   before the cycle takes the session, and the cross-check asks it for the ask after the grant and
   immediately before the one held opening is sent (`broker_cross_check`, called from
   `Opening::cross_check` inside `granted_opening`). So the ask compared is the broker's latest,
   not the preflight's. A missing, refused, garbled, crossed or wrong-instrument answer refuses
   `quote_absent`; a stale read refuses `quote_stale`; then the ask goes to
   `cross_check_the_opening` against the preflight's IEX ask within the registered collar's own
   `x` (DEC-529 item 12, DEC-912 item 2), and a disagreement refuses `quote_disagreement`. Each
   refusal sends nothing. Alpaca's ask stays the preflight's IEX ask (`BrokerAsk::Quoted`), so
   the paper path is unchanged and reads no extra quote.
4. **Gap, not fixed here: the runner's Robinhood session still holds no MCP session.**
   `mandate-mcp`'s `McpTransport` carries no bearer token, so `Linked` (the logged-in Robinhood
   session) cannot connect an `McpClient` over the login's token, and its `preflight` and `cycle`
   still refuse `SESSION_UNBUILT` (DEC-937 item 2) before any opening exists. Its `broker_ask`
   stays `BrokerAsk::Absent`. Carrying the token on the transport, connecting the client and
   building the `RobinhoodBridge` the session's preflight, cycle and `BrokerAsk::robinhood` share
   is the rest of O2, in the credential-handling crates. When that lands, `Linked::broker_ask`
   returns `BrokerAsk::robinhood(bridge)` and nothing else in the cross-check changes.

## Rationale

The cross-check exists to catch a bad broker tick before the order leaves (the brief's adversary
row). A quote read at the preflight and compared after a grant that can take up to the ask's
deadline could be minutes old, which defeats it. Reading it at the check, and bounding the read
by the run's own clock, keeps the comparison about the price the order will meet. The IEX quote's
age at that point is the binding gate's to judge (DEC-940 item 6, third gap), unchanged here.

## Alternatives considered

- **Read Robinhood's quote at the preflight (`PreflightFacts::quote`).** Rejected: its age at the
  check is the whole grant wait, and it has no stamp to judge it by.
- **Apply DEC-936's 250 ms tolerance to the Robinhood read.** Rejected: the founder approved it
  for the IEX quote's own stamp only, and an age measured on one clock has no skew to tolerate.
- **Measure the age from `answered` only.** Rejected: a call that hangs for longer than the bound
  could answer with a quote from its start; counting from `sent` refuses it.
- **A separate, longer bound for Robinhood.** Rejected: no decision names one, and a longer bound
  only loosens.
