## Decision

`RobinhoodConnector::account_snapshot` and `RobinhoodConnector::preflight_facts` map the answers
of the preflight's reads to what DEC-470 item 1 checks. The contract publishes no output shapes
([robinhood-contract.md](../tasks/robinhood-contract.md), U-R6), so DEC-875 item 4's assumption
holds until the founder's run reads real answers (R0 step 4), and the records are read as below.

1. **Closed shapes.** Each record has exactly the keys listed here. A key missing, a key not
   listed, a value of another type, a JSON number where decimal text is listed, or decimal text
   that `mandate-num` does not read (canonical text only, so `250.70` and `1e3` refuse) makes the
   whole read `Unreadable` with the read's code (`portfolio`, `positions`, `orders`,
   `tradability`, `quotes`). Nothing is defaulted and no number is filled in. A real shape that
   differs refuses, and that is a finding for the connector, never worked around (DEC-875 item 4).
2. **`get_portfolio`:** one record, `account_number`, `cash` and `buying_power`, the two amounts
   decimal text at or above zero. The snapshot carries those two amounts and no other account
   figure: no equity, margin or multiplier is assumed. The gate's 1× buying power, the lower of
   the two, is the runner's (DEC-529 item 11, G1a).
3. **`get_equity_positions`:** `positions[]`, each `account_number`, `symbol`, `quantity` (signed
   decimal text) and `average_buy_price` (a positive price). Every record of the agentic account is
   mapped in the broker's order, a zero quantity included, and none is merged or dropped, so
   DEC-470 item 1 sees and refuses it.
4. **`get_equity_orders`:** `orders[]`, each with the keys `mandate-rh-sim` serves: `id` (non-empty
   text), `account_number`, `symbol`, `side` (`buy`, `sell`), `type` (`market`, `limit`,
   `stop_market`, `stop_limit`), `quantity`, `limit_price` and `stop_price` (decimal text or
   `null`), `time_in_force` (`gfd`, `gtc`), `market_hours` (`regular_hours`, `extended_hours`,
   `all_day_hours`), `state` (the contract's ten), `filled_quantity`, and optionally `ref_id` as
   text. A record in a working state (`new`, `queued`, `confirmed`, `unconfirmed`,
   `partially_filled`) is an open order, with §6.2's status (DEC-860 item 3) and no
   `client_order_id` (U-R2). One in any of the other five is read in full, then left out.
5. **One page or none.** A list answer's `structuredContent` holds its records' member and nothing
   else. A next-page cursor, or any other member, refuses the read: reading only the first page
   could hide an open order. Following pages is C3's list request; the preflight does not page.
6. **`get_equity_tradability`:** read through `read` with the filter `symbol`, which the
   contract's input lists for that tool (`contract/tools.json`), so DEC-879 item 5 adds it to
   that tool's allowlist. One record: `account_number`, `symbol` byte-equal to the instrument's,
   and `tradable` a boolean. `false` is `RobinhoodError::NotTradable`; zero records (another
   account's record is dropped by `read`), two, or another symbol is `Unreadable`.
7. **`get_equity_quotes`:** called on the `Ordinary` budget with exactly `{"symbols": [symbol]}`.
   The contract names no account for it and its answer is about no account, so it is not an
   account read and does not go through `read` (CN-8 is kept: nothing about another account is in
   it). `quotes[]` must hold exactly one record, `symbol` byte-equal, `bid_price` and `ask_price`
   prices with the bid at or below the ask (DEC-470 item 2's uncrossed quote), or it is
   `Unreadable`. No time field is assumed. The quote's age is measured against the runner's clock
   read after the call, as DEC-470 item 2 reads its clock after the GETs. The cross-check with
   IEX is G1b's (DEC-529 item 12).
8. **Every account read is scoped.** `get_portfolio`, `get_equity_positions` and
   `get_equity_orders` go through `read` with no filter but the recorded account, and
   `get_equity_tradability` with `symbol` too, so each runs the account check afresh (DEC-875
   items 1 and 2). A refusal of any read is the preflight's refusal, unchanged. No order, cancel
   or exit waits on a read (`AGENTS.md` rule 13, DEC-875 item 5).
9. **Not in C4's tests PR.** `mandate-rh-sim` serving `get_portfolio`, `get_equity_positions`,
   `get_equity_tradability` and `get_equity_quotes`, and the end-to-end preflight against it in
   that crate's `tests/`, come with C4's implementation (DEC-875 item 6). The in-crate tests use a
   recording double.

## Rationale

Every item refuses where the contract is silent. A refused preflight blocks an opening, which
adds no risk. Dropping a zero row, ignoring a cursor, defaulting a missing price, or reading an
unknown key as harmless could each let a non-flat account look flat. None of these readings can
hold a risk reduction, because exits never pass through the preflight. That makes each one a
DEC-176 reading an agent may accept.

## Alternatives considered

- Ignoring keys the shape does not list: rejected. An unknown key could carry the fact that
  matters (a margin flag, a held quantity), and the brief asks that any unknown field refuse.
- Mapping into `mandate-executor`'s `BrokerAccount`: rejected. Its status, multiplier, margin and
  fee fields have no source in the contract, so they would have to be invented. The snapshot
  reuses `BrokerPosition` and `BrokerOrder`, which the contract's records fill.
- Treating `rejected`, `failed` or `voided` records as open: rejected. §6.2 reads them as not
  working. Their re-read by `order_id` concerns our own intents (C3), and a flat account's history
  would otherwise refuse every run.
- Following every page here: left to C3, which owns paging. Until then, a second page refuses.
