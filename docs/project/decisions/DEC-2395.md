# DEC-2395: `AccountRestrictionChanged` closes with its cause, and the executor writes it

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (the coordinator, under DEC-176: the reading that only tightens; it adds members and rules and loosens nothing) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-26 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | `claude-code` (coordinator), on lane L1's proposal |
| **Story or claim** | E7-2u; [journal spec](../../specs/journal.md) §9 and §9.5; [trading spec](../../specs/trading-domain.md) §7.3; [connections spec](../../specs/connections.md) CN-6; [DEC-441](DEC-441.md) item 23 |

## Context

`append` refused `AccountRestrictionChanged` as `unknown_schema`: §9 listed its members but no
schema closed it. So the executor's batch for a blocked or restricted account was refused whole and
its process stopped (found by DEC-2514's journal test). The specs and the writer disagreed: journal
spec §9, trading spec §7.3 and connections spec CN-6 give the record a closed `cause`
(`broker_reject`, `broker_notice`, `connection_unavailable`; DEC-441 item 23), while the executor's
`restrict()` writes `restriction`, §7.3's `reason_code` and `risk_clock`, and no `cause`.

## Decision

1. §9.5 closes `AccountRestrictionChanged` at schema version 1 with `restriction`, `cause`,
   `reason_code` and `risk_clock`, the specs' members and the writer's together.
2. Rule 279: `restriction` is `blocked` exactly when `reason_code` is `account_trading_blocked`.
   Rule 280: a `blocked` restriction's `cause` is `broker_notice`. Together they admit exactly §7.3's
   rows; a third rule proposed for `connection_unavailable` (only with `closing_only`) follows from
   them and is not written. Rules 281 and 282, reserved for this lane, stay unused.
3. Order: the spec and vectors, then `mandate-journal`, then the executor writes `cause` from the
   signal (§7.3 rows 1 and 4 `broker_notice`, rows 2 and 3 `broker_reject`, row 5
   `connection_unavailable`). Until the executor's change lands, a restricted account's batch is
   still refused, at `payload.cause`, which halts as before and adds no risk.
4. Registering the writer's shape without `cause` was rejected: it loosens the spec toward the code.
5. The record's `causation_id` is `null`: a run of 403s has no one reject to name, and the reject
   itself is `RejectObserved`'s. A lift is never another `AccountRestrictionChanged`.
6. A second cause raised while a restriction is in force is not journaled today (the executor
   journals only a stricter state, and the fold keeps the state alone), so causes cannot yet lift
   independently as §7.3 says. That is executor and fold work, recorded as backlog row E7-2v with
   the cause-keyed owner alert, not part of this change.

## Rationale

- The cause is what lets causes lift independently and keeps a restriction the broker never imposed
  from being journaled as the broker's; a record without it could not be folded as §7.3 says.
