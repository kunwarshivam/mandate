# DEC-1002: The forward-paper evaluation harness scores journaled theses as they resolve, one scorecard per signal model, and records its report as an artifact

| **Date** | 2026-10-17 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | The lane I4 builder (Claude Code), under DEC-79 and DEC-176 |
| **Story or claim** | E17-8, E15-3; DEC-99, DEC-103, DEC-111, DEC-122, DEC-281; claim [#1463](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/1463) |

## Context

`mandate_research::score::evaluate` is the pure scorer (DEC-281): closed theses, close series, the
registered decision and a modeled cost in, one scorecard out. E17-8's crate doc leaves the rest to
a caller: select the window's closed theses from the journal, fetch the closes, and compute each
thesis's round-trip cost from the backtest's fee model. E15-3 wants one scorecard per signal model
beside the baselines. Nothing does either yet.

## Decision

1. **Input from the journal.** The harness reads `ThesisProposed` and `ThesisRevised` records
   (journal spec §9.4) from the agent streams of the team's internal paper workspaces (DEC-103).
   Every record passes `mandate_spec::context::check_thesis_record` before it is read (DEC-414
   item 3); one that fails refuses the evaluation, naming its event, rather than being skipped.
2. **Every thesis, admitted or not.** Each record is scored as an idea (DEC-99's "every thesis"):
   the scorer takes no admission outcome. A revision is its own thesis with its own row and no
   predecessor's score (DEC-111 item 2, MI-18). Each scored row carries its `revision` within its
   lineage and its `lineage_id`, which is how the report shows the lineage's revision count that
   DEC-111 item 2 asks for.
3. **As they resolve.** A thesis is closed when its horizon end (`expires_at`) is at or before the
   evaluation's cut-off and its instrument's series (when the request has one) and every
   baseline's hold a close at or after the horizon end; until then it is `open` and counted, never
   scored, so an exit is never read from a stale close. The exit itself stays DEC-281's, the last
   close at or before the horizon end ([DEC-1140](earlier-900-1999.md#dec-1140) item 2). A record read twice (one
   stream position)
   refuses the evaluation. The registered window (DEC-122) is applied by the
   scorer, which refuses a closed thesis outside it; the harness passes only the theses whose
   horizon closes inside the window, and lists the others by count as `outside_window`.
4. **One scorecard per signal model.** Theses are grouped by the research agent that proposed
   them, keyed by `(model_id, model_version, content_hash)`, so a new version starts with an empty
   scorecard. Each group is evaluated under the same registered decision and the same baselines
   (buy-and-hold of the eligible basket, and the broad index ETF). A group the scorer refuses
   (`window_not_closed`, `empty_scoreable_set`) reports its refusal code and no figure.
5. **The cost model.** A thesis's round-trip cost is the fill model's half spread and impact,
   charged on both legs, plus `mandate-accounting`'s fees for a buy at the entry close and a sell
   at the exit close of a reference notional, divided by that notional and rounded up at 12
   places. It is never understated: every rounding moves against the thesis.
6. **Where it is shown.** The evaluation report is the founder's, for the internal paper
   workspaces only (DEC-103). It is not an owner's scorecard: `GET /agents/{id}/scorecards` and
   any scorecard in an approval stay unshown to owners until counsel answers compliance question
   35 (product brief, approvals). `GET /evaluations/{id}` answers `not_found` outside an internal
   paper workspace.
7. **The record.** The report is a canonical artifact, journaled as `EvaluationRunRecorded` on the
   internal workspace's control stream (journal spec §9.18): `evaluation_id`, `decision` (the
   registered DEC-122 decision), `cut_off`, `theses` (the digest of the records read) with the
   range read per agent stream (its first and last `stream_id:seq` and the last record's hash),
   `closes` and `cost_model` (digests), `code_build`, `report` (ref), `report_version`, and
   `passed` (true only when at least one group was scored and every scored group passed).
   Registration in `mandate-journal`, with vectors, is E17-8's follow-up, as E4-5's is for
   DEC-1001.

## Rationale

The scorer is already pure and independently tested; the harness only selects, groups and costs,
so each of those steps is where an evaluation could quietly flatter itself. Refusing an unchecked
record, scoring every thesis, never scoring an open one, and costing against the thesis close
each of those doors. Grouping by content hash is what keeps a changed model from inheriting an old
model's record.

## Alternatives considered

- **Score only admitted theses.** Rejected: it lets the admission's filter flatter the model.
- **One scorecard for the whole research agent across versions.** Rejected: a version change would
  carry a track record it did not earn.
