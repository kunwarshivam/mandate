## Decision

The [cost model](../../product/12-cost-model.md) v0.1 is a model with variables and labelled
assumptions. Its scenarios find that research inference is the largest line wherever research runs
more than daily, and that the always-on workspace floor and fixed cell cost decide the cost of
small workspaces and of the design-partner phase. These items follow. **Meanwhile, for every
item:** the guardrails already decided hold (DEC-431 item 15, DEC-432 item 4, DEC-433 items 15
and 16, DEC-434 items 13, 18, and 19), no new paid service is used, and no price is set.

**Proposed (founder):**

1. **Target gross margin.** **Recommendation:** 70% blended at scale, measured per plan from the
   meter and the infrastructure bill; the design-partner phase is run at a planned loss, since five
   workspaces carry a whole cell (cost model §3.5).
2. **Inference: bundled or passed through.** **Recommendation:** each plan includes a monthly
   inference allowance; usage above it is billed at cost (aggregator fee included) plus a stated
   margin, as 07-pricing principle 3 and HLD §10 already say. The owner's
   `cost_cap_usd_per_day` is then the visible control on the variable part of the bill.
3. **Inference caps per plan.** **Recommendation:** each plan's policy sets a maximum
   `research_cost_cap_usd_per_day`, a `research_interval_s` floor, and the workspace model-spend
   quota, sized so the included allowance keeps the plan inside item 1's margin. Policy only
   tightens the envelope (mandate spec, child ≥ parent), so this adds no risk.
4. **A free paper tier.** A paper agent's research costs what a live one's does. **Recommendation:**
   paper agents are free on compute, with a research interval floor of one day and a low research
   cap set by the founder.
5. **Decisions as a usage component.** HLD §10 lists decisions among usage components. Pricing them
   tracks trading activity closely enough to read as per-trade pricing (07-pricing principle 2).
   **Recommendation:** meter decisions for quotas and analysis; do not price them.
6. **Values for DEC-434 item 18 in the internal phase.** **Recommendation:** a monthly inference cap
   of about 1.5 times the expected spend (cost model §3.4: about $96 a month expected), below the
   DEC-431 ceiling of $608 per internal workspace; an infrastructure cap at the founder's current
   host cost. Revisit both with DEC-431 item 15's caps after the spike's paper runs.
7. **A lean workspace for retail.** The full per-workspace process set is about a quarter of a
   retail workspace's cost at the model's assumptions. **Recommendation:** design shared control
   services and gateway replicas per cell (within HLD §8's isolation rules, which keep agent
   processes and executors per workspace) before retail prices are set; recorded as an
   infrastructure question, not a change to the topology now.

## Rationale

Gap 12 had no document, and pricing (07) names levers without any cost under them. Every number in
the model is spending or price, which DEC-79 reserves, so each item is Proposed with the
conservative option in force. The recommendations keep pricing away from trades, assets, and
profits, put the variable cost under a cap the owner already confirms, and avoid committing to a
retail price before the spike measures the real cost per research call.

## Alternatives considered

- **Bundle unlimited inference in each plan.** Simpler to sell, but research cost is linear in the
  owner's cadence and model choice, so heavy users would set the plan's margin.
- **Pure pass-through with no allowance.** Most transparent, but every user meets a variable bill
  from the first day, which may deter Individuals.
- **Price per decision.** Tracks our cost for fast-tier calls, but too close to per-trade pricing
  (item 5).
- **Set prices now.** Rejected: 07 waits on design-partner interviews, and the largest assumption,
  tokens per call, is not yet measured.
