## Decision

With the agent-scoped kill switch live (#668), every executor property that used to stop at the
switch's stub runs to its own verdict, and twelve of them failed away from any stub or passed while
checking nothing. A triage against #668's head (03894f6) found two executor defects (backlog, E7-4)
and test defects in the rest. This DEC-77 tests correction fixes the test defects only. Test files
change; no `src/` file does. These are the readings the corrected oracles take.

ES-13 splits a change over 400 lines in a safety-critical crate into stacked PRs, so this one ships
as three, each holding on its own on `main` and the PRs below it:
- **stack 1 of 3**, [#671](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/671): this file, the backlog
  rows, the shared helpers and the corrections that read the clock and the companion;
- **stack 2 of 3**, branch `claude/hejxo4-e7-4-s7-tests-correction-b`: the shadow book, the
  protection accountant, and the cancel and marketability oracles;
- **stack 3 of 3**, branch `claude/hejxo4-e7-4-s7-tests-correction-c`: the two session hand tests.

Each item names the PR that carries it.

1. **`risk_clock` is a timestamp.** (carried by [#671](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/671), stack 1 of 3) Journal spec §4.7 and its `risk_clock` row ("never integer
   seconds"). `properties.rs`' `number()` read it as an integer, which never matches, so the bound,
   the age and the absence window were all measured as zero (#456 round 1; DEC-390 item 4). A
   test-side parser, `risk_seconds`, reads the timestamp with the civil-date arithmetic written out
   rather than borrowed from `mandate-time`. It is used by `no_interval_exceeds_the_limit_without_an_alert`,
   `no_submission_carries_an_intent_older_than_its_maximum_age`,
   `no_recovery_submits_without_a_confirmed_absence` and
   `every_risk_input_draft_carries_a_non_decreasing_risk_clock`.
2. **A submission's intent rides its companion.** (carried by [#671](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/671), stack 1 of 3) `OrderSubmitted` version 2 carries no `intent_id`
   (journal spec §9.5, rule 45; DEC-446). `submissions_with_requests` merges in the
   `OrderRequestRecorded` that a submission's `causation_id` names, never a neighbour's, so
   `a_client_order_id_is_a_function_of_the_intent_id_alone` and the age property read the intent
   and the purpose from there. Before this, the client-order-id property rejected every script and
   aborted after 257 to 385 s, which pushed `fast` past its 15-minute limit on #668.
3. **A broker-created leg holds a zero reservation.** (carried by stack 2 of 3, branch `claude/hejxo4-e7-4-s7-tests-correction-b`) Trading spec §5.4 ("Ownership of
   broker-created legs": "reserving nothing") and DEC-160 (3) ("with a zero reservation"). The
   shadow book adds each order a `ProtectionChanged placed` names that no `OrderSubmitted` recorded:
   `accepted`, the covered quantity, holding a (zero) reservation until a terminal state. The live
   state and the fold agree on the leg, so the triage's `pending_cancel` leg was an oracle gap, not a
   replay divergence.
4. **Protection rests until it is cancelled, terminal or filled.** (carried by stack 2 of 3, branch `claude/hejxo4-e7-4-s7-tests-correction-b`) Trading spec §5.4's tranche model
   and §5.7's terminal states. The protection accountant sums each placement's remaining quantity:
   it covers its `qty` until `ProtectionChanged cancelled` names one of its orders, one of them is
   terminal or abandoned, and each fill of one of them takes the fill's quantity off it. An
   instrument ever placed keeps an entry, zero once nothing rests. Before this, a filled or partly
   filled re-placed OCO still counted in full (seeds 1 and 77).
5. **The bound is measured on the clock, and the alert is the interval's.** (carried by [#671](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/671), stack 1 of 3) Trading spec §5.4
   ("bounded unprotected intervals"). An interval runs to its end or, still open, to the run's last
   clock. One past `max_unprotected_s` needs a notification whose subject draft names the interval's
   instrument and falls inside the interval, not any alert anywhere.
6. **Only an opening ages out.** (carried by [#671](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/671), stack 1 of 3) Trading spec §5.7, with rule 13, DEC-160 (12) and the coordinator's
   ruling D2 (an exit is held and priced at its release, never too old). Age runs from an intent's
   first `IntentReceived`, and only an `open` or `increase` submission is judged.
7. **Which cancels hold which submission.** (carried by stack 2 of 3, branch `claude/hejxo4-e7-4-s7-tests-correction-b`) Trading spec §5.4. Only sells are judged: they are what
   §5.4's sequences order after a confirmation. A buy may be accepted while an exit waits, and the
   exit then asks its cancel too (DEC-160 (13)). A cancel journaled overdue (`cancel_overdue`)
   ends rule 5's wait (DEC-160 (7), (13), (18); the backlog's #286 round 1, minor 2 row). The OCO
   for a partly filled entry's filled quantity, which DEC-346 item 6 places once the entry is
   terminal whatever else is pending and caps by rule 12, does not wait on a plain buy's cancel: a
   plain buy that fills only adds to the position the protection covers. A bracket entry's cancel
   still holds it, since that entry's fill activates sell legs.
8. **Marketable while protection is cancelled, for every order.** (carried by stack 2 of 3, branch `claude/hejxo4-e7-4-s7-tests-correction-b`) Trading spec §5.4: "Orders
   submitted while protection is canceled must be marketable at submission"; §5.6. Protection is
   cancelled from an `unprotected_start` that names no `bracket` until protection is placed again
   or the interval ends. A partly filled bracket's interval is not one: its legs are held, not
   cancelled. Inside, every order but the protection itself must be marketable against the script's
   one quote: a sell at or below the bid, a buy at or above the ask, or a market order. The
   coordinator ruled the scope on this PR's triage: the sentence covers every order submitted in
   the instrument while its protection is cancelled for a sequence, not only the sequence's own
   orders. This tightens the rule and adds no risk. The executor fix is to hold such an opening
   until protection is placed again, never to reprice it, and it is a later change (backlog, E2).
   Before this, the oracle judged purpose, not price, over bracket intervals too, and its final
   count compared submissions with themselves.
9. **Each property's subject is put in every script and asserted, not assumed.** `DOUBTED_LEAD` and `STALE_LEAD` are carried by stack 1, `scripted_exiting` by stack 2.
   - `DOUBTED_LEAD`: an opening times out and the broker reports it absent three times across waits,
     so it is resubmitted at attempt 2. Used by the client-order-id and recovery properties.
   - `STALE_LEAD`: a restart holds a fresh opening `startup_reconciliation_pending` past 120 s,
     then the snapshot ends the hold. Used by the age property.
   - `scripted_exiting`: a completed protected position, a risk exit, and its protection's cancel
     confirmed. Used by the marketability property.

   Each property asserts that its subject is there (a resubmission, a received aged opening, a
   judged order), or `prop_assume`s it where a random script may legitimately lack it (a risk
   input).
10. **The session tests move onto the calendar.** (carried by stack 3 of 3, branch `claude/hejxo4-e7-4-s7-tests-correction-c`) `hand::an_automated_flatten_defers_equity_sells_to_the_session`
    and `hand::an_unconfirmed_owner_exit_waits_for_the_session` ran at the suite's 1970 clock, which
    `session` reads as the regular session (DEC-260 (13)). They passed because the sell waits on the
    protection's cancel, and `deferred` was `[]`, so the presence check checked nothing. Each now
    runs at 2026-09-22 08:00 ET, pre-market, with a regular-session control at 11:00 ET the same day:
    - the automated switch asks no protective cancel, sells nothing and defers `["AAPL"]`
      pre-market, and in the session cancels the protection and defers nothing (§5.5, DEC-485
      items 5 and 10);
    - the unconfirmed owner switch sends no extended-hours sell pre-market (DEC-260 (13): an owner
      exit there is a regular-session limit the broker queues), and in the session its close is
      submitted once the cancel is confirmed.
11. **What this leaves.** On `main` every test this change touches still fails at the kill switch's
    stub (`step.rs`'s `later_slice`, `Unimplemented { story: "E7-3" }`). Each property's shrunk
    script holds one `KillSwitch`, and `xtask ci pending` reports no problem. On #668's head, nine of
    the corrected properties and both session tests pass, so they go live with #668. Two still fail,
    on executor defects the triage found:
    - `protective_sell_quantity_never_exceeds_the_position_in_any_script` (E1);
    - `no_resting_order_is_submitted_inside_an_unprotected_interval` (E2).

    Both are in the backlog. Until they are fixed, #668 needs those two in `BEHAVIOUR_ONLY_TESTS`
    or the fixes first: the coordinator's call. `hand::an_owner_exit_outside_the_session_prices_from_the_confirmed_bid`
    needs #668 to stub the confirmed-owner, outside-session pricing path (the coordinator's ruling,
    E3). It is not changed here.

## Evidence

The planted bugs could not be seeded in `src/`: the session's permission classifier refuses that,
and the coordinator's ruling keeps the refusal. Each corrected oracle was therefore run, on #668's
head at `PROPTEST_RNG_SEED=20260927`, against a fault seeded in the run's observed drafts and effects
by a test-side hook that was never committed:

- the marketability oracle (item 8), on E2's minimal script, at all four seeds tried (20260927, 1,

- `protective_sell_quantity_never_exceeds_the_position_in_any_script`, with item 4's accountant, on

Every other corrected property passed on #668's head at those four seeds.

## Rationale

Each reading is the spec's or an accepted decision's, transcribed into the oracle rather than read
from the crate. Each oracle is shown to fail on a fault before it is trusted (`AGENTS.md`,
"Independent oracles"). No assertion was dropped: items 7 and 8 narrow what an oracle judges to what
the spec and DEC-160 (13) and DEC-346 item 6 rule, and item 8 judges price where the old oracle
judged purpose.

## Alternatives considered

- **`BEHAVIOUR_ONLY_TESTS` rows for all twelve.** Rejected: nine of them pass vacuously or fail on
  the oracle's own defect, and a row would hide that.
- **Leave the session tests pending until the clock-0 backlog item.** Rejected: they pass on #668,
  and the pending gate refuses a pending test that passes.
- **Judge every order in every unprotected interval, the bracket's included.** Rejected: §5.4
  states the rule for protection that is cancelled, and a partly filled bracket's legs are held, not
  cancelled.
