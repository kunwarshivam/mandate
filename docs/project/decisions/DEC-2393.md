## Context

`mandate_executor::risk_state::AccountRiskFold` folds the account stream into `mandate_spec`'s
`RiskState`, but reads only marks and ticks. Every other risk input fails closed. The first
`FillApplied` makes the view unreadable, so wiring the fold into the deployed gate today would hold
every opening after the agent's first fill (DEC-2307 item 2). Mandate spec §5.2 lists `FillApplied`,
`LateFillApplied` and `FeesCharged` among the inputs, and §5.1 defines E as A plus realized P&L **net
of fees**. `RiskState` has no fee input, and the reference cases run at zero fees. On paper, Alpaca's
fills carry no fee: the executor books the regulatory fee it simulates as a `FeesCharged` for that
fill's order. On live, a broker's commission rides on the `FillApplied`'s own `fees` list. Neither
says which fills are the agent's on a shared account.

## Invariants first

- E is §5.1's: A + cash + Q × mark, where cash is every own fill's proceeds less its cost, less every
  fee charged to the agent's own orders. A fee only ever lowers E, so counting one never lifts or
  delays a limit.
- E moves only on the agent's own inputs: another agent's fill or fee on the same account never moves it.
- A row the fold cannot read makes the view unreadable: openings wait and exits go on (DEC-2301
  item 3).
- The fold stays pure and journals in step order (DEC-2304, DEC-2307 item 3).

## Decision

1. **The agent's own orders** are those the executor's own fold attributes to it: an
   `OrderSubmitted` whose companion, the `OrderRequestRecorded` its `causation_id` names (journal
   spec rule 45), names the agent, or, with no companion, whose own `agent_id` does; an order an
   `OrderStateChanged` says `replaces` one of them; and the successor a terminal state of one of
   them names in `replaced_by`. The order records are read at their known versions
   (`OrderSubmitted` and `OrderStateChanged` 1 and 2, `OrderRequestRecorded` 1); any other version
   makes the view unreadable rather than miss whose an order is. The fold folds from seq 1 (its replay,
   B3), so it sees every submission before its fills.
2. **An own fill is `Input::Fill`** (`FillApplied` or `LateFillApplied` at version 1: its `side`,
   `qty_gross` and `price`) at its `risk_clock`, in the session the calendar places that instant in.
   It binds the fold's instrument, under DEC-2308 item 4 and DEC-2314: a fill on another instrument,
   or binding one that is not a canonical asset id, makes the view unreadable. A fill of any other
   order (another agent's, an unattributed one, or external activity) steps nothing, like any
   record the fold does not read. A sale of more than the sub-ledger holds is `RiskState`'s refusal
   to decide (no short sales, `AGENTS.md` rule 12), so the view becomes unreadable.
3. **Fees are a risk input of their own,** `mandate_spec::risk::Input::Fee { usd }`: a non-negative
   charge that lowers the sub-ledger's cash, and so E, at its instant, with the evaluation order of
   §5.2 like any input. Two rows charge one:
   - an own fill's `fees` entries in `USD` (a live broker's commission), summed and stepped right
     after the fill, at the same instant, with no time passing. A fill with an entry in any other
     asset (a crypto fee taken in the asset, which also changes the quantity received, §8.3) is a
     refusal to decide until a slice folds asset fees;
   - a `FeesCharged` at version 1 whose `client_order_id` is the agent's own (the fee the paper
     executor simulates for that order's fill, DEC-948 item 3): its `charged`. A `FeesCharged` of
     another order steps nothing.

   A fee of 0 steps nothing. The charge, not the accrual, is what lowers E: on paper the
   `FeesCharged` is the only record of the fee, and on live the fill's commission is already charged
   (DEC-938 item 2).
4. **No reference case changes.** The cases run at zero fees and their fills are the agent's own,
   so walking them through rows gives the harness's figures unchanged. The fee input is pinned by
   `mandate-spec` tests and the fold's own tests, with figures computed by hand.

## Consequences

- `mandate-spec` gains `Input::Fee`, tests first. Mandate spec §5.2 gains one sentence citing this
  decision, in this spec PR.
- `AccountRiskFold` reads `OrderRequestRecorded`, `OrderSubmitted` and `OrderStateChanged` for
  ownership, `FillApplied`, `LateFillApplied` and `FeesCharged` as risk inputs. The rest of B2b-2b
  (owner acknowledgments, versions, the universe) is lane RK's R2.
- The walks drive each MC-R case that holds a fill through journaled rows, up to its first input
  that R2 reads.

## Alternatives considered

- **Ignore fees until a reference case states them.** Refused: E would overstate the agent's equity
  by every fee, and a limit would latch later than §5.1's E says it should.
- **Count a fee at its accrual.** Refused: the paper fill carries no accrual, and on live a fill's
  commission is already charged, so the accrual would only add a second record of one fee.
- **Fold every fill on the account into the sub-ledger.** Refused: on a shared account another
  agent's buy would move this agent's E.
