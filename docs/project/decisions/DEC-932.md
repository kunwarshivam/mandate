## Decision

1. **One header.** The long page's bar (`scroll-bar.tsx`) is pinned to the top only once the page has
   covered the desktop, and is hidden (and inert) before, so it is never on screen with the desktop's
   own bar. Sign in and Sign up stay in view throughout (DEC-906 item 8). Amends DEC-907 item 1.
2. **Removed:** the flying voxel owl (`owl-flight.tsx`, DEC-907 item 5), the pixel thread
   (`pixel-thread.tsx`, item 9), the moon and its reflection over the pixel sea (DEC-908), the broker
   strip under the opening, and the Met painting behind the request form (`plate.tsx`). The sea's
   waves and the lock screen's night stay.
3. **Three parts, not four.** "Your rules" and "Set the stakes" become one part, limits in dollars,
   with the gap caveat beside it; the verdict and check pictures are dropped. Then asking you (on
   tide) and the reason behind every move.
4. **"It won't" is a plain list** under "What it won't do.", on paper, with no strike-throughs.
5. **Copy** is shorter and plainer; the opening's button reads "Join the beta".
