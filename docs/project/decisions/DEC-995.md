## Context

DEC-967 item 7 put cell #1 on k3s on one or two Hetzner VMs in Ashburn, VA, and item 16 approved
two of them at about $35 to $45 a month, bought once every local-cell drill passes (DEC-990 item 2:
when the drills first pass on `main`). The founder could not create a Hetzner account. The
coordinator asked again, and relayed the answer:

| Question (as relayed) | Options offered | The founder's answer, as relayed |
|---|---|---|
| Where does cell #1 run, now that the Hetzner account cannot be created? | Retry Hetzner (recommended); DigitalOcean; Vultr; OVHcloud US | "DigitalOcean": "Easy sign-up, NYC or Toronto region, about $48 a month per VM ($96 for two). Reliable and well documented, but about 2× Hetzner." |

## Decision

1. **Cell #1 is k3s on two DigitalOcean droplets of about 4 vCPU and 8 GB each**, in place of
   DEC-967 item 7's Hetzner VMs. The architecture is unchanged (DEC-967 item 6): the same Helm
   chart, which uses no DigitalOcean-specific API.
2. **Region New York (`nyc3`) by default.** DigitalOcean has no Ashburn region. Latency to
   Robinhood and Alpaca from New York is expected to be comparable to Ashburn's for this cell's
   order rates (not measured; DEC-967 item 7 chose Ashburn for proximity); the
   founder may pick Toronto at purchase instead, which changes only the values file.
3. **About $96 a month at list price** for the two droplets, plus about $8 for two 40 GB block
   volumes. The founder confirms the price when buying. This replaces item 16's $35 to $45 for H1;
   R2 (H3) and the second-region copy (L-2) are unchanged.
4. **Bought only when every C1 drill first passes on `main`** (DEC-990 item 2), by the founder;
   agents never get account or host access (DEC-822).
5. **Same shape as before, so the cell stays portable:**
   - no public ports: a cloud firewall denies all inbound, and the only ingress is a Cloudflare
     Tunnel from a `cloudflared` pod;
   - operators reach the cluster through Cloudflare Access, never an open SSH or API port (the
     free tier covers the founder and a few operators);
   - Cloudflare R2 for storage, behind the plain S3 endpoint and the lock check (E21-40);
   - CloudNativePG on local volumes for the journal;
   - no DigitalOcean managed database, managed Kubernetes or load balancer.
6. **What changes in the disclosures.** DigitalOcean offers no placement groups that guarantee two
   droplets sit on different hardware. Two droplets in one region are two machines, not two sites,
   and are not guaranteed to be on separate hosts; the plan says so where OPS-2's second failure
   domain is discussed (E21-41, E21-48). DigitalOcean also has no key management service, so H4
   (OpenBao's unseal) is unchanged: a key service elsewhere, or a manual unseal by the founder.

## Rationale

The founder could not open the Hetzner account, and DigitalOcean is the option they chose. The
chart was written to need no provider-specific API, so the move changes the values file and the
price, not the plan's lanes. Keeping to droplets, block volumes and a firewall, with no managed
services, keeps the next move (another provider, or managed Kubernetes) a values change too.

## Alternatives considered

- **Retry Hetzner** (recommended in the question; not chosen): about half the price, but the
  account could not be created.
- **Vultr, OVHcloud US** (offered; not chosen).
- **DigitalOcean managed Kubernetes or managed Postgres:** rejected: it breaks DEC-967 item 8
  (CloudNativePG in the cell) and ties the cell to one provider.
