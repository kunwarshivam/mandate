# DEC-350: Tripwires are an envelope field over the agent's fills, with three v1 metrics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-10-02 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | The stream H builder (agent), under DEC-79 and DEC-176: it reads DEC-187 by the option that adds no risk and only tightens |
| **Story or claim** | E6-13, claim [#439](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/439); mandate spec §3, §6.7 |

## Decision

1. `autonomy.tripwires` is an optional array of at most 20 tripwires, sorted by `id`, each
   `{id, metric, threshold, action}`. `action` is `end_delegations` or `exits_only`; `paused` is not in
   the schema (rule 13). A tripwire fires when its metric is at or above its threshold.
2. The v1 metrics are counted from the agent's fills only (`FillApplied` and `LateFillApplied`), all of
   which the executor already folds as risk inputs with `risk_clock`:
   - `consecutive_losing_exits`: the latest sell fills whose net realized P&L (gross realized per
     trading spec §8.1 less the fill's own fees) is below 0, stopping at the first that is 0 or more.
     Counted per fill, not per order, so the count is never lower than per order; buys neither count
     nor break the streak.
   - `realized_loss_usd`: max(0, −Σ net realized) of the window's fills in the current risk day, buys'
     fees included, gains offsetting losses.
   - `new_instruments`: fills that are the agent's first fill ever in their instrument.
3. DEC-187's other examples wait (mandate spec §12 question 12): a client request rate needs the
   executor to see `requested_by` on an intent, and firing on an opening order's submission rather
   than its fill needs `GateDecided` as a risk input with `risk_clock`, a journal spec change. MI-30
   already makes every client-requested opening an `ask`.
4. Marks never enter a metric, so a bad tick cannot fire or delay a tripwire.

## Rationale

Fills are journaled risk inputs the executor folds already, so every metric is deterministic, replays
identically, and needs no new event. Per-fill counting and including buy fees fire no later than the
alternatives. A closed metric list keeps V-044, §9.2, and the reference model exact.

## Alternatives considered

- Per-order losing exits: needs order terminal states in the fold and fires later on split exits.
- Gross losses without gains offsetting: not what "realized loss" means, and the daily loss limit
  already bounds equity loss.
- An open condition language over arbitrary journal fields: a new surface with no bounds V-044 could
  check.
