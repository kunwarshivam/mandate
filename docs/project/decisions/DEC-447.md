# DEC-447: `StreamOpened` and `OwnerCommandRefused` carry no `risk_clock`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Reserved |
| **Date** | 2026-10-03 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | Reserved by the stream K builder (claim [#137](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/137)) under DEC-79 and DEC-176, a rider on [DEC-446](DEC-446.md) |
| **Story or claim** | E7 journal: the account stream's executor records ([DEC-360](DEC-360.md)); backlog stream K |

Reserved for the reading that the two account-stream payload schemas §9.5 leaves at
`schema_version` 1 — `StreamOpened` and `OwnerCommandRefused` — do not gain `risk_clock`: the
fold reads no clock from the opening, and the refusal schema is shared across streams.
