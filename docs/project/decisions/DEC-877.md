## Decision

1. **The cap's instant and its stop.** DEC-858 item 5's "end of the regular session" is the end of
   the regular session the bound is in, on the same `mandate_time::ExchangeCalendar` that DEC-853
   item 5 reads. So it is 16:00 New York on a full day, and the early close on a short one.
   - The watch stops at the first wake at or past that instant, before any read there. It sends
     nothing at or after the session's end.
   - The run then ends with `ShellError::CancelUnconfirmed`, whose stable code is
     `cancel_unconfirmed`. Its message names no order (rule 6).
   - That code is the key-only alert DEC-858 item 5 asks for. The binary prints it alone on stderr
     and exits non-zero. A stop carries no `Report`, so the key is not in `Report.alerts`. A
     notification text key would be a notification spec change (§4.2's closed set), which this
     slice does not make.
   - Nothing is placed, cancelled or journaled at the stop. The entry's last state stays the
     executor's `pending_cancel`, which DEC-858 item 5 names as the record that carries it. So no
     journal spec PR is needed.
2. **Every mid-watch reconciliation is gathered.** DEC-858 item 3 says a reconciliation the
   executor asks for mid-watch "is gathered and run through the reconciler". That covers one asked
   for by the answer to a request the watch performs, not only by a step the watch feeds directly.
   The cancel's own read-back is one such answer: an entry that filled as the bound's cancel left
   asks for one. Stepping its answers one by one leaves the executor refusing with
   `AppendUnresolved` before the fill's protection is recorded. That leaves the risk the stop meant
   to remove, so the stricter reading holds (rule 13).
3. **No partial fill is reachable in the paper run, so part 2 pins none.** The production cycle
   sizes one whole share. `paper/artifacts.rs` fixes the increment at `WHOLE_SHARE`, and
   `paper/context.rs` builds the classification action at that quantity, so a proposal of more is
   refused as `classification facts for the proposed action`. A one-share bracket entry cannot fill
   partly, so DEC-858 item 4's partial-fill wait has nothing to wait on here. The executor's
   partial-fill protection is pinned in `mandate-executor` (#1217, #1226).
4. **An open question, not decided here.** The shell refuses every submission after the entry as
   `SecondSubmission` (`one_order_only`), and that includes a protective one. A multi-share entry
   that fills partly would have its one GTC OCO (§5.4) refused, which is protection denied (rule
   13). Admitting protective submissions would loosen the shell's one-order guard, and an agent may
   not take that reading (DEC-176). It goes to the founder before any slice lets the cycle size
   more than one share. Today it cannot arise (item 3). It also cannot arise inside the watch after
   a complete fill, because the watch ends at that wake.

## Rationale

Each reading sends less, or stops sooner, than the alternative (`AGENTS.md` rule 3). Item 2 is the
one that does more: it lets the executor finish recording protection it already owes, and it adds no
request of the watch's own (rule 12, FT-1). Item 4 is written down so that the conflict with rule
13 is visible before the one-share limit is lifted.

## Alternatives considered

- **The cap's alert in `Report.alerts`, with the run ending `Ok`.** Rejected. The run would exit
  zero with an order whose cancel is unconfirmed. Failing closed means the operator sees a stop.
- **One last read at 16:00.** Rejected. "At most until the end" leaves nothing to read at the end.
- **Pinning partial fills with a scripted `partially_filled` answer on a one-share entry.**
  Rejected. The answer could not come from the broker, and the test would pin behaviour on facts
  that cannot occur.
