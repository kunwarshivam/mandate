## Context

Workspace API spec §4.7 names `GET /agents/{id}/positions` and `GET /agents/{id}/orders` for D3:
"ledger and broker quantities, cost basis, risk mark and its age, protection and unprotected
intervals; orders with state, `Unknown` shown as unknown". No schema closes either shape, and
DEC-1020 records that no journaled fold attributes holdings to one agent yet. `web/` (lane W2)
needs both shapes fixed before it can show a paper trade from the API.

## Decision

1. **Two read models.** `read-models/orders` and `read-models/positions` close the responses under
   `schemas/workspace-api/`, each with valid and invalid examples and the mutation sweep. Both carry
   the envelope, `agent_id`, the `account_ref` of the account read (or `null`), and `as_of` naming
   the control stream and the account stream read.
2. **Which account.** The account stream is the one of the connection the agent's deployed mandate
   document names, as `GET /agents/{id}` finds it (DEC-1020 item 3). When the document is not held,
   or the stream is not found, `account_ref` is `null` and the lists are empty (`account_positions`
   is `null`). An agent the control stream does not name is the one `not_found`.
3. **Orders are the agent's own.** An order belongs to the agent when its `OrderRequestRecorded`
   (journal spec §9.5, the order's owner) names the agent. One row per `client_order_id`, in the
   order first submitted; the request is the first `OrderSubmitted`'s and its companion's, and
   `state` and `filled_qty` are the last `OrderStateChanged`'s. Before any `OrderStateChanged`
   both are `null`, not reported, never assumed. `unknown` is served as `unknown`. Another agent's
   orders on a shared account are never listed.
4. **Positions are the agent's own fills, named for what they are.** `positions` holds, per
   instrument, the net quantity of the `FillApplied` and `LateFillApplied` records of the agent's
   own orders (buys add, sells subtract), leaving out an instrument that nets to zero. It is called
   `filled_qty`, not a ledger quantity: corporate actions, transfers and the ledger's corrections
   are the accounting fold's, which no read serves yet. `account_positions` is the account's latest
   `BrokerPositionObserved` per instrument, as `GET /agents/{id}` serves it, labelled the account's.
5. **What is not served is `null`, never invented.** `cost_basis`, `risk_mark` (with its age) and
   `protection` are present on every position and always `null` until the ledger fold, the risk
   mark and the protection fold are read by the API. Each lands as a new decision that gives it a
   type; the coordinator deferred them on 2026-10-19.
6. **Authorization.** Both routes are the matrix row "View agents, positions, decisions"
   (`Permission::ViewAgents`), authorized from the control stream's memberships as the other agent
   reads are (DEC-2110). A principal with no membership gets the one 404 (API-9).

## Rationale

Serving only what the journal records, under names that say what it is, lets `web/` show a paper
trade's orders and the agent's exposure now without a figure the platform cannot stand behind.
Attributing quantities through the order's own companion record is the attribution the executor's
restrictions and exit rules already use.

## Alternatives considered

- **Serve the account's holdings as the agent's positions.** Refused: on a shared account it shows
  another agent's exposure as this one's.
- **Compute cost basis from fill prices here.** Refused: fees, corporate actions and lot rules are
  the accounting fold's (trading spec), and a second reading of them would drift from it.
- **Omit the `null` members until they are served.** Refused: `web/` would have to change its
  types when they land; present-and-`null` says "not reported" in one place.
