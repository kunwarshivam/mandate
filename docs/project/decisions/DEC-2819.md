## Context

The executor process reads each deployment from its confirmed version and registrations
(DEC-2380 item 2). It resolves only a mandate with exactly one pinned instrument and one signal
model. A research agent's mandate pins none (`universe.pinned` false) and names the admitting model
beside a quant one. So the executor resolved nothing for the cell's research agent:
- every opening was held;
- its fills could not name their configuration;
- the broker's answers were not mapped back to asset ids (DEC-2813).

A research agent's instruments come at runtime, inside the envelope. They are admitted through
mandate spec §8.5's checks, the eligibility floor among them, and journaled on the agent stream as
`ThesisProposed` and `ThesisRevised` with the admission's verdict (journal spec §9.4). The cell's
runtime already replays those records into its working universe (DEC-2249), in `mandate-daemon`,
which the executor may not depend on.

## Decision

1. **One replay of the theses.** The record reading of the runtime's research fold moves into
   `mandate-research` as `replay_theses`. It is pure and reads each record's type and payload,
   never judging again. The runtime's research source calls it unchanged. Besides the universe,
   the lineages and the surviving theses, it returns every instrument any record admitted.
2. **What the executor reads for an unpinned mandate.** Only an instrument an admitted thesis
   record names is the agent's (`AGENTS.md` rule 11).
   - An opening, its protection and its binding-gate input are answered only on an instrument in
     the working universe at the opening's instant: admitted, its thesis unexpired and not
     invalidated, and its lineage not retired. The instrument must also have an instrument snapshot
     registered on the deployment's stream.
   - Every instrument the stream ever admitted stays the agent's for exits, fills, marks and the
     broker's ticker map. A removal is exits-only (mandate spec §8.5).
   - A stream, a record or a registration that cannot be read holds openings and never blocks an
     exit.
3. **A research deployment's reviewed artifacts.** These are the mandate, the fee schedule, the
   calendar, the rule set, and each admitted instrument's registered snapshot, all on the
   deployment's own stream as DEC-952 requires. The deployment's model is the one with
   `admits_instruments`, registered as today. A pinned mandate is read as before.
4. **What stays E17-3's.** Mandate spec §8.5 has the executor write `UniverseChanged` on the
   account stream for each admission. The executor's fold does not yet interpret `UniverseChanged`
   (E17-3), so this decision writes none. It only reads the agent stream. That writing is still
   unclaimed, and is the backlog row
   [`E17-3-executor-writes-universe-changed`](../backlog/E17-3-executor-writes-universe-changed.md).

## Rationale

The cell's research workspace needs its admitted instrument traded on the same executor path as a
pinned one. Reading the admission's own journaled verdict, through the runtime's one replay, keeps
the executor's universe the runtime's, and adds no second judgement of §8.5.

## Alternatives considered

- **A second fold of the thesis records in the executor.** Rejected: two folds of one record can
  disagree, and the replay already exists.
- **Write `UniverseChanged` from the executor now.** Rejected: the executor's own fold refuses it
  until E17-3, so the executor would stop on its own record.
- **Treat an unpinned mandate's every instrument as openable.** Rejected: it would let the agent
  open in an instrument its admission never admitted (`AGENTS.md` rule 11).
