## Decision

1. **The core is a pure fold in `mandate-notify`** (DEC-701 item 1): `Dispatcher::step(input,
   context)` returns the drafts, the sends, and the next time it has work due. It reads no clock:
   every input carries its time, and the process wakes it with a tick. The quiet-hours verdict, the
   configured channels, and the random source come from a `Context` trait whose methods have no
   defaults, so no caller can skip the verdict for an `action` or `info` send; a `safety` notice
   never asks for it.
2. **Order inside a step.** `NoticeIssued` is the step's first record (NT-8); recipients are listed
   in ascending id order; sends follow the channels' order in the schema enum.
3. **A user's kill switch is one cause** (spec §3.4): the first `OwnerAlertSent` that names an owner
   command is the cause, and every later record naming the same command issues nothing.
4. **Recipients** are the row's audience narrowed by the receive column, read as data: a listed
   approver who holds only the viewer role gets no `action` notice.
5. **Retry gaps** (spec §5.3) are measured from the failed attempt's outcome: 15 s, 60 s, 5 min,
   then 15 min each. A `safety` notice stops at 24 hours and an `info` notice at 6 hours after its
   cause, each recorded `abandoned` with `retry_window_ended`. An `action` notice stops when the
   approval closes or when its next attempt would fall at or after the deadline, recorded
   `abandoned` with `not_pending`, whichever comes first.
6. **A permanent failure stops that channel for that notice** and is recorded `failed` with its
   reason; whether the address is lost follows from the reason alone (spec §5.6, DEC-720).
7. **`channel_lost`** goes to the member's other channels at once, never to the lost one even if
   the process still lists it, and quiet hours do not apply (it is `safety`).
8. **Quiet hours.** A suppressed `action` push is journaled per channel and never sent later; a
   deferred brief is journaled `deferred_quiet_hours` and sent at the window's end, which the
   verdict carries; an `info` kind with no text key is issued for the pull channels and never
   attempted.

## Rationale

Each item fixes an order or a boundary the spec states only in words, in the direction that sends
a safety notice sooner, sends nothing after an approval stops being pending, and keeps the fold
replayable from its inputs alone.

## Alternatives considered

- **Retry gaps measured from the first attempt.** Rejected: a slow provider would compress the
  later gaps into immediate resends.
- **A default "not quiet" verdict.** Rejected by the lead: an `action` push could then interrupt
  inside quiet hours whenever a caller forgot the verdict.
