## Context

DEC-2307 item 3 has the fold return `AgentModeApplied` when a step changes the effective mode, and
DEC-2308 item 3 names its `restriction` as the action of the rung that changed it. A mode change can
also follow no trigger in its step: a lift (MC-R07's daily loss, MC-R19's cleared hard breach), a
completed goal (MC-R22's `profit_stop`), or a retirement. DEC-2308 item 3 says nothing for those.

## Invariants first

- Every `AgentModeApplied` the fold returns names the cause in its own step (DEC-2307 item 3).
- The fold records a cause; it never decides one. Whether a lift may happen (`AGENTS.md` rule 2) is
  `mandate_spec`'s risk state's to decide, and the fold only writes what that state journaled.
- Nothing here holds an exit, a protective order, a risk exit, or a kill switch (`AGENTS.md` rule 13).

## Decision

1. **With no `RiskLimitTriggered` before it in its step, `AgentModeApplied.restriction` names:**
   - (a) after a lift, the step's last `RiskLimitLifted` before it: its ladder action (the record's
     `action`, such as `scale_sizes`), or its limit's §9.27 journal name when it carries none (such as
     `max_daily_loss`, or `drawdown_ladder[2]` for a hard breach cleared);
   - (b) after `GoalCompleted`, `goal_completed`;
   - (c) after `AgentStopped`, `retired`.

   Any other mode change with no cause in its step fails closed: the view becomes unreadable. With a
   trigger before it, DEC-2308 item 3 stands.
2. **These are spellings, not enum members.** Journal spec §9.6 types `restriction` as free text, so
   no rule refuses another value; the fold's tests pin these.
3. **`RiskLimitTriggered.breach_time_s` is the whole breached interval** up to the step that records
   the trigger, which is the next input after the breach confirms. Mandate spec §5.4's "at most
   `breach_confirm_s`" bounds how long a breach still confirming at the rollover keeps confirming
   against the previous day's E₀, not the duration the trigger records. MC-R06 breaches at
   03:59:30 and latches `resolved_at_rollover` at its next input, the 04:30:00 mark, so it records
   30 + 1800 = 1830 s. The value is a journaled fact; it changes no limit and adds no risk.

## Consequences

- B2b-2b-1's tests pin (a) on MC-R07 (`max_daily_loss`) and MC-R19 (`drawdown_ladder[2]`), and (b) on
  MC-R22 (`goal_completed`); until the implementation PR the fold stops at its stub there. No
  reference case reaches (c) on marks and ticks alone.
- The walk's record check expects these restrictions wherever a case reaches them.
- Item 3 is pinned by the fold's MC-R06 walk. The reference cases carry no `breach_time_s`, since
  their harnesses do not read one; asserting it there is a backlog row. Mandate spec §5.4 gains one
  clarifying sentence citing this decision, as its own change, since the spec guard keeps specs and
  code apart.
