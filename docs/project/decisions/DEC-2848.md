## Context

The executor's own gate decisions write three members that journal spec §9.5's closed
`GateDecided` version 2 does not admit:

- `sized_qty`: a discretionary exit allowed and sized to what the exit ladders' rungs leave of the
  position (DEC-410 item 3);
- `parked`: a ladder step held while no session is open (DEC-260 (18));
- `held_long`: an exit held past its age (ruling D2).

Its fold reads all three back. A park also wrote the legacy `evaluation`, which a reference case
already refuses. So on a real journal:

- a sized discretionary exit's whole batch was refused, and the exit lost on every pass, as
  DEC-2846's owner exit was;
- a park's and a long hold's records were refused, with their owner alerts, and DEC-2805 held
  every opening.

## Decision

1. **A new version.** §9.5 adds `GateDecided` version 3: version 2's members, then `sized_qty`
   (`decimal?`), `parked` (`boolean?`) and `held_long` (`boolean?`), then `risk_clock`. Each is
   present, `null` where the executor writes none.
2. **Three rules bind each member to its decision:**
   - **313:** `sized_qty` only on an `allow` under `account_stream_only`, and above zero;
   - **314:** `held_long` is `true` or `null`, and `true` only on a `hold`;
   - **315:** `parked` is `true` or `null`, and `true` only on a `hold` for `session_closed` or
     `session_unknown` that is also `held_long`.
3. **`evaluation` is not admitted.** `data_profile` already says the decision was the executor's
   own (DEC-457).
4. **Nothing earlier changes.** Versions 1 and 2 are unchanged, and no stored vector's outcome,
   body or hash changes. The regenerated `journal.yaml` and `journal.json` only gain the `gate_v3`
   section, and the vectors stay version 3.
5. **The vectors.** The `gate_v3` section holds:
   - four bases: the chain's binding allow, a sized local allow, a long hold and a park;
   - 6 valid drafts and 21 invalid ones;
   - an oracle that reads the rules directly over every combination of verdict, reason, profile,
     size and both flags;
   - 9 validator mutants and 3 vector mutants, each caught by the cases, and every validator
     mutant by the oracle as well.
6. **Then the code.** It follows in its own change (ES-22): the journal registers version 3, and
   the executor writes it and folds versions 2 and 3. Tests on a judging journal show a sized
   discretionary exit, a park and a long hold journaled.

## Rationale

The members already decide behaviour: the fold sizes, parks and suppresses repeat alerts from
them. The spec only failed to say they exist. Each rule states where the executor writes its member
and refuses any other place, so the journal now proves what it used to refuse outright.

## Alternatives considered

- **Move the three to a companion record.** Rejected. It adds a record and a batch rule, and the
  fold would read two records for one decision.
- **Drop the members and re-derive them in the fold.** Rejected. Parking depends on the calendar at
  the time of the decision, and sizing on the rungs in flight then; neither can be recomputed from
  the record alone.
