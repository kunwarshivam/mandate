## Context

The interim rule has been one agent per account:
- [DEC-2804](DEC-2804.md) item 8 opens no risk fold on an account where a second agent is followed;
- [DEC-2809](DEC-2809.md) item 2 refuses a second agent's deployment;
- [DEC-2846](DEC-2846.md) item 8 carries the gap left by a watchdog exit, which waits wherever a
  position is not exactly one agent's lots.

The founder ruled on 2026-10-27:

> "It's not about stocks. It's about positions. Two agents can have same stocks."

On what happens when the owner sells, outside Mandate, shares the agents hold:

> "Every agent shrinks by full gap."

The trading domain spec already gives each agent its attributed lots, its sub-ledger (§8.1,
§5.5), and ingests external activity as unattributed (§7.1, §11). This decision adds what the ruling
needs on top: who holds what, how much each agent may sell and protect, and what a fold reads.

## Definitions

For one account and one instrument (long-only in v1):
- **N** is the executor's own position in the instrument. It is derived from the journal, so it
  replays. It is reconciled with the broker's by §11, whose mismatch rules are unchanged.
- **Lᵢ ≥ 0, agent i's lots.**
  - Each fill of agent i's own orders (an order is the agent's its `OrderRequestRecorded` names;
    DEC-2393 item 1) adds the quantity it received. For crypto that is net of the asset fee taken
    on a buy (§8.1, §11).
  - Each sell fill of its orders subtracts. That includes an owner exit naming the agent, whose
    fills come out of that agent's lots; an owner exit naming no agent comes out of R.
  - Each release to the owner (DEC-2354) subtracts. The owner may release any of an agent's lots,
    not only its Sᵢ: a release moves no share at the broker, so it adds no risk. It is the owner's
    way to end a deficit.
  - A sell of agent i's that fills beyond Lᵢ (a resting leg after a release) leaves Lᵢ at 0. Its
    excess comes out of R.
  - A split scales Lᵢ by the split's ratio (§8.5). Its rounding residual leaves the position as
    cash in lieu. It comes out of the lots of the holder it is computed on, so N and that holder fall
    together and R is unchanged.
  - The fills of an account-scoped switch's close come out of R first, while R > 0. Then they come
    out of the agents' lots in Pᵢ's order (largest Lᵢ first, ties by agent id). So a close that
    fills in part leaves each remaining share attributed, and a complete one leaves every Lᵢ at 0.
  - A stopped or retired agent's lots still count. They are real attributed shares until sold or
    released.
- **R = N − Σᵢ Lᵢ, the owner's residual.** It is everything no agent's lots hold: unattributed fills
  (§7.1, §11), releases, and the owner's own exits. Being a residual, it replays with N.
- **D = max(0, −R), the deficit**: how far the agents' lots exceed the position, exactly. Only the
  hold (I6) treats a deficit below the instrument's quantity increment, a crypto rounding residue,
  as none. Sᵢ and Pᵢ use D exactly.
- **Sᵢ = max(0, Lᵢ − D)**: agent i's **sell authority** (item 6).
- **Pᵢ, agent i's protective coverage.** The deficit is charged once, across holders, in a fixed
  replayable order: largest Lᵢ first, ties by agent id. Each takes as much of the remaining
  deficit as its Lᵢ allows. So Σᵢ Pᵢ = min(N, Σᵢ Lᵢ) exactly.

## Invariants first

- **I1 Partition.** N = Σᵢ Lᵢ + R holds by R's definition. The checks are every Lᵢ ≥ 0, and the
  fuzz's separate per-holder accumulator with a modeled broker position.
- **I2 Once.** Each fill and partial fill moves exactly one holder's quantity, once, by its fill id.
- **I3 Sell authority.** No discretionary exit or new sell the executor sends for agent i exceeds
  Sᵢ, together with i's working sells. Σᵢ Sᵢ ≤ N.
  - **Proposed (founder), interim:** a discretionary exit for more is trimmed to what Sᵢ leaves.
    With nothing left it is refused `sell_exceeds_available`, the one denial rule 13 and §5.3 rule 4
    allow a discretionary exit, read on Sᵢ (item 6). So that refusal also covers a thesis-ended exit
    (MI-34). It applies to discretionary exits only.
  - The refusal is not a hold. The agent may propose again, and once the deficit ends its Sᵢ is
    restored.
  - Its risk exits and the owner's exits are unaffected (I4).
- **I4 Risk reduction first (rule 13).** A risk exit, an owner exit, an agent-scoped kill switch's
  flatten, or a triggered protective leg, watchdog or bound exit for agent i is sized min(Lᵢ, N).
  It is never sized to Sᵢ, and never held by a deficit.
  - It is clipped to what is still available: N less every working sell in the instrument, as
    protection.rs's `still_selling` counts them. It is never refused while that is above 0.
  - Two rule-13 sells in one step, such as two holders' stops in a deficit, so never sum past N.
  - The gate's `sell_exceeds_available` (DEC-410 item 3) and the broker remain the backstop.
  - The residual this leaves, stated for the founder: in a deficit, such a sell may take shares
    another holder's lots count, because rule 13 puts the risk exit first. Once each holder's legs
    are its own (item 8), another holder's resting legs must not cut such a sell: the exit sequence
    resizes them to the coverage left after it (I9).
- **I5 Kill switch scope.**
  - An agent-scoped switch cancels only the agent's orders (and §5.5's unattributed legs) and sells
    min(Lᵢ, N).
  - An account-scoped switch cancels all and closes N. Its close's fills come out of R, then the
    agents' lots (see the definitions), so it leaves no false deficit, whether it fills in part or
    whole.
  - Neither waits on another agent's state.
- **I6 A deficit holds openings and never an exit.**
  - While D > 0, every opening in the instrument is held, whichever agent's (`attribution_deficit`).
    The owner is alerted once per deficit episode.
  - Openings already working are not cancelled by it. Exits go (I4), and nothing pauses.
  - The deficit ends when R ≥ 0 again: the owner buys, the owner releases D shares of any agents'
    lots (DEC-2354), or the account switch closes the instrument.
  - A release keeps its own rules (DEC-2354; mandate spec §3.1, §5.7, MI-14):
    - it cancels the agent's working orders and protection in the instrument;
    - its loss is carried, so releasing lots never resets a floor or a latched limit.
  - The alert names the instrument and that the owner's buy or release ends the hold.
  - A total sale outside Mandate (N = 0 with lots left) is a deficit of every lot. Openings hold
    until the owner releases them.
  - §7.1's `exits_only` for external activity is unchanged and applies first.
- **I7 Per-agent risk.** Agent i's risk fold steps only its own fills and fees and the counted
  marks of its bound instrument (single-instrument v1, until E6-15m). Another agent's fills and
  fees, the owner's activity and another instrument's marks change nothing in it: its snapshot and
  records are equal with or without them. Its mode is §7.1's and the gate's to change, not this
  equality's.
- **I8 Replay.** N, every Lᵢ, R, D, Sᵢ, Pᵢ and each fold are functions of the journal: a replay
  from any head reaches the same.
- **I9 Every agent-held share is protected once.**
  - Each agent's resting protective legs cover Pᵢ, with an OCO's legs counted once, as its larger
    leg. So the legs together cover min(N, Σᵢ Lᵢ).
  - The broker-side sum of resting sell legs never exceeds N, once the executor has read the change
    that would make it. When Pᵢ falls below an agent's resting coverage, those legs are resized by
    cancel and re-place. That re-placement is an unprotected interval bounded by §5.4's
    `max_unprotected_s`.
  - Shares in R are the owner's, covered only as §5.4 covers the owner's own.
  - Isolation (I3, I9) holds at submission and once a resize is confirmed. In the window before,
    `sell_exceeds_available` and the broker's own reservation for each resting leg are the
    backstop.

## Decision

1. **Founder ruling (Accepted).** Positions are per agent. Several agents, and the owner, may hold
   the same instrument in one account. Each agent's position is its own lots, attributed (I1, I2),
   protected (I9), exited (I3 to I5) and risk-folded (I7) on its own. It settles q10 and q11.
2. **DEC-2804 item 9 is accepted: another instrument's mark steps nothing** in a fold bound to its
   instrument. An own fill or fee on another instrument stays a refusal to decide (DEC-2308 item 4).
   It also replaces DEC-2834 item 4 for marks: while unpriced, such a mark steps nothing and does
   not price. The executor daemon binds every fold at opening (DEC-2804 item 2). A fold opened
   unbound still binds by DEC-2310 item 1. Property: views are equal with or without other
   instruments' marks.
3. **One fold per followed agent.** The risk book opens one fold per followed agent on an account.
   DEC-2804 item 8's `not_opened` for a second followed agent stays until item 9 is complete, and
   then is withdrawn. Item 8's rule for a second instrument stays until E6-15m.
4. **DEC-2809 item 2 (`second_agent_on_account`) stays until item 9 is complete**, so a second agent
   is still refused at deploy rather than held at the fold. It is withdrawn with DEC-2804 item 8's
   rule, by one decision that lane RK writes when item 9 is met.
5. **Attribution in the executor.** `kill::sub_ledger` answers min(Lᵢ, N) on the definitions above,
   for I4's sells. A new sell authority answers Sᵢ for I3's, and coverage answers Pᵢ for I9.
   Account-switch closes and the Lᵢ floor follow I5 and the definitions.
6. **A deficit's reading (founder, Accepted: "Every agent shrinks by full gap").** Each holder's sell
   authority shrinks by the whole deficit (I3), and openings in the instrument hold while D > 0
   (I6). Rule 13 keeps its exits (I4), and protection is charged once (I9).
7. **Per-holder exits (lane X).** A triggered stop, watchdog or bound exit is the triggering leg's
   agent's exit, sized by I4 and journaled under that agent's mandate version. A shared position's
   watchdog therefore needs no `*` intent. When this lands, it closes DEC-2846 item 8's gap and
   supersedes that item's last bullet.
8. **Protection per holder (lane X).** Each agent's legs are sized to Pᵢ (I9).
   - Until then, §5.4's instrument-wide exit sequence stands: an exit cancels the instrument's
     protective orders and re-places protection for what remains. That is safe, because every other
     holder's coverage is re-placed after the exit.
   - Under shared positions it is rewritten to cancel only the exiting agent's legs and any
     unattributed leg.
   - §5.6's two-ladder cap per instrument is reviewed with it.
   - Its acceptance criteria, with the fuzz:
     - a rule-13 exit is never cut by another holder's resting legs (I4);
     - Σ Pᵢ plus the owner's uncovered residual equals N.
9. **"Attribution complete"** means items 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8 are merged on main, with I1 to I9 under
   the fuzz below. Then lane RK's follow-up decision withdraws DEC-2804 item 8's second-agent rule
   and DEC-2809 item 2.

## Confirmation asked of the founder

The reading in I3 is put to the founder through the coordinator: rule 13's "the exits already
allowed sell the whole position", for an agent's discretionary exit, is the agent's Sᵢ.
- It follows from item 6, but it is a reading of rule 13's text. It refuses a sale that would
  reduce risk, so it is not a tightening, and only the founder can confirm it.
- Until the founder confirms it, it applies as the interim: the trim, and at Sᵢ = 0 the refusal.
  It applies to discretionary exits only, never to a risk or owner exit. No account runs two
  agents meanwhile anyway (item 4).

## Worked example

Agents A and B each buy 5 shares, so N = 10, Lᴬ = Lᴮ = 5 and R = 0. The owner then sells 4
outside Mandate:
- N = 6, R = −4, D = 4.
- Sᴬ = Sᴮ = 1. A discretionary exit for either is trimmed to 1. After that, another is refused
  `sell_exceeds_available` until the deficit ends.
- Pᴬ = 1 and Pᴮ = 5. A's 5 is charged the deficit first, by agent id on the tie, so the legs cover
  6 = N.
- A's risk exit sells min(5, 6) = 5 (I4), clipped only by what is still available.
- Every opening in the instrument is held, and the owner is alerted.
- The owner releasing 4 of A's lots gives Lᴬ = 1, R = 0 and D = 0, which ends the deficit.

## Tests (each invariant is a test)

- **A property fuzz** over 2 to 3 agents and the owner. It interleaves:
  - own fills, partial and whole, on equities and on crypto with asset fees;
  - owner buys and sells outside Mandate, including while an agent's stop rests;
  - releases, a split, marks of the instrument and of another;
  - stops firing before and after a resize;
  - watchdog, bound and discretionary exits;
  - agent-scoped and account-scoped kill switches, and a restart.

  It asserts I1 to I9 at every step.
- **The oracle** is an independent per-holder accumulator over the raw generated events, with a
  modeled broker position. It is shown to fail on these seeded bugs:
  - a discretionary sell sized to Lᵢ in a deficit;
  - a fill attributed twice;
  - an account close left unattributed, or a partial close zeroing every lot;
  - two rule-13 sells summing past N;
  - coverage charged per holder;
  - another agent's fill stepping a fold;
  - another instrument's mark stepping a fold.
- **The risk side** gets a property for item 2, and daemon process tests in which two agents on one
  instrument each keep a readable fold that steps only their own fills.

## Consequences

- Trading domain spec v0.24: the definitions and I6 in §8.1, I9 in §5.4, I4 and I5 in §5.5, and
  `attribution_deficit` in §9.1. Registering that code, like `protection_unattributed`, needs the
  journal and alerts registration in the code change, with its own spec change if the journal's
  closed lists need one. Mandate spec v0.16: I7.
- Every reference case is unchanged. The fuzz's cases come with the code.
- Until item 9, a second agent on an account is refused at deploy (item 4), so no account runs two.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep one agent per account.** Overruled by the founder (item 1).
- **Size every sell, the risk exits included, to Sᵢ.** It breaks rule 13: a deficit could shrink
  a flatten to nothing.
- **Charge a deficit pro rata, or to the last buyer.** The founder chose the full gap (item 6).
- **Charge protection per holder, as sell authority is.** It leaves (k − 1)·D real shares under no
  stop (I9).
- **One account-level exit for a shared position.** It would sell the owner's residual and every
  agent's lots, which is the account switch's job.
