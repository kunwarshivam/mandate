## Context

The interim rule has been one agent per account. [DEC-2804](DEC-2804.md) item 8 opens no risk fold
on an account where a second agent is followed. [DEC-2809](DEC-2809.md) item 2 would refuse a second
agent's deployment. [DEC-2846](DEC-2846.md) item 8 carries the open gap: a triggered stop's
watchdog exit waits wherever the position is not exactly one agent's lots. The founder was asked
two things: whether another instrument's mark may step nothing in a fold (q10, DEC-2804 item 9),
and whether agents may share an account or an instrument (q11). He ruled:

> "It's not about stocks. It's about positions. Two agents can have same stocks."

The trading domain spec already holds most of this:
- each agent's attributed lots, its sub-ledger (§8.1, §5.5);
- the agent-scoped kill switch, which sells "exactly the agent's sub-ledger quantity" and never
  another agent's or the owner's shares (§5.5);
- a protective leg's agent (§5.4);
- external activity ingested as unattributed (§11).

What it lacks is a rule for when the agents' claims exceed what the account holds. The code lacks
one exit per holder, a risk book with one fold per agent, and a fold that ignores another
instrument's marks.

## Invariants first

For one account and one instrument, with broker net quantity N (long-only in v1):

- **I1 Partition.** N = Σᵢ Lᵢ + R. Lᵢ ≥ 0 is agent i's lots: the fills of its own orders (an order
  is the agent's its `OrderRequestRecorded` names; DEC-2393 item 1), buys less sells, less what it
  released to the owner (DEC-2354). R is the owner's residual: every unattributed fill (§11),
  release and corporate-action residual. R may be negative (I6).
- **I2 Once.** Every fill and partial fill moves exactly one holder's quantity, once (by
  `fill_id`): its order's agent's, or else R's.
- **I3 Sellable.** Agent i's sellable quantity is Sᵢ = max(0, Lᵢ − D), where D = max(0, −R) is the
  deficit. So Σᵢ Sᵢ ≤ N: no agent's sell can take another holder's shares.
- **I4 Every agent sell is bounded, the ones already resting at the broker included.** No sell
  the executor sends for agent i exceeds Sᵢ at submission, together with i's working sells. That
  covers exits, protective legs, a watchdog or bound exit, and an agent-scoped flatten.
  - A protective leg already resting at the broker is bounded too. When Sᵢ falls below agent i's
    resting protective quantity, for example because the owner sold shares outside Mandate, the
    executor resizes those legs to Sᵢ by cancel and re-place (DEC-2360 item 1). It does this before
    any new protective order in the instrument, so no resting stop can sell more than Sᵢ at the
    broker once the change is read.
  - Until the resize is confirmed, the gate's `sell_exceeds_available` (DEC-410 item 3) is the
    account-level backstop, and the broker holds the shares each leg reserves.
- **I5 Kill switch scope.** An agent-scoped switch cancels only the agent's orders and sells exactly
  Sᵢ. An account-scoped switch cancels all and closes N. Neither depends on another agent's state
  (rule 13).
- **I6 A deficit holds openings and never an exit.** While D > 0, every holder of the instrument
  has its openings in it held (`attribution_deficit`, alerted once). Exits go, sized by I3, and
  nothing pauses.
- **I7 Per-agent risk.** Agent i's risk fold steps only i's own fills and fees and the counted
  marks of its bound instrument. Another agent's fills and fees, the owner's activity and another
  instrument's marks change nothing in it: the views are equal with or without them. Eᵢ is
  computed on Lᵢ, and never on N or R.
- **I8 Replay.** Each holder's quantity, Sᵢ and each fold are functions of the journal: a replay
  from any head reaches the same.

## Decision

1. **Founder ruling (Accepted).** Positions are per agent. Several agents, and the owner, may hold
   the same instrument in one account. Each agent's position is its own lots, attributed (I1, I2),
   protected (I4), exited (I3 to I5) and risk-folded (I7) on its own. This settles q10 and q11.
2. **DEC-2804 item 9 is accepted:** a mark for an instrument other than the fold's bound one steps
   nothing. An own fill or fee on another instrument stays a refusal to decide (DEC-2308 item 4
   guards that, and is unchanged). Property: views are equal with or without other instruments'
   marks.
3. **The risk book opens one fold per followed agent** on an account. DEC-2804 item 8's
   `not_opened` for a second followed agent is withdrawn from the moment item 9 below is complete.
   Until then it stays, as the conservative interim. Its rule for a second instrument stays until
   the multi-instrument risk state (E6-15m).
4. **DEC-2809 item 2 (`second_agent_on_account`) is withdrawn before it lands.** R4c (#2615) keeps
   only item 1 (`second_instrument`).
5. **Sellable quantity** is I3's Sᵢ. `kill::sub_ledger` and every agent-sized sell use it, which
   replaces today's per-agent cap at N, whose sum can exceed N.
6. **Proposed (founder): a deficit's reading.** When the owner's residual goes negative (most
   often the owner selling, outside Mandate, shares the agents hold), the conservative reading is
   I3 and I6: every holder's sellable shrinks by the whole deficit, and openings in the instrument
   wait until R ≥ 0. Some agent-held shares may then be sellable by no agent, but they are never
   lost. An account-scoped switch and the owner's own exits still sell them. The alternative is to
   charge the deficit to the agents pro rata, or to the last buyer. Both are owner policy, so they
   go to the founder.
7. **One exit per holder** for a triggered stop, a watchdog or a bound exit (DEC-2846 item 8,
   option 1). Each holder's exit is sized to its Sᵢ and names its agent. The owner's residual is
   never sold by an agent's exit. Lane X owns this, in the executor's protection and watchdog code.
8. **Protective legs per agent:** each agent's legs are sized to its Sᵢ, re-derived when Sᵢ
   changes (DEC-2360 item 1), so Σ ≤ N.
9. **"Attribution complete"** means items 5, 7 and 8 have landed with I1 to I8 under test. Then
   item 3's withdrawal takes effect.

## Tests (each invariant is a test)

- A property fuzz over 2 to 3 agents and the owner. It interleaves own fills (partial and whole),
  owner buys and sells outside Mandate, releases, marks of the instrument and of another, stops
  firing, watchdog and bound exits, and agent-scoped and account-scoped kill switches. It asserts
  I1 to I8 at every step.
  - It always includes the case of the owner selling, outside Mandate, while an agent's protective
    stop rests. That stop's quantity is resized to the new Sᵢ, and a stop that fires before the
    resize sells no more than the broker allows.
- The oracle is an independent per-holder accumulator over the raw generated events, not the
  executor's fold. It is shown to fail on seeded bugs: a sell sized to Lᵢ in a deficit, a fill
  attributed twice, another agent's fill stepping a fold, and another instrument's mark stepping a
  fold.
- The risk side gets a property for item 2, and process tests in the executor daemon: two agents on
  one instrument each keep a readable fold that steps only their own fills. Once item 9 holds,
  neither is `not_opened`.

## Consequences

- Trading domain spec §5.5 and §8.1 gain Sᵢ and the deficit hold. §5.4 gains one exit per holder.
  Mandate spec §5.2 gains item 2. Spec PRs come first (ES-22).
- Until item 9, a second followed agent on an account still opens no fold, so its openings are
  held. Its exits are not.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep one agent per account.** Overruled by the founder.
- **Size each agent's sell to min(Lᵢ, N), as today.** With a deficit, the sum can exceed N, so one
  agent could sell another's shares.
- **One account-level exit for a shared position.** It would sell the owner's residual and every
  agent's lots at once. That is the account switch's job, not an agent's.
