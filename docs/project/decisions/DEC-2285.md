## Context

On 2026-10-27 the founder accepted a compliance risk: customers' workspaces may read IEX bars
fetched with the platform's data-only paper key (DEC-2283 item 9.1, F1). DEC-2284 settled how
those bars reach customers. The compliance document still says only that each user's market data
comes through their own account, and its question 18 asks counsel about SIP data only. Its text is
the founder's to change.

## Proposed text

1. **Under "Questions for counsel", a new question after 18:**

   > 19. In managed mode the platform fetches IEX daily and minute bars, and the corporate actions
   > that qualify them, with one platform-owned Alpaca paper data key. It stores them in each
   > workspace's point-in-time store, where that workspace's signal models and liquidity checks
   > read them; one central fetcher holds the key. Is serving those bars to customers'
   > workspaces redistribution, or another use, under Alpaca's market-data terms and IEX's, and
   > what license or per-workspace keys does Mandate need? Until answered, the founder accepts
   > this as a known risk (DEC-2283 item 9.1).

   The questions after it are renumbered.
2. **Under "Posture safeguards in the product", one sentence:**

   > Market data for trading decisions (quotes, marks, gate inputs) comes only through the user's
   > own broker connection. The one exception is stored IEX bars fetched centrally with a
   > platform key for signal models and liquidity checks, a risk the founder has accepted until a
   > data license or per-workspace keys are in place (DEC-2283, E2-20).

## What happens on acceptance

When the founder accepts this, with or without edits, a docs-only PR applies the text to
`docs/product/08-compliance-and-regulatory.md`. This decision's status then records the founder's
acceptance and the PR.
