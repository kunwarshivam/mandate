## Context

On 2026-10-27 the founder accepted a compliance risk: customers' workspaces may read IEX bars
fetched with the platform's data-only paper key (DEC-2283 item 9.1, F1). DEC-2284 settled how
those bars reach customers. The compliance document still says only that each user's market data
comes through their own account, and its question 18 asks counsel about SIP data only. Its text is
the founder's to change.

## Proposed text

1. **Under "Questions for counsel", a new last question, 42:** it is appended, not inserted, because
   other documents cite the existing questions by number.

   > 42. For paper workspaces on managed cells, the platform fetches IEX daily and minute bars,
   > and the corporate actions that qualify them, with one platform-owned Alpaca paper data key,
   > and stores them in each workspace's point-in-time store. That workspace's signal models, its
   > liquidity check and the gate's liquidity figures read them. Live workspaces use SIP through
   > their own connection instead (question 18). Today only the founder's own workspace reads
   > these bars; customers' workspaces will receive them from one central fetcher that holds the
   > key, once it is built. Is serving those bars to customers' workspaces redistribution, or
   > another use, under Alpaca's market-data terms and IEX's, and what license or per-workspace
   > keys does Mandate need? Until answered, the founder accepts this as a known risk (DEC-2283
   > item 9.1).
2. **Under "Posture safeguards in the product", one sentence:**

   > Quotes and marks come only through the user's own broker connection. The one exception to
   > own-connection market data is the stored IEX bars of paper workspaces, fetched with a
   > platform key and read by signal models, the liquidity check and the gate's liquidity
   > figures: a risk the founder has accepted until a data license or per-workspace keys are in
   > place (DEC-2283, E2-20). Customers' workspaces receive them only through one central
   > fetcher (DEC-2284), and until it is built only the founder's workspace has them.

## What happens on acceptance

When the founder accepts this, with or without edits, a docs-only PR applies the text to
