# DEC-2112: An agent's own fill that cannot be read fails its positions read

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (agent: a reversible engineering reading under DEC-79 and DEC-176; it only refuses, and loosens no rule) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-19 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | an agent, under DEC-79 (`claude-code`, lane MB, assigned by the coordinator on claim [#1443](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/1443)) |
| **Story or claim** | E11-9's server-side reads, claim #1443; [workspace API spec](../../specs/workspace-api.md) §4.7 (D3); [DEC-2111](DEC-2111.md) item 4 |

## Context

[DEC-2111](DEC-2111.md) item 4 serves `positions` as the net filled quantity of the agent's own
orders, from the `FillApplied` and `LateFillApplied` records its orders' companions attribute to
it. It does not say what the read answers when one of those fills cannot be read: an envelope that
does not parse, or a `side`, `instrument_id` or `qty_gross` that is missing or not in its form.
Skipping it would serve a quantity built from part of the agent's fills as if it were whole.

## Decision

1. **An unreadable own fill fails the read.** When a fill of an order the agent owns has an
   envelope that does not parse, no `instrument_id`, a `qty_gross` that is not a canonical
   quantity, or a `side` other than `buy` or `sell`, `GET /agents/{id}/positions` answers 503
   `journal_unavailable` and serves no position. It is never skipped (`AGENTS.md` rule 3).
2. **Another agent's fill is not this agent's to refuse.** A fill of an order the agent does not
   own is passed over without being read, as DEC-2111 item 4 passes over its quantity.

## Rationale

A position that silently omits a fill understates or overstates the agent's exposure, and the
owner reads it as complete. Failing the read is the answer that adds no risk, and the journal's
closed `FillApplied` schema means it should not happen in practice.

## Alternatives considered

- **Skip the fill and serve the rest.** Refused: the served quantity would be wrong with no sign of
  it.
- **Serve the instrument with a `null` quantity.** Refused: the other instruments would still be
  served from the same partly read stream, and the schema's `filled_qty` is not nullable.
