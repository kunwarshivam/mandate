## Decision

1. **Both, from day one.** A customer's owner and explain runs, like every model call of theirs, are
   paid either from the plan's model allowance or by the owner's own provider key (DEC-432 item 16),
   at the owner's choice. The gateway meters every call per workspace either way (`key_owner`,
   inference spec §7.1), and every cap still applies.
2. **What still holds.** A customer workspace's desk calls a model only once DEC-2241 (the wording)
   and DEC-2243 (what may reach a hosted provider) are also accepted (agent harness spec §15.1).
   Until then it serves the deterministic fallback: the agent's latest 10 decisions and its current
   mode, no model call, no cost. The platform's own demo and development workspaces use the
   platform's key, metered against their configured budgets and the deployment cap (DEC-997's $50 a
   month there).

## Rationale

Spending and pricing are the founder's (DEC-79), and the founder answered. The gateway's per-workspace
meter and `key_owner` already serve both ways of paying, so the answer needs no new mechanism.

## Alternatives considered

- **A plan allowance only, or the owner's key only.** The founder chose both.
- **Let customer workspaces call on the platform's key under the $50 cap.** That cap is the
  platform's demo and development spend, not a customer limit (the founder, 2026-10-17).
