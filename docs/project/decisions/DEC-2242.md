## Decision (proposed)

*Relayed answer, not yet an acceptance:* on 2026-10-17 the coordinator session relayed that the
founder chose both a plan allowance and an optional bring-your-own key, from day one (lane AH
records it with DEC-2203 item 16, the one canonical record of the who-pays answer; this file
follows it and adds only the desk's conditions). This decision becomes Accepted when the founder accepts it in
their own words; until then the conservative option below stays in force.

1. **Open for the founder:** whether a customer's owner and explain runs are paid by the platform's key
   (and priced into the plan), billed as model tokens at cost plus margin (HLD §10), or paid by a
   customer's own key (DEC-432 item 16); and whether a plan includes a desk allowance.
2. **In force meanwhile, the conservative option** (with DEC-2241 and DEC-2243, all three must be
   accepted before a customer workspace's desk calls a model): owner and explain runs run only on the platform's key in
   the platform's own demo and development workspaces, metered against their configured budgets and
   the deployment cap (DEC-997's $50 a month there). A customer workspace's desk serves the
   deterministic fallback (agent harness spec §15.5): the agent's latest 10 decisions and its current mode, no model
   call, no cost.

## Rationale

Spending and pricing are the founder's (DEC-79). The fallback keeps the feature's records and links
available everywhere without anyone paying for a call that has not been priced.

## Alternatives considered

- **Bill customers at cost plus margin now.** A pricing decision; not an agent's.
- **Let customer workspaces call on the platform's key under the $50 cap.** That cap is the
  platform's demo and development spend, not a customer limit (the founder, 2026-10-17).
