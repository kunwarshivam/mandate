# DEC-2860: The infra apply makes the founding Job, since no service account may make a control Job

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (agent: a reversible engineering decision under DEC-79 that keeps DEC-2046's admission rule exactly as it is and loosens nothing) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-31 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | An agent (Claude Code), on the founder's instruction to get staging ready |
| **Story or claim** | Staging apply 41165017569; supersedes [DEC-2114](DEC-2114.md) item 4's hook only; [DEC-2046](DEC-2046.md) |

## Decision

1. With `founding.enabled`, the workspace chart no longer renders the `founding` Job as a Helm
   hook. It renders the same Job, with no Helm annotation, as the key `job.json` of the ConfigMap
   `founding-job`. The Job is unchanged: the control process's pod, image, role, journal
   settings, egress and hardening, running `workspace found --open` for `founding.member`.
2. Staging's infra apply makes the Job after it resets a stalled release, as the apply's own
   identity against the cluster (a DigitalOcean cluster-credentials token, which is a user and not
   a Kubernetes service account; the first staging apply after this change is what proves it):
   - it waits up to 300 seconds for the workspace HelmRelease to be Ready, so the Job is the new
     release's and runs after that release's migration, and goes on past that, saying so;
   - it looks for the ConfigMap up to 30 times, 10 seconds apart, while the release upgrades;
   - it requires the ConfigMap to hold a Job named `founding`;
   - it deletes the last run, makes this one from the ConfigMap's manifest on standard input,
     and waits up to 240 seconds for it to complete.
   It logs `founding: founded`, or that the Job did not complete. That Job stays in place, so
   verify names its refusal by class, as before.
3. Everything else in DEC-2114 stands: the founder's subject never reaches the cluster, a re-run
   for the same member commits nothing, and verify reports the `founding` Job by its Complete
   condition.

## Rationale

Every staging apply since founding was turned on (#3024) failed its workspace release with
`hook-failed, forbidden`. The founding Job's pods carry `mandate.dev/process: control`. The
admission policy `mandate-control-jobs` (DEC-2046) refuses a control Job made by any service
account, and helm-controller makes hooks as one. So the founding grant never ran, and the founder
had no membership. DEC-2046 exists so that no workload can mint a pod the signature webhooks skip.
Exempting helm-controller would weaken that rule, which is the founder's to decide (DEC-79). The
infra apply is not a service account: it runs in the `infra-staging` environment, whose required
reviewer is the founder, and it already writes the vault and the founder's subject link the same
way.

## Alternatives considered

- **Exempt helm-controller's service account from `mandate-control-jobs`.** Rejected: anything
  that could change a HelmRelease could then mint a control pod, which the signature webhooks skip.
- **Give the founding Job a process label of its own.** Rejected for now: it would need a journal
  role, network policies and a vault identity of its own, for a one-off grant the control role
  already makes.
- **Turn founding off once it has run.** Rejected: it never ran, and DEC-2114 wants every apply to
  re-assert the grant.
