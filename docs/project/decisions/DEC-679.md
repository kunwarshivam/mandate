# DEC-679: The host CLI's E7-11 slice may exceed ES-13's 400 lines by 26

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-10-09 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | The coordinator (size waiver ruling, 2026-10-09), on the L2 lead's request |
| **Story or claim** | E7-11, claim #764, #907 (tests #836) |

## Decision

1. #907, the implementation of `mandate connection record` and its `run`, may change 426 lines
   (411 added, 15 deleted) against ES-13's 400: about 340 in `crates/mandate-cli/src/connection.rs`
   and about 92 in the `commit_caused` helper DEC-802 item 8 names, in `src/control.rs`.
2. It grows no further. A later change that needs more lines goes in its own PR.

## Rationale

The overrun is 26 lines. Splitting the helper into its own PR would land about 92 lines that
nothing calls until #907 does, so no live test could kill its mutants there. One reviewed PR with
zero missed mutants is the safer unit.

## Alternatives considered

- **Split the `control.rs` helper first.** Rejected: untested on its own until #907 lands.
- **Trim the implementation to 400.** Rejected: the lines are the request, check and binding rules
  #836's tests pin, and cutting them would mean merging checks into less readable code.
