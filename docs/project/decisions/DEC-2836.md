## Context

The account risk fold holds one position. [DEC-2308](DEC-2308.md) item 4 binds it to the first
instrument a row names and never resets it, and [DEC-2804](DEC-2804.md) item 2 binds it to the
deployment's reviewed instrument. A research agent's instruments come at runtime, from its admitted
theses ([DEC-2819](DEC-2819.md)), so two of its ordinary paths hold every opening for good once its
account has traded:
- its deployment had no surviving thesis at the deployment instant, so the fold binds nothing it
  can open in (DEC-2819 item 4);
- its thesis moves from A to B: A is invalidated and sold, B is admitted, and the fold stays bound
  to A.

A risk state over every instrument (DEC-2308 item 5) is its own change. This proposal lets the
one-instrument fold follow the agent's one instrument instead, only at a moment that cannot move a
limit: while the agent holds nothing, has nothing working, and nothing it ordered can still fill.

## Scope

Only an agent whose confirmed version does not pin its universe, has `universe.max_instruments` 1,
and allows exactly one asset class. Every other version's fold is unchanged: a pinned one binds as
DEC-2308 item 4 says, and one with `max_instruments` above 1 opens no fold and is refused at deploy
([DEC-2804](DEC-2804.md) item 8, [DEC-2809](DEC-2809.md) item 1). A version that allows two asset
classes never rebinds; its openings stay held as today, and the owner sees the executor's
`opening_held` alert. A version change (R2b) ends any rebinding under the old version, as it does
every other fold reading.

## Invariants first

- **A bind leaves every limit's state as it was.** E, H, E₀, C, L, the latched limits, the rungs
  and their timers, the daily latch and its rollover, the breach confirmations and hard waits, the
  tripwire latches, the agent restrictions, the goal's state and the mode are the same immediately
  after the bind as immediately before it. With Q = 0, E = A + cash, so no price enters E.
- **No position is ever outside the fold.** A bind happens only when no order of the agent can
  still fill, so the agent never holds an instrument the fold is not bound to.
- **The risk state never prices one instrument at another's quote** (DEC-2308 item 4's guard).
- **A bind never shortens a wait.** No timer is credited faster after a bind than before it.
- **The fold remains a function of the journal's rows.** Every input to the bind is fixed by rows
  journaled at or before the bind's row, so a replay from any head binds at the same row. No new
  record type is written.
- **Nothing here holds an exit**, a protective order, a risk exit or a kill switch (`AGENTS.md`
  rule 13). Where a condition is unmet, the fold stays bound and openings stay held.

## Proposal

1. **When.** The fold binds, or rebinds, to instrument B at the first account-stream row after
   which all of these hold:
   - the agent's working universe, as its `UniverseChanged` rows on the account stream fold to it
     ([DEC-2823](DEC-2823.md) items 4 and 6, which keep that fold equal to the universe the agent
     stream's theses derive), is exactly {B};
   - B is not the bound instrument;
   - item 2 holds.

   The row is stepped first, as before; the bind follows it, before the next row. A fold whose
   opening instant already finds the universe at {B} with the agent flat binds B at its opening,
   the universe read only from the rows at or before DEC-2804 item 2's boundary row.
   A fold bound to nothing binds the same way, and no mark or fill binds the fold of a version in
   scope: a mark naming an instrument that is not bound steps as DEC-2308 item 4 says.
2. **Flat.** At the row:
   - the fold's Q is zero, and `dividends_due` is zero;
   - every order of the agent, in any instrument, protective legs and an exit ladder's rungs
     included, is in a terminal state (none `PendingSubmit`, working, `PendingCancel` or
     `Unknown`);
   - a `ReconciliationRun` with result `clean` and a non-null activity checkpoint lies after, in
     journal `seq`, the row that moved the last of those orders to its terminal state, so the
     broker's activity has been read past it and no late fill
     (`LateFillApplied`, a fill after a terminal state, trading spec §5.7) of such an order is still to come;
   - the fold's `owed()` is empty.

   When any one fails, the fold stays bound, openings stay held, and the next row is judged
   again. A `mismatch`, an `adopted` run, or a null checkpoint does not count. An opening intent
   for an instrument outside the universe is never ordered, and an approval granted for one is
   judged again at the binding gate when it is processed, against the universe at that instant
   (DEC-2819 item 2), so a pending approval for A is never ordered once A is removed. An exit
   intent needs a position. So no intent can put an order on the account between the row and the
   bind.
3. **What a bind changes, and how.** A bind carries the fold's state across and changes only what
   belongs to the instrument. It is never a new opening (`RiskState::open`), which would set
   E = H = E₀ = C to the allocation and clear every latch: the reset DEC-2804 item 2 refused for a
   redeployment.
   - **Changed**: the bound instrument; the mark, which no longer exists (item 5); the mark's age;
     the instrument restrictions, which start empty for B.
   - **Carried, unchanged**: the allocation, cash, H, E₀, C, the inherited loss and net
     contributions, the clock, the scale rungs and their timers, the confirmations and hard waits,
     the latched set, the daily latch and rollover, the tripwire latches, the agent restrictions,
     the mode, the goal's state (`on_complete`, `disarmed`, `stepping`), the asset class, which the
     version's one allowed class fixes, and `mark_max_age_s`, the value the fold opened with, which
     is not read again. For a research version, which reviews no instrument, the fold opens with
     the staleness limit of the data profile its one asset class names (§5.2); R4b's opening fixes
     the source, and this proposal adds none. Every account-fold field that is not the
     instrument's is carried too: the agent's orders and fills, what it owes, its opening instant,
     and the calendar, which the asset class fixes.
   - **Outside the fold, untouched**: the re-entry cooldown, orders per day, the day-trade budget,
     settlement and conduct state are the gate's and the executor's, keyed by instrument group or
     account. A bind changes none of them; churning the universe never resets the cooldown (§5.3).
   - A's restrictions are not carried and nothing is journaled for them: A stays unopenable through
     the universe (DEC-2819 item 2). A reader of instrument restrictions reads them for the bound
     instrument only.
4. **Restrictions are scoped to their instrument.** A `UniverseChanged` row naming an instrument
   that is not the bound one changes none of the bound instrument's restrictions. Today
   `change_universe` applies a change to the one position whatever instrument it names, so B's
   admission would lift A's `removed_instrument` while A is still held. The fix is backlog row
   E17-3a's, and it is a tightening that does not wait for this proposal.
5. **A bound fold is unpriced** ([DEC-2834](DEC-2834.md)) until its first counted sane mark of B,
   at or after the bind's row. An own fill in B before it is booked with E unknown (DEC-2834 item
   2), never valued at A's last mark. While unpriced, nothing trips or lifts, so a pending
   confirmation or lift waits; that only delays, and the agent's openings are held meanwhile. An
   equity bound after the close stays unpriced until the next regular session, deliberately.
6. **What the executor marks.** The executor marks an agent's instruments only where the risk
   state, a position or an order needs it: its bound instrument, and every instrument in which the
   agent holds a position or has a non-terminal order, protective legs included. A stopped agent's
   leftover positions and orders stay marked, as today. It no longer marks every instrument a
   record ever admitted, so no mark of B reaches a fold still bound to A, and B is first marked
   after its bind. The set is computed after folding every committed row, immediately before the
   marks' own append, so a mark is never written for an instrument a committed row has unbound.
   The readers of `MarkUpdated` need nothing more:
   - the binding gate reads the mark of the instrument it opens in, which is the bound one
     (`mandate-gate-context`);
   - the positions read and the accounting's reporting mark value held instruments
     (`mandate-gate-context` positions, `mandate-accounting`);
   - the runtime's `ReferenceMark` for an approval and its re-validation (DEC-156 item 3) is read
     for the proposal's instrument, which is the bound one, and is nullable;
   - the composer reads daily bars ([DEC-2827](DEC-2827.md)); the executor's own fold and the
     shell's tracer only record marks.
7. **The binding gate.** An opening on a traded account is answered only in the bound instrument,
   on a readable, priced view (DEC-2804 item 7, DEC-2834). The first such opening after a bind
   waits for B's first mark, at most one marking interval (DEC-2380 item 3). An opening on an
   untraded account is answered from the allocation (DEC-2802) and does not read the fold.
8. **If a fill still names A after a bind**, against item 2, the fold refuses to decide on it and
   the view is unreadable, as for any instrument it is not bound to (DEC-2308 item 4): openings are
   held, exits, protective orders, risk exits and the kill switch go on, and the late fill's own
   reconciliation alerts the owner. Such a fill is the residual item 2 rules out; it is never
   stepped against B's mark.
9. **Replay of journals written before this proposal.** A journal whose marks named an admitted
   instrument the fold was not bound to replays to the unreadable view it had then; this proposal
   changes what the executor writes, not how an existing row is read.
10. **Order of work.** E17-3a (the account fold reads `UniverseChanged`, instrument-scoped as item
    4 says) lands first and on its own. If the founder accepts this proposal: the mandate spec §5.2
    amendment with reference cases in `mandate.yaml` (a bind carrying a latched rung, a pending
    lift timer, a daily latch and a confirmation; a bind refused for each item-2 condition) and the
    reference fuzz and mutants, then the code (backlog row E6-15r), after R4b.

## Tests (each item's)

- A property test with an independent oracle: random sequences of marks, fills, late fills, fees,
  ticks, `UniverseChanged` rows, order states and reconciliation runs for one research agent. The
  oracle replays the rows its own way, deciding item 1's row and item 2's conditions, and asserts:
  - every carried field of item 3 is equal immediately before and after each bind;
  - no bind happens while any item-2 condition fails;
  - after a bind the view is unpriced until a counted sane mark of B;
  - a replay from seq 1 binds at the same rows.

  The oracle is shown to fail on seeded bugs: a bind through `open`; a bind with Q above zero; a
  bind that resets any timer; a bind that keeps A's mark or A's restrictions; a bind before a
  passed reconciliation after the last terminal order.
- Item 4: B's admission while A is held leaves A's `removed_instrument` in force.
- Item 6: the executor marks every instrument with a position, a non-terminal order or a
  protective leg, a stopped agent's included, whatever the universe holds, and no other admitted
  instrument; a pass that sells A and binds B writes no mark of A after the bind's row.
- An A→B thesis move on the executor process's fixture journal: A held and sold, the reconciliation
  passes, B bound, B marked, B's opening answered.

## Not covered

- A risk state over several instruments (DEC-2308 item 5) and a second agent on one account
  (DEC-2809) are unchanged.
- [DEC-2804](DEC-2804.md) item 9 (another instrument's mark steps nothing) stays the founder's.
  This proposal relies on item 6, not on it, and does not relax it: a mark naming an instrument
  the fold is not bound to still makes the view unreadable.
- Two surviving theses at once: no single B, so no bind, and openings stay held.

## Alternatives considered

- **Bind at the agent's first opening intent.** Rejected: the gate judges that intent on a view
  that would already need to be bound and priced in B, and the intent is not on the account stream
  when it is judged; a new record type would be needed.
- **Let another instrument's mark step nothing (DEC-2804 item 9).** Not relied on: it is the
  founder's, and it would not cover a late fill.
- **A new opening at the bind.** Rejected (item 3): it resets every limit.
- **Read B's asset class and staleness from its registered snapshot.** Rejected: the snapshot's
  registration is on the control stream and not anchored to the bind's row, so a replay could bind
  at another row. The scope's single asset class and the deployment's staleness fix both.
