## Context

The account risk fold holds one position. [DEC-2308](DEC-2308.md) item 4 binds it to the first
instrument a row names and never resets it. [DEC-2804](DEC-2804.md) item 2 binds it to the
deployment's reviewed instrument. A research agent's instruments come at runtime, from its admitted
theses ([DEC-2819](DEC-2819.md)), so two of its ordinary paths hold every opening for good once its
account has traded:
- its deployment had no surviving thesis at the deployment instant, so the fold binds nothing it
  can open in (DEC-2819 item 4);
- its thesis moves from A to B: A is invalidated and sold, B is admitted, and the fold stays bound
  to A.

A risk state over every instrument (DEC-2308 item 5) is its own change and the founder's question.
This decision lets the one-instrument fold follow the agent's one instrument instead, at the only
moment that cannot move a limit: while the agent holds nothing and has nothing working.

## Invariants first

- **A bind while flat leaves every limit's state as it was.** E, H, E₀, C, L, the latched limits,
  the rungs and their timers, the daily latch and its rollover, the breach confirmations and hard
  waits, the tripwire latches, the agent restrictions and the mode are the same immediately after
  the bind as immediately before it. With Q = 0, E = A + cash, so no price enters E.
- **The risk state never prices one instrument at another's quote** (DEC-2308 item 4's guard).
- **A bind never shortens a wait.** No timer is credited faster after a bind than before it.
- **The fold remains a function of the account stream.** A replay from any head binds at the same
  row, and no new record type is written.
- **Nothing here holds an exit**, a protective order, a risk exit or a kill switch (`AGENTS.md`
  rule 13). Where a condition is unmet, the fold stays bound as before and openings stay held.

## Decision

1. **When.** The fold of an agent whose confirmed version does not pin its universe binds, or
   rebinds, to instrument B at the first account-stream row, at or after both of these, at which
   item 2 holds:
   - the `UniverseChanged` (admitted) of B for the agent ([DEC-2823](DEC-2823.md));
   - the row that leaves the agent flat.

   B must be the agent's one surviving instrument at that row: the working universe the agent's
   `UniverseChanged` rows on the account stream fold to holds exactly B, as [DEC-2827](DEC-2827.md)
   item 3 reads it for the composer and [DEC-2819](DEC-2819.md) item 2 for the executor. B differs
   from the bound instrument; a fold bound to nothing binds the same way. The row is first stepped
   as before; the bind follows it, and then the next row. A pinned version's fold never rebinds:
   DEC-2308 item 4 is unchanged for it.
2. **Flat.** All of these hold at the row:
   - the fold's Q is zero;
   - no order of the agent is `PendingSubmit`, working, `PendingCancel` or `Unknown`, in any
     instrument, protective legs and an exit ladder's rungs included;
   - no risk record the fold owes is unjournaled or in doubt;
   - no dividend receivable waits for a mark (`dividends_due` is zero);
   - B's asset class is the bound instrument's (item 4).

   When any one fails, the fold stays bound, its openings in B are held, and the next row is
   judged again.
3. **What a bind changes, and how.** A bind carries the fold's state across and changes only what
   belongs to the instrument. It is never a new opening (`RiskState::open`), which would set
   E = H = E₀ = C to the allocation and clear every latch: the reset DEC-2804 item 2 refused for a
   redeployment.
   - **Changed**: the bound instrument; the mark, which no longer exists (item 5); the mark's age;
     `mark_max_age_s`, from B's registered snapshot (DEC-2804 item 2); the instrument restrictions,
     which start empty for B. A's `removed_instrument` stays the reason A is not openable, through
     the universe (DEC-2819 item 2), and does not carry to B; `stale_mark` never holds while Q is
     zero.
   - **Carried, unchanged**: the allocation, cash, H, E₀, C, the inherited loss and net
     contributions, the clock, the scale rungs and their timers, the confirmations and hard waits,
     the latched set, the daily latch and rollover, the tripwire latches, the agent restrictions,
     the mode, the goal's state, and every account-fold field that is not the instrument's (the
     agent's orders and fills, what it owes, its opening instant).
4. **No bind across asset classes.** A risk state counts a scale rung's lift and active time, and
   staleness, in regular-session seconds for an equity and in every second for crypto (§5.2, §5.5).
   A bind from an equity to crypto would credit a pending lift faster. So a bind keeps the asset
   class: a B of another class fails item 2, and openings stay held.
5. **A bound fold is unpriced** ([DEC-2834](DEC-2834.md)) until its first counted sane mark of B,
   at or after the bind's row. An own fill in B before it is booked with E unknown, as DEC-2834
   item 2 says, never valued at A's last mark.
6. **What the executor marks.** The executor marks an agent's instruments only where the risk
   state, a position or an order needs it: its bound instrument, and every instrument in which the
   agent holds a position or has an order of item 2's states, protective legs included. It no
   longer marks every instrument a record ever admitted. So no mark of B reaches a fold still bound
   to A, which DEC-2308 item 4 would make unreadable, and B is first marked after its bind, which
   prices the fold. An instrument the agent holds, or protects, is always marked, whatever its
   universe says. No other reader needs a mark of an instrument the agent neither holds, orders,
   nor is bound to: the binding gate reads the mark of the instrument it opens in, which is the
   bound one; the positions read values held instruments; the runtime's approval `reference_mark`
   is nullable and read for the bound instrument; the composer reads daily bars (DEC-2827).
7. **The binding gate.** An opening is answered only in the bound instrument, on a readable,
   priced view (DEC-2804 item 7, DEC-2834). The first opening after a bind waits for B's first
   mark, at most one marking interval (DEC-2380 item 3).
8. **The account fold reads `UniverseChanged`.** The fold steps the executor's `UniverseChanged`
   rows (backlog row `E17-3-risk-fold-reads-universe-changed`) before or with this decision's code:
   item 1 reads them, and today the fold cannot owe its own (`append_failed`).

## Tests (each item's)

- A property test with an independent oracle: random sequences of marks, fills, fees, ticks,
  `UniverseChanged` rows and order states for one research agent. The oracle replays the rows its
  own way, deciding item 1's row and item 2's conditions, and asserts:
  - every carried field of item 3 is equal immediately before and after each bind;
  - no bind happens while any item-2 condition fails, or across asset classes;
  - after a bind the view is unpriced until a counted sane mark of B;
  - a replay from seq 1 binds at the same rows.

  The oracle is shown to fail on seeded bugs: a bind through `open`, a bind with Q above zero, a
  bind across classes, a bind that keeps A's mark.
- The executor marks every instrument with a position, a working or `Unknown` order, or a
  protective leg, whatever the agent's universe holds, and marks no other admitted instrument.
- An A→B thesis move on the executor process's fixture journal: A held and sold, B admitted, B
  bound at the flat row, B marked, B's opening answered.

## Not covered

- A risk state over several instruments (DEC-2308 item 5) and a second agent on one account
  (DEC-2809) are unchanged.
- [DEC-2804](DEC-2804.md) item 9 (another instrument's mark steps nothing) stays Proposed and the
  founder's. This decision relies on item 6, not on it.
- Two surviving theses at once: no single B, so no bind, and openings stay held.

## Alternatives considered

- **Bind at the agent's first opening intent.** Rejected: the gate judges that intent on a view
  that would already need to be bound and priced in B, and the intent is not on the account stream
  when it is judged; a new record type would be needed.
- **Let another instrument's mark step nothing (DEC-2804 item 9).** Not taken here: it relaxes when
  a view fails closed, which is the founder's. Item 6 reaches the same result by not writing those
  marks.
- **A new opening at the bind.** Rejected (item 3): it resets every limit.
