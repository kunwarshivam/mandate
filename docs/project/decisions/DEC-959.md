## Context

R11, observed at the rehearsal's 2l: after the MCP session's connect (`initialize`,
`notifications/initialized`, `tools/list`: three calls), the live start reads the agentic
account's snapshot for the lapse (DEC-947 item 1): `get_portfolio`, `get_equity_positions` and
`get_equity_orders`, each behind its own `get_accounts` check, six calls. The preflight then read
the same snapshot again, with the tradability and the quote, nine calls more. The ordinary bucket
of `TransportConfig::CONSERVATIVE` (connections spec §6.5: 10 tokens, one more a second) was
empty by then, and a throttled call was a refusal, so the preflight's first `get_portfolio`
refused `Throttled` and the run stopped at `Reconcile: the agentic account's snapshot` with
nothing sent.

## Decision

1. **The preflight stands on the lapse's snapshot while it is fresh.** `live_start` keeps the
   snapshot its lapse counted as a `LapseSnapshot` (mandate-paper), with the run clock read just
   before the snapshot's first read was sent, and returns it beside the session it was read
   through; `run` hands it to that session's `Session::preflight` and nowhere else, so only the
   same run and the same session ever use it. The Robinhood session's preflight
   (`robinhood_reads`) reuses it when, at the preflight's clock, it is at most the rule set's
   `iex_quote_max_age_s` old (10 s in the fixture), the bound included, counted from `sent`, as
   DEC-945 item 2 counts the age of Robinhood's quote, and when it lists no position record and
   no working order: it then reads only the tradability, behind its own `get_accounts` check,
   and the quote (`RobinhoodConnector::preflight_facts_on`, which refuses an unregistered
   ticker before any read, as DEC-956 item 1 has `preflight_facts` do). The lapse is read before
   the preflight registers the pinned ticker (DEC-956 item 1), so a position or order in it
   would name its instrument by the broker's ticker rather than the asset id; the lapse already
   refuses a live run on any such record (DEC-529 item 3), and the reuse refuses it again rather
   than lean on that. A snapshot older than the bound, one across which the clock ran backwards
   (its age cannot be judged, rule 3), one listing any position or order, or none is never
   reused: the whole account is read again, exactly as before. Paper
   runs, which keep no snapshot, are unchanged.
2. **A read waits a bounded time for its token; nothing else waits.** `mandate-mcp` gains
   `CallClass::Read`, which draws on the ordinary bucket only, never the reserved one. When the
   ordinary bucket is empty, the transport asks the budget when its next token comes
   (`RateBudget::read_token_in`); if that is within `TransportConfig::read_wait` of the call
   (2 s in the conservative default, two refill periods), it waits for it and asks again,
   and otherwise refuses `Throttled` at once with nothing sent, so no read is ever refused after
   waiting past the bound and none skips a check. The budget's lock is never held across a wait.
   Every broker read of the Robinhood connector is a `Read`: `get_accounts`, the scoped reads
   (portfolio, positions, orders, tradability) and the quote. `CallClass::Ordinary` (openings,
   their review, and the session's handshake and listing) and `CallClass::RiskReducing` (exits,
   protective orders, cancels, kill-switch orders) are admitted or refused at once as before:
   they never wait, and an exit's reserved tokens are never spent by a read (rule 13).
3. **The rehearsal's sequence fits.** Connect (3) plus the lapse (6) leave one token; the
   preflight on the kept snapshot needs three, so its last two reads each wait one refill period
   (about 1 s). The cycle's later reads (the reconciler's snapshot, the broker ask at the check)
   wait at most a period each as well, rather than refusing.

## Rationale

The least-risk option the task named: no check is skipped (the tradability and the quote are
always read, and the account is re-read whenever the kept snapshot could be older than the bound
every other preflight read is held to); no stale read is reused; no order-path or exit budget is
borrowed; and a wait only delays a read, which adds no risk (rule 3), by at most the fixed bound.
The cycle's reconciler still reads the account afresh before any opening, so the one opening
the run may send is never judged on the kept snapshot alone.

## Alternatives considered

- **Wait only, no reuse.** Works, but spends six more tokens and about six seconds re-reading
  what was read a moment earlier, and pushes every later read of the run into a wait.
- **Reuse only, no wait.** The preflight on the kept snapshot still needs three tokens with one
  left, so it would refuse at the tradability; and the cycle's reconciler would refuse next.
- **A larger ordinary bucket.** Rejected: §6.5's conservative default stands until Robinhood
  publishes its limits (U-R9), and a larger bucket only loosens it.
- **Let openings wait too.** Rejected: an opening that waits after its cross-check meets the
  market later than the quote it was checked on; it stays refused at once, as before.
- **Unbounded wait.** Rejected: a read must end, answered or refused, within a known time
  (rule 3).
