## Decision

1. **The landing browser's controls either work or look like they cannot** (amends DEC-213's
   browser, whose menu bar and toolbar were `aria-hidden` scenery). The menu bar is a real menu
   bar (`role="menubar"`, `site/browser-chrome.tsx`): View (Reload, Change wallpaper…), Go (Home;
   Back and Forward greyed), Bookmarks (the page's sections), Directory (the guides), Window (the
   desktop's windows) and Help (the handbook, the questions, About Owlhead…). File, Edit and
   Options have nothing to do on a one-page site, so their titles are etched grey and announced
   as disabled. On the toolbar Home scrolls to the top and Reload reloads; Back, Forward, Images,
   Open, Print, Find and Stop are `disabled` and etched, as a period browser greyed what it could
   not do. The Location field is a read-only address that selects on focus. The menus answer the
   arrow keys and Escape, and the chrome stays out of the Tab order, so the page's keyboard order
   is unchanged. Only the status bar stays `aria-hidden`.
2. **The sign-in pages are a logon window on the landing page's desktop** (amends DEC-211 item 2's
   frame for `/login` and `/auth/passkey`). The site header goes: each public page draws its own
   window. Sign in and Add a passkey are one raised window (`site/logon.tsx`) on the same
   wallpaper, with a title bar carrying the brand owl and a close box back to `/`, the key icon
   beside what the page asks, and the landing page's faces, fields, sunken notices and bevelled
   buttons (the default button in the highlight, ringed in ink), each 44px. What the pages say,
   do and announce does not change, and no message says whether an account or an address exists.
   The landing page's three faces are imported once, by `site/faces.ts`, which only the landing
   page and the logon window load.
3. **Every app button is the Approve and Skip key** (amends DEC-452 item 5 and DEC-467 item 8).
   `KEY` (`src/components/kumo/key.ts`) is DEC-467's decision key at a 44px height: the card, an
   ink edge with a heavier bottom edge that presses down, Public Sans semibold, and Kumo's focus
   ring. `KEY_SM` is the same key with tighter padding for a row of small actions, still 44px, and
   `DECISION_KEY` stays Approve and Skip's 48px full-width key. The bevel and its Pixelify label
   leave the product's buttons; the primary ink pill, the outline pill and the lapis button go
   with them, so the back links, New agent, Close position, "Show the current version", the
   step-up dialog's Cancel and Use passkey, the passkeys section, the error and access pages, and
   the new-agent prototype all share one key. Pixelify stays on the empty board's title and the
   all-clear line.
4. **What keeps its own shape.** Stop, the Stop sheet's choices, Pause and the kill switch keep
   theirs (DEC-206, DEC-452; crimson stays the kill switch's alone), as do navigation (the dock,
   the tab bar, menus, header icons, list rows and choice tiles) and the chart's range control.
   These are not buttons that act on a page, and Stop must never look like any of them.

## Rationale

A browser that draws a Back button that does nothing invites a click and then fails it; greying
out what cannot act is the period's own answer and keeps the window honest. The sign-in pages sat
in the app's glass header between a retro landing page and the app, so the first step from the
landing page looked like a different site. In the app, six button styles had grown up (ink pill,
outline pill, lapis, bevel, quiet, decision key), and the founder named the decision key as the
one to keep; one key means a button looks the same wherever it acts, and the decision stays
distinct only by its width and height.

## Alternatives considered

- **Remove the menu bar and toolbar.** Rejected: they are the period browser the landing page
  draws, and the founder asked for them to work or be greyed out, not to go.
- **Make every toolbar button act** (Print prints, Find opens a search). Rejected: printing a
  scrolling desktop and a home-made find are new surface for little use; greying them is honest.
- **Keep the glass header on the sign-in pages and restyle only the buttons.** Rejected: the page
  would still not match the landing page it is reached from.
- **Keep the bevel on a few app buttons beside the key.** Rejected: two families of button is the
  inconsistency the founder asked to remove.
