# DEC-659: The writer refuses a membership record out of order before commit

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-10-09 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | Agent (lane L1 builder, E9-7) under DEC-176, since it only tightens; agreed by the merge coordinator on 2026-10-09 (the ruling on DEC-657 item 4 in [#987](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/987)) |
| **Story or claim** | E9-7, claim [#760](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/760); journal spec §8 (late events) and §9.12; identity spec §5.3, ID-7 |

## Decision

1. **The guard is `check_order(last, next)`** in `mandate-identity`, a pure function the
   workspace store's writer calls with the stream's last membership record (`last`, `None` when
   there is none) and the record it is about to commit (`next`). It returns
   `Err(RecordRefusal::OutOfOrder)` or `Ok(())`. The commit path lives in another lane's crate,
   so this crate provides the check and that crate calls it.
2. **It refuses** when `next.event_time` is before `last.event_time`, or when `next.seq` is not
   above `last.seq`. An equal `event_time` passes: DEC-657 item 4 refuses only "before", and two
   records committed in the same nanosecond are in order by `seq`. No `seq` is above `u64::MAX`.
3. **The first membership record passes** (`last` is `None`), whatever its `seq` and instant.
4. **It applies to all seven §9.12 record types** alike, as `next` and as `last`, the founding
   grant included.
5. **It runs before commit**, so a writer that calls it never commits a record the fold reads as
   out of order, and one late membership record cannot latch a healthy workspace's
   `workspace_users` at 1 (ID-7).
6. **DEC-657 item 4 stays the fold's rule** and the backstop: a record that reaches the stream out
   of order, from a writer that skipped the guard or from a store fault, still makes the fold
   unreadable from its `event_time`, which fails safe.

## Rationale

DEC-657 item 4 fails safe but not available: journal §8 appends late events at their arrival
`seq` with their earlier `event_time`, so one membership record written late, or a clock step
back, would latch the fold unreadable and pin `workspace_users` at 1 for good. As the coordinator
ruled in #987, a membership record's `event_time` decides cool-offs and expiries (rule 106 makes
it the record's own instant), so out-of-order membership time is a fault, refused at its source.
Refusing a commit adds no risk (rule 3) and only narrows what the writer accepts, so DEC-176 lets
an agent take it. Journal §9.12 states the same rule in a later journal-spec PR.

## Alternatives considered

- **Clamp `event_time` to the last record's.** Rejected: it rewrites the record's own instant
  against rule 106, and would move a cool-off or expiry the record decided.
- **Do nothing and rely on DEC-657 item 4.** Rejected: safe, but one late record latches a
  healthy workspace unreadable, with no way back short of a fold change.
- **Re-sort the fold by `event_time`.** Rejected: journal §8 never re-sorts on replay.
