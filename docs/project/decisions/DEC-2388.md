# DEC-2388: The cell's runtime's gate dry run over the journaled fold

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (agent: a reversible engineering decision under DEC-79 and DEC-176. It only tightens: every opening the daemon's dry run sees is still denied, now by the gate's own checks first, and no fact it cannot see is ever reported to the owner as passed) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-26 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | an agent, under DEC-79 (`claude-code`, lane D1) |
| **Story or claim** | E21-30; [DEC-2378](DEC-2378.md) items 9a and 11 (the S2b gate); `AGENTS.md` rules 1, 3 and 13 |

## Context

DEC-2378 item 11 lets the cell's runtime's advisory dry run defer four checks to the executor's
binding gate, and #2477 added `mandate_risk::evaluate_deferring` for it. The daemon still ran the
fixture's deny-all dry run. Building the real one needs the gate's mandate and configuration, which
`mandate-gate-inputs` reads, and that crate links the executor, which `xtask/layers.toml` forbids
the daemon. It also needs a value for every field of the gate's input, and the fold
(`mandate-gate-context`) journals only some of them.

## Decision

1. **`gate_mandate` moves to `mandate-ports`, unchanged** but for its error type
   (`PortError::Absent` for `InputError::Missing`). The paper shell calls it there; there is one
   copy.
2. **The rule set's gate half moves to `mandate-gate-context`** as `gate_rule_set`: the identity
   members (`gate`, `ruleset_version`, `story`) and `gate_config`, read exactly as before.
   `mandate-gate-inputs` (layer 7) and `mandate-ports` (layer 7) cannot depend on each other, so the
   one reading sits below both, beside the facts both gates read. `mandate-gate-inputs::rule_set`
   calls it and keeps its signature.
3. **The daemon's configuration is the registered rule set.** At start, the latest
   `ConfigSnapshotRegistered` of kind `rule_set` in the deployment's environment names the
   artifact; its canonical bytes must read under item 2. A missing snapshot, an unreadable artifact,
   or one that is not a gate rule set (the cell registers its backtest settings under that kind)
   is never skipped for an older one, nor is a newer registration whose row does not parse: the gate denies every opening `rule_set_unavailable`. A later
   registration takes effect at the next start.
4. **Only openings and increases consult it.** Any other purpose is allowed by the dry run, and
   the binding gate judges it (DEC-2378 item 5, `AGENTS.md` rule 13).
5. **The context, field by field.** From the fold: the risk clock, the account's snapshot, the
   instrument's sane mark, and what the agent holds. Everything else takes the value that refuses
   most, or is named below as unseen:
   - **The mandate** is item 1's over the stored document; the universe is its pinned instruments.
   - **The agent's equity** is its allocation, flat, with no rung active, as the plan reads it
     (DEC-2378 item 10). The agent's mode is `normal`: the runtime's core applies its own mode
     before it asks the dry run.
   - **The account**: `blocked` on a status other than `ACTIVE` or any blocking flag, as the
     executor's fold reads it; cash at multiplier 1, else margin; the 1× buying power is the lesser
     of cash and the non-marginable figure, as the executor computes it; the account's long market
     value is its equity less its cash, never below zero, which the account's 1× gross check sums.
     The regime is `legacy_pdt` with a prior close of zero, the reading that refuses most if
     check 8 is ever evaluated.
   - **Any order of the agent's still working** denies the opening `working_orders_unseen`: the
     fold has no order's cost, so the gross and concentration checks could not count it.
   - **The instrument**: inactive, untradable, no exchange, an IPO, no ETP classification, no
     prior close or volume, whole shares only. Every one of these is read only by a deferred check
     (eligibility, the floors), so each would refuse the moment its deferral were lifted without
     its fact.
   - **The quote** is the sane mark's bid on both sides: the collar's ceiling for a buy is then at
     or below the true one. Its passive floor is below the true one too, so a buy limit far under
     the ask may pass here and be refused by the binding gate.
   - **Held instruments** are each valued at their own sane, current mark; one without one holds
     the opening `facts_held`. The account's `positions` are the agent's, the only ones the fold
     knows.
   - **The mandate version** the fold has in force must be the one the gate was built on: after an
     applied change, every opening is `facts_held` until the process restarts on the new version.
   - **A proposal labelled as adding risk** that the gate types as anything but an opening or an
     increase is `gate_refused`, since an exit defers nothing.
   - **Unseen, read as none:** the agent's last exit fill (the re-entry cooldown), its orders today,
     §9.6's conduct counters, the related accounts' resting orders, the fee reservation, and the
     instrument's halt (check 3), read as not halted, with no current status feed. Each could only
     let the dry run pass where the binding gate refuses.
6. **Every opening is still denied in this slice.** After the gate's own checks, an allow with any
   deferred check is denied `deferred_to_binding`. DEC-2378 item 11's four checks are deferred
   until their facts are journaled, and item 8's ten unseen parts until theirs are folded. A held fact is `facts_held`, a refusal to evaluate is
   `gate_refused`, and a gate denial or hold carries its own reason code.
7. **No default ever reports a pass (binding; the coordinator's ruling, 2026-10-26).** Before the
   dry run may answer an opening anything but a denial (the `DecisionMade` v2 slice), every check
   that reads a fact the fold does not carry either folds that fact from the journal or is added
   to the deferred set (item 8 meets it by naming). That covers each unseen input of item 5: the last exit fill, the orders
   today, the conduct counters, the related accounts' resting orders, the fee reservation and the
   halt. None may read a default and report a pass, so the owner is never shown a check as passed
   that the dry run could not see (DEC-2378 item 11, condition 4; the lesson of #2471). A test
   journals, for each of them the journal can hold today, a value that should refuse, and shows
   the opening is never answered an allow with nothing deferred.

8. **The unseen inputs are named as deferred (the coordinator's follow-up, 2026-10-26).** Item 7
   is met by naming, not by folding. `mandate_risk::Deferred` gains ten parts the dry run defers
   beside DEC-2378 item 11's four. Each is a fact the daemon fills with a default:
   - `AgentMode` (check 1): the agent's mode and the risk engine's, and the account's
     `closing_only` restriction;
   - `AgentEquity` (check 2): the agent's marked equity, which the position cap scales by, and its
     instrument restrictions;
   - `ReentryCooldown` (check 2): the last exit fill;
   - `Halt` (check 3);
   - `OtherOrders` (check 4): every agent's working and unknown orders on the account;
   - `QuoteAsk` (check 5): the ask the collar is measured from, and a `stale_mark` restriction;
   - `ConductHistory` (check 6): the orders and fills today and the last opposite fill;
   - `RelatedAccounts` (check 6): the related accounts' resting orders;
   - `FeeReservation` (check 7);
   - `AccountExposure` (check 7): every agent's working opening orders and the agent's marked
     equity in its gross limit.

   **How they work:**
   - Unlike the four, they are never skipped. The gate evaluates each part on what its caller
     gives it, so a refusal still refuses with the binding gate's reason, and its check reads
     `Failed`.
   - A check one of them touches that passes is reported `Deferred`, never `Passed`. With all
     fourteen deferred, the daemon's dry run reports no check of an opening or an increase as
     passed; it evaluates nothing else (item 4).
   - A part leaves the daemon's set (`UNSEEN`) only when a later slice folds its fact from the
     journal, under its own decision.

   **Why naming, not folding:**
   - Folding them now would copy the executor's folds, its conduct counters, its order book and
     its risk state, into a second set that could disagree with them (DEC-2378 item 6's reason for
     one fold).
   - Naming them adds no risk: the binding gate evaluates each (condition 3).

## Rationale

The dry run can only narrow (`AGENTS.md` rule 1): the binding gate decides. Running the gate's own
checks first means a denial the owner would see is the gate's real reason, not the fixture's, while
item 6 keeps the cell from asking about anything until the journal can show what was checked.

## Alternatives considered

- **Raise `mandate-gate-inputs` above `mandate-ports`.** The shell, at layer 8, depends on it, so it
  cannot rise, and a layer change is the founder's (DEC-503 item 2).
- **Copy the rule-set reading into the daemon.** Two readings of one artifact could judge it two
  ways.
- **Allow when the gate allows, deferrals aside.** That would report the item 5 unseen inputs as
  passed.
