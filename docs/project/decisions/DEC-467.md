## Decision

1. **Home leads with the decisions** (amends DEC-207 item 3, and its "from `lg` up, desktop is
   unchanged" for Home). The brief's D1 shows recent decisions; they now take Home's main column as
   a timeline, newest first: the time, the agent's owl on a hairline, the action as a link to the
   decision, its verdict in words (Allowed on the well; Not allowed, Held and Waiting outlined in
   ink, never in gain, loss or crimson), the agent and the purpose, the gate's rule when the
   verdict is not Allowed, and what happened next. Six on desktop, with a one-line tally ("The latest
   6 decisions: 3 allowed, 3 not allowed") and All decisions; three on a phone, with See all
   decisions. Recent activity leaves Home; the Audit timeline keeps it.
2. **Needs you and the account share the rail at every width.** On desktop the 20rem rail holds
   Needs you (requests with their static times, then alerts, or the all-clear) and the account
   figure; the separate "Waiting for you" cards and the alerts summary are gone, so a request
   still appears once on Home. The rail comes first in the document, so a phone reads Needs you,
   Account equity, Decisions, Agents, and a screen reader reads the same order at every width.
3. **The account figure is compact.** Its chart is 200px on desktop (180px on a phone, as before),
   and opens on the past week, the range the fixture's history fills. Both hero lines, the
   account's and an agent's, are 3px so they hold on a compact chart; an agent's chart keeps its
   300px height and its one-day range.
4. **News moves to the agent page**, filtered to the symbols that agent holds or may trade, after
   Activity. Home has no news.
5. **One "Paper P&L, simulated." note per list of agents**, above the rows, instead of one per row.
   Each desktop agent row now ends its P&L line with its headroom, as the phone row does.
6. **One page grid.** `PAGE_GRID` (`web/src/components/screens/common.tsx`): one column, and from
   64rem a main column and a 20rem rail with a 3.5rem gap. Home, an agent and the approvals inbox
   use it; the inbox's rail says what sends the owner a request: each agent's asking rules and how
   long a request waits before it is skipped, linking to its mandate. Reading pages (a request, the
   record screens, the coming-soon and section index pages) keep their reading measure.
7. **One approvals count.** The header's Approvals link and its count go: the dock (DEC-208) and
   the tab bar (DEC-207 item 2) carry Approvals and the one count. Stop stays out of the header
   (DEC-452).
8. **Approve and Skip are one solid key** (amends DEC-452 item 5). On an approval request both are
   the same 48px full-width key: the card, an ink edge with a heavier bottom edge that presses
   down, Public Sans semibold, equal in every class. They lose the bevel and the Pixelify label,
   because the decision is the page's one act and reads best in the product's own face. The bevel
   stays on the request's "Show the current version", the back links, New agent and Close position.
9. **The landing page's record, questions and guestbook open as windows on the desktop**, as their
   icons always did, rather than scrolling to sections at the end of a long page. The hero gains
   "See the record" beside "Sign the guestbook"; the guide row, the contents list and both
   guestbook buttons open the windows. Without a script the guestbook form stays in the page. The
   ASCII wordmark is set upright (figlet Standard, not Slant), and the page uses three faces:
   Pixelify Sans, DotGothic16 and VT323. The wallpaper is unchanged.
10. **A mandate-writing prototype at `/design/new-agent`** (brief A0, A2 and A5) on fixture rules:
    three questions or a description draft a mandate by a fixed, deterministic reading, with no
    model call. What the owner states is quoted as theirs; every other value is proposed by the
    platform or a platform default and stays inactive until the owner confirms its section. The
    platform never fills in the instruments, any `auto` rule, a delegation, the environment or the
    connection. Constraints no field can check are listed as not enforced. Nothing is saved or
    sent, and the confirmation says so. New agent's coming-soon page links to it.

## Rationale

Screenshots of every screen showed Home leading with a large chart of a week of simulated money,
while the decisions, the one thing only this product shows, sat below the fold as generic activity.
Leading with what each agent decided and why, in the owner's words and the gate's, is functional
and is the product's character; the account figure keeps its place beside it without dominating.
Putting Needs you in the rail at every width removes a desktop-only duplicate. The header count
duplicated the dock's. Approve and Skip in the period face made the page's most serious act look
like the landing page's scenery. On the landing page the record, the questions and the guestbook
were the end of a long scroll on a desktop that already had icons for them; windows make the
desktop the navigation, and the slanted wordmark was hard to read at a glance.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the large account chart first and add the timeline below.** Rejected: the decisions would
  still sit below the fold on a laptop.
- **A one-month default range.** Rejected: the fixtures hold a week of history, so the month read
  as the week with an empty start.
- **News on Home, filtered to all agents.** Rejected: a headline matters next to the agent that
  holds the symbol, not in a general feed.
- **Keep the bevel on Approve and Skip.** Rejected for the reason in item 8; equal size and weight
  are kept.
- **A conventional marketing landing page.** Rejected by the founder: the retro desktop stays.
