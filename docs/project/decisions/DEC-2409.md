# DEC-2409: The approval's action is the page title, so the risk sits with the choice

## Decision

1. **The action is one line at the page's title size.** On the approval request, fixture and API, "Buy … at a limit of …" is `text-h1`, not `text-hero`. The order value, the purpose and the mandate version sit under it. `text-hero` stays in the type scale.
2. **The risk shares the first screen with Approve and Skip, from 64rem.** The record's gap is the column's, not the agent page's section gap. Skip remains the refusal. There is no Deny. Doing nothing still skips the action, and Approve and Skip stay the same key.

## Rationale

DEC-208 item 6 set the approval's action at the hero size so it would read first on a screen with no equity figure. On a 1440×900 screen that size, stacked over a second line, pushed the risk in dollars under the pinned choice. The owner was looking at the action and the buttons, and the evidence for the choice was a scroll away. The action stays the loudest line. It no longer costs the risk.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the hero size and only close the gaps.** The display line is most of the height above the risk. Closing the gaps still left the risk under the bar.
- **A Deny button.** The product's refusal is Skip, and silence is the same outcome. A third word would break the No-Nudge Rule.
