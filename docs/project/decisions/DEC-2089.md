## Decision

1. **One deployment.** Staging, the cell on DigitalOcean with the workspace at `api-staging.owlhead.ai`
   and the web app at `app.owlhead.ai`, is the platform's only deployment. Production's OpenTofu
   root stays in the repository and is not applied. Spend stays inside staging's caps (DEC-2015);
   no node, cluster or service is added for this decision.
2. **No Access in front of staging's API.** Staging's Cloudflare module runs with `access = false`:
   no Access application or policy guards `api-staging.owlhead.ai`. The API's own sign-in, the
   founder's Supabase session (DEC-2057), with its route rate limits (DEC-2117), is the one gate,
   so the founder signs in once, in the app. Production's root keeps Access as it was.
3. **Staging releases are signed by CI with no approval.** A merge to `main` yields binaries staging
   starts without the founder's approval of the `release-signing` environment. How (a staging
   key held by CI, or the keyless signature admission already checks) is the implementing
   change's, recorded there. Production keeps DEC-2024's approval-gated key.
4. **Staging deploys itself.** Each green post-merge release promotes staging (DEC-2016's
   `gitops promote staging`) and applies it, with no promotion PR and no dispatch by hand.
5. **Live money (Proposed).** The founder wants live trading on his own account. It is built after
   the product works end to end on paper, behind hard caps the founder sets (total capital, size
   per order, loss per day). The founder connects his live broker account himself through the
   app's OAuth; no agent reads, holds or uses a live credential or places an order (AGENTS.md
   rule 8). The change that enables it names the caps and is the founder's to confirm.

## Rationale

There are no customers, and the founder is past his deadline. Every gate above was built for a
platform holding other people's money. On a paper-only cell that only the founder uses, each one
cost a manual step on every change: a daily Access sign-in, an approval click per release, and a
promotion PR and an apply per merge.
