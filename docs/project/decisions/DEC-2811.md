# DEC-2811: Reason codes for trading spec §5.3 rules 2 and 7

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (agent, under DEC-176: a refusal to decide becomes a coded denial, which adds no allow and weakens nothing; naming reason codes is none of DEC-79's reserved kinds; the coordinator's ruling of 2026-10-26; the founder may rename the codes) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-26 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | `claude-code` (lane XF), under DEC-176 |
| **Story or claim** | E21-30 (the cell's trade loop); E6-6; supersedes [DEC-129](../04-decision-log.md#decisions) item 27's "Proposed (founder)"; trading spec v0.22 §5.3 |

## Context

Trading spec §5.3 rule 2 (quantity at least `min_order_size`, and a multiple of the increment) and
rule 7 (fractional and notional equity orders are TIF day, and §5.2 keeps fractional orders out of
an OCO or a bracket) bind openings at the risk gate's check 4. Neither had a registered reason code.
DEC-129 item 27 proposed `below_min_order_size` and `quantity_off_increment` to the founder, and
until then `mandate-risk` refused to decide an opening that broke either rule
(`GateError::Unimplemented`, "DEC-129 item 27"), since minting an unregistered code breaks ES-09.

A refusal to decide is safe (the executor's default holds the opening) but tells the owner nothing,
and the cell's first deployed openings meet it: a fractional opening in an instrument whose
registered increment is a whole share is refused, not denied (lane XF's G2b-2 finding, #2605).

## Decision

1. **Three codes are registered** in `docs/specs/reference-cases/trading-domain.yaml`'s
   `reason_codes`, under order constraints:
   - `below_min_order_size`: an opening or increase whose quantity is below the instrument's
     `min_order_size` (rule 2);
   - `quantity_off_increment`: an opening or increase whose quantity is not a multiple of the
     instrument's increment, a fractional quantity in an instrument that is not fractionable
     among them (rule 2);
   - `fractional_order_tif_or_bracket`: a fractional equity opening or increase that is not TIF
     day, or is a bracket's entry (rule 7, §5.2).
2. **The gate denies with the code** at check 4, in §5.3's list order: rule 2's two codes before
   rule 3's `would_cross_zero`, and rule 7's after rules 5 and 6 (`working_order_limit`) and before
   rule 8 (`add_blocked_by_protective_order`). Rule 2's minimum is judged before its increment.
3. **Exits are unchanged.** Neither rule binds a reduction: a sell of the whole position is always
   valid, and the broker judges an exit's size (`AGENTS.md` rules 3 and 13; DEC-150 item 4).
4. **The names are provisional.** The founder may rename any of them by a later decision; a rename
   changes the registry, the gate's enum and nothing else.

## Rationale

The change only tightens: every opening the gate refused to decide for these rules is now denied,
and no order it refused is allowed. A coded denial is journaled and shown to the owner, so the cause
is visible instead of a refusal. DEC-79 reserves live money, spending, legal and compliance text, and
weakening a safety rule; naming a reason code is none of these, so DEC-176 lets an agent accept it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep refusing until the founder names the codes.** Rejected: the refusal is opaque to the owner
  and blocks the cell's demo path on an explanation, not on a risk.
- **One code for both halves of rule 2.** Rejected: the owner's remedy differs (size up, or size
  in whole shares).
