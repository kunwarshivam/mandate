## Decision

1. **Stop moves out of the header** (amends DEC-206 item 1 and DEC-207 item 1). From 64rem it ends
   the dock, past a hairline after More; below 64rem it ends the tab bar, after More. It is still
   on every screen at every width from 320px, never disabled, quiet or loud from the same
   `stopAttention`, with a hit area of 44px or more, and both places open the one Stop sheet.
   Viewers and auditors still have no Stop. The phone header holds the mark and the paper badge.
   The More sheet and its backdrop sit under the tab bar, so Stop stays pressable while the sheet
   is open or sliding.
2. **The logo on screen is the brand owl.** The product's own pixel owl, awake, in one of the four
   feather colours an agent's owl may take, picked at random on each page load by a script in
   `<head>` that runs before the body paints, so the logo never changes colour after it appears and
   the server's markup names no colour. It replaces the traced mark in the app header, the sign-in
   frame and the landing page's title bar, beside the unchanged outlined wordmark. The traced mark
   (DEC-203) stays the source of the favicon, the app icons and the share image, which are static
   files and cannot vary per load.
3. **Minesweeper is removed** from the landing page's desktop.
4. **The pixel owls are redrawn** in the same style and grid (DEC-217): a dark outline, round eyes
   with a glint, shaded wings, a pale belly and a two-tone beak. Moods, motion and their rules are
   unchanged.
5. **A few product surfaces borrow the landing page's look** (amends DEC-213's rule that the period
   faces are imported only by the landing page). The bevel of the landing page's window chrome,
   in flat colour, with a Pixelify Sans label, is given to Approve and Skip and Retry on an approval
   request, the back links on the agent and approval pages, New agent, and Close position. Pixelify
   Sans also sets the empty board's title and the dashboard's all-clear line. DotGothic16 and VT323
   stay on the landing page alone. Stop, Pause and Kill never take the bevel, and figures, rows and
   reading text stay in Public Sans.

## Rationale

The founder found Stop in the top header out of place. The dock and the tab bar are the controls
the owner already reaches for, on every screen, so Stop stays one press away everywhere; the kill
switch remains always available (`AGENTS.md` rule 13). Using the agents' own owl as the logo, in
an agent's colour, ties the brand to what the product shows. Lending the landing page's bevel and
chrome face to a handful of decisive buttons, not to every button, gives the two surfaces a shared
family without making the product a period piece or harder to read.

## Alternatives considered

- **Stop as a floating button over the content.** Rejected: it would cover rows and charts, and a
  second floating layer beside the dock competes with it.
- **Stop in the More sheet.** Rejected: two presses, and it would hide the kill switch.
- **A colour picked on the server or stored per user.** Rejected: the server would render a colour
  the client then changes, or the browser would store more than the theme cookie.
- **Bevel every button, or set the product in the landing faces.** Rejected: the founder asked for
  some, not all, and figures must stay in one legible face.
