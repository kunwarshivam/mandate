## Context

DEC-1130 item 2 listed Alpaca news on allowlist version 1 as **unconfirmed**. Under DEC-433 item 20,
a source is allowlisted only once its terms are confirmed to allow keeping what a thesis cited.
Confirming it is a new allowlist version, and it is the founder's call (DEC-79: legal). Until now
the platform read no news, so the agent could not act on news (product-flow map step 5).

## Decision

The founder answered: "Yes, Alpaca news (Recommended)". The option he picked read: "Agent reads
Alpaca's news feed, journals each item, and may research on it. Opinions only, never orders."

1. **Allowlist version 2** (`config/sources/allowlist-v2.toml`) is version 1 with `alpaca.news` in
   effect (`counsel_confirmed = true`, citing this decision). It keeps the same endpoint
   (`https://data.alpaca.markets/v1beta1/news`), the same caps and minimum interval, and
   `licensed = true`. Version 1 is not edited. The flag is set on the founder's decision under
   DEC-79, which DEC-1130 item 2 made his call. Counsel has **not** separately confirmed Alpaca's
   terms on keeping what a thesis cites, so DEC-433 item 20's retention question stays open. The
   founder's yes decides that the platform reads Alpaca news, journals each item, and may research
   on it, and nothing more.
2. **How it must be read.** The reader is read-only, over `mandate-sources`' fetcher, which already
   exists; the wiring below follows in its own changes:
   - it uses a data key the caller leases from the vault (DEC-1130 item 10; `NewsAuth` is an
     opaque pair, and nothing yet checks that the key is a paper one, which rule 8 requires and
     the cell's wiring must hold), never logged and never in a journal or notification payload
     (rules 6 and 7);
   - it reaches only the allowlisted host (DP-9; the private `PermittedUrl`);
   - each item is stored by its raw bytes and must be journaled before any run uses it
     (`ObservationRecorded` at the read, or a trigger's record under DEC-2392), so no unrecorded
     item reaches a model. Today `mandate-sources` builds the typed record (`observation()`), and
     the runtime journals what a run reads; the news fetch's own journaling is wiring to come;
   - CI and every test use recorded items only. No agent ever uses a real credential.
3. **Opinions only.** News reaches only the research agent, whose output is theses and
   invalidations judged by mandate spec §8.5 and §8.6. A news item never sizes, places, or
   approves an order (rule 4), and the order path cannot reach its text (DEC-1130 item 11).
4. **Licensed text stays withheld.** Alpaca's text is licensed, so readers still withhold it
   (`LicensedText::Withheld`, DEC-1130 item 6) until the founder and counsel decide inference spec
   §13 question 6 (DEC-432 item 23: a prompt record that keeps no licensed text). Until then a
   model sees an item's typed fields: source, tickers, times, and its digest, not its headline or
   body. This decision does not decide that question. It is raised to the founder separately.

## Consequences

- **Wiring follows in separate changes** (the vault lease with a paper-key check, journaling at
  the fetch, and the rest):
  - the cell's data process fetches news for the agent's held and thesis instruments with the
    vaulted key;
  - the store moves onto the workspace data service (DEC-1130 item 8);
  - the research source reads allowlist version 2;
  - E19-12's trigger (DEC-2392) offers matching items to the runtime.
- **What adds no risk:** a read-only source, read only by a model whose output cannot add risk on
  its own (admission still needs corroboration and the autonomy rules). The risk it does carry is
  prompt injection (RAID R-05). That is bounded by HI-10, and while the text is withheld it is
  bounded further.
