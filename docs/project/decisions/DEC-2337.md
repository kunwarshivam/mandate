## Context

[DEC-2333](DEC-2333.md) sequences the account kill switch: final mode, then `KillSwitchActivated`,
cancel-all, confirm, and close-position, each step journaled before it is sent. The first four
steps have records:

- `AgentModeApplied`;
- `KillSwitchActivated`, from which the cancel-all goes out;
- the open-orders read.

The close-position has none. The switch's `closes` list names every instrument at once, but DEC-2333
item 2 sends each instrument's close only once that instrument is confirmed, one at a time. Without
a record per close, three things go wrong:

- **Replay cannot tell sent from waiting.** A restart either resends every close or sends none.
- **The interval cannot end on the close.** DEC-2333 item 3 ends an instrument's unprotected
  interval with `unprotected_end` when the close leaves nothing held. The fold must know a close is
  out:
  - to tell that end from a position that fell to zero some other way;
  - to stop the bound's re-placement (item 5).
- **Rule 5 of `AGENTS.md`**, journal before acting, has nothing to hold the close to.

## Invariants first

- Every close-position request is journaled before it is sent, and no request is sent twice.
- A crash or a lost answer never strands an instrument. The switch completes (`AGENTS.md` rule 13),
  but only through a fresh read, never by resending blind.
- No re-request goes beside a sell of ours that may still fill, or for a position already gone.
- After an instrument's close is journaled, nothing re-places protection there (DEC-2333 item 5).
  Its interval ends only with `unprotected_end`.
- A close is only ever for a position held long (v1 has no short sales, `AGENTS.md` rule 12).
- The record carries nothing sensitive beyond what the account stream already holds, and it never
  reaches a notification (rule 6).

## Decision

1. **A new closed record, `AccountCloseRequested`.** It sits on the account stream at
   `schema_version` 1 (journal spec v0.75, §9.5). Its members, in order:

   | Member | Type | Meaning |
   |---|---|---|
   | `instrument_id` | `text` | The instrument closed, as the executor names it: the same text the switch's `KillSwitchActivated` names in its `closes` or `deferred`, not a canonical asset id |
   | `qty` | `decimal` | The executor's held quantity when the request was journaled. It is informational: the close-position sells whatever the broker holds |
   | `kill_switch_event` | `ulid` | The account stream's `KillSwitchActivated` the close carries out |
   | `attempt` | `integer` | 1 for the first request of the instrument under the switch, one more for each re-request |
   | `risk_clock` | `risk_clock` | The latest tick the executor had seen (§2) |

   The schema is closed: every member is present and no other member is allowed (§9.2).
2. **Rules at `append`** (reason `schema`):
   - **Rule 258:** `qty` is positive, then `attempt` is at least 1.
   - **Rule 259:** `causation_id` equals `kill_switch_event`.
   - **Rule 260, a stream rule:** no two records of the stream share `kill_switch_event`,
     `instrument_id` and `attempt`. `append` reads the stream's earlier records, as for §9.19's
     rules 153 and 158, so a duplicate request is refused and its second send never happens.
3. **Journaled before every request; re-requested only on a fresh read.**
   - The executor writes the record in its own batch, then sends the broker's close-position for
     that instrument.
   - It writes the first request (`attempt` 1) only for an instrument DEC-2333 item 2 confirms: after
     the cancel-all's answer, and after a read taken at or after the switch shows no sell of ours
     working there.
   - Replay reads a journaled request as sent. Two cases can still leave the instrument held:
     - a crash between the record and the send;
     - a lost answer, so the close-position may or may not have reached the broker.
   - In either case the executor waits for a broker read taken after its last request: the
     restart's reconciliation, or the next read at the lookup window (DEC-2333 item 3). Only when
     that read shows the instrument still held, and no sell of ours working in it, does it journal
     `attempt` + 1 and send again.
   - A read showing the instrument flat ends it. A read showing a sell working holds it until that
     sell's cancel is confirmed, as for the first request.
   - The read condition is the guard. Nothing here relies on the broker refusing a duplicate close.
4. **The interval waits on it.** Under the account switch, an instrument's unprotected interval ends
   with `unprotected_end` only once a request for it is journaled and nothing is held. Once one is
   journaled, the bound's re-placement (DEC-2333 item 3) places nothing there.
5. **What `append` does not check yet.** That `kill_switch_event` names an earlier account-scope
   `KillSwitchActivated` whose `closes` or `deferred` lists the instrument waits until that record
   is closed on the account stream. The backlog holds the row. Until then, the executor's fold
   guarantees it.
6. **Slices.**
   1. This record ships as a spec change: the spec, the reference implementation, and the vectors.
   2. A DEC-77 pair in `mandate-journal` then registers it with rules 258 to 260.
   3. A1b's executor pair then writes and folds it.

## Consequences

- The account stream's catalogue gains one record. `KillSwitchActivated`'s schema is unchanged.
- A1b's executor tests pin items 3 and 4:
  - a record before each close-position;
  - no resend after a restart until a fresh read shows the instrument still held and clear, and then
    `attempt` 2;
  - the interval ending on the close.

## Alternatives considered

- **(b) Read a sent close from the broker's answer alone.** Rejected: an answer lost to a crash
  leaves nothing to read, and rule 5 asks for the record before the send.
- **(c) Reuse `OrderRequestRecorded` and `OrderSubmitted`.** Rejected: a close-position has no
  client order id of ours and no limit, and those records' rules (45, 39 and 40) assume an order the
  executor built.
- **Never re-request after a journaled close.** Rejected in review: a crash between the record and
  the send would strand the instrument, and the switch would never complete (rule 13).
- **Re-request on restart without a read, trusting the broker to refuse a duplicate.** Rejected: the
  guard must be the platform's own read, not a broker behaviour.
- **Add progress members to `KillSwitchActivated`.** Rejected: that record is written once, and its
  schema on the account stream is not yet closed.
