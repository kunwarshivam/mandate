## Context

[DEC-2333](DEC-2333.md) sequences the account kill switch as final mode → `KillSwitchActivated` →
cancel-all → confirm → close-position, each step journaled before it is sent. The first four steps
have records: `AgentModeApplied`, `KillSwitchActivated`, the switch itself (its cancel-all goes
out from it), and the open-orders read. The close-position has none. Its intent is the switch's
`closes` list, but that list names every instrument at once, and DEC-2333 item 2 sends each
instrument's close only once that instrument is confirmed, one at a time. Without a record per
close:

- **A restart resends a close.** The fold cannot tell an instrument whose close went out from one
  still waiting, so replay either sends every close again or none.
- **The interval cannot end on the close.** DEC-2333 item 3 ends an instrument's unprotected
  interval with `unprotected_end` when the close leaves nothing held. The fold needs to know a
  close is out to tell that end from a position that fell to zero some other way, and to stop the
  bound's re-placement (item 5) once the close is out.
- **Rule 5 of `AGENTS.md`**, journal before acting, has nothing to hold the close to.

## Invariants first

- Every close-position is journaled before it is sent, once per instrument per switch, and never
  resent by replay.
- After an instrument's close is journaled, nothing re-places protection there (DEC-2333 item 5),
  and its interval ends only with `unprotected_end`.
- A close is only ever for a position held long (v1 has no short sales, `AGENTS.md` rule 12).
- The record carries nothing sensitive beyond what the account stream already holds; it never
  reaches a notification (rule 6).

## Decision

1. **A new closed record, `AccountCloseRequested`**, on the account stream at `schema_version` 1
   (journal spec v0.75, §9.5). Its members, in order:

   | Member | Type | Meaning |
   |---|---|---|
   | `instrument_id` | `text` | The instrument closed |
   | `qty` | `decimal` | The quantity held long when the close was requested; the close sells all of it |
   | `kill_switch_event` | `ulid` | The account stream's `KillSwitchActivated` whose `closes` or `deferred` named the instrument |
   | `risk_clock` | `risk_clock` | The latest tick the executor had seen (§2) |

   The schema is closed: every member present, no other member (§9.2).
2. **Rule 258** (reason `schema`): `qty` is positive (`payload.qty`). A zero or negative quantity
   closes nothing, and a close never opens a short.
3. **Journaled before the close-position, one per instrument per switch.** The executor writes it
   in its own batch, then sends the broker's close-position for that instrument. It writes one
   only for an instrument DEC-2333 item 2 confirms: after the cancel-all's answer and a read taken
   at or after the switch shows no sell of ours working there. Replay reads it as sent: an
   instrument with an `AccountCloseRequested` for the switch is never closed again under it. A
   lost answer to the close is the broker's to settle through reconciliation, as for any `Unknown`
   order (trading spec §5.7); the executor never resends it under the same switch.
4. **The interval waits on it.** An instrument's unprotected interval under the account switch
   ends with `unprotected_end` only once its `AccountCloseRequested` is journaled and nothing is
   held. Once it is journaled, the bound's re-placement (DEC-2333 item 3) places nothing there.
5. **One per instrument per switch is the writer's.** As with rule 251, the executor's fold
   guarantees it and `append` does not check it: the stream is the executor's alone.
6. **Slices.** This record ships as a spec change (spec, reference implementation, vectors), then a
   DEC-77 pair in `mandate-journal` that registers it, then A1b's executor pair, which writes and
   folds it.

## Consequences

- The account stream's catalogue gains one record; `KillSwitchActivated`'s schema is unchanged.
- A1b's executor tests pin items 3 and 4: one record per confirmed instrument, before the
  close-position; none resent after a restart; the interval ending on it.

## Alternatives considered

- **(b) Read a sent close from the broker's answer alone.** Rejected: an answer lost to a crash
  leaves nothing to read, and rule 5 asks for the record before the send.
- **(c) Reuse `OrderRequestRecorded` and `OrderSubmitted`.** Rejected: a close-position has no
  client order id of ours and no limit, and those records' rules (45, 39, 40) assume an order the
  executor built.
- **Add `KillSwitchActivated`-level progress members.** Rejected: that record is written once, and
  its schema on the account stream is not yet closed.
