## Decision

The [data plane spec](../../specs/data-plane.md) v0.1 is a draft. Items 1 to 14 are readings an
agent may take because each only tightens a rule and adds no risk (DEC-176). Items 15 to 20 stay
Proposed; work continues on the conservative option each names.

1. **Precedence.** The data plane spec adds no risk-gate rule of its own. Where it overlaps the
   trading domain, mandate, or journal spec, those win. It says only what the data plane must
   deliver so their rules hold.
2. **Epic.** The data plane's stories continue epic E2 (E2-5 onward). E18 on `main` is the
   enterprise harness, and sibling specs drafted in parallel may take new epic numbers; continuing
   E2 collides with none of them.
3. **Licensing, until items 15 and 16 are decided.** The shared plane carries no exchange market
   data and no licensed vendor text. It carries only public data (SEC filings and XBRL facts), their
   factual classification, calendars, and reference lists whose terms allow sharing. Each workspace
   reads market data and Alpaca news through its own connection (HLD §12 risk 3, RAID R-12).
4. **Two time axes.** Every stored record has an event time and a knowledge time. Knowledge time
   is the receive time for live data, and the source's own publication time for history (a bar's
   end, a filing's acceptance time, a news item's published or update time). A correction's
   knowledge time is its arrival. Point-in-time queries filter on knowledge time.
5. **Quote age and clock skew.** A quote's age is measured on the risk clock from the earlier of
   its vendor time and its receive time. A quote stamped more than `future_skew_ms` (default
   1,000 ms) after its receive time is not sane. While `ClockToleranceExceeded` stands (journal spec
   §5.4), no quote is fresh for an opening. Exits are unaffected.
6. **Unknown status blocks openings.** Trading spec §4.4 makes a dropped status feed a presumed
   halt for market orders. On a profile whose feed carries a status channel, a channel that is down
   or not yet resynced since a start or reconnect is also treated as a halt for openings in every
   instrument on that connection.
7. **Backfill never marks.** Data fetched to fill a gap feeds bars and the store only; a risk mark
   comes only from a live quote received after resync.
8. **Order and corrections.** For one instrument and feed, a quote whose vendor time is at or
   before the last accepted quote's never marks. Stored records are never edited; a correction is a
   new record naming the original.
9. **Fan-out is broadcast.** The shared plane publishes whole signed datasets that workspaces pull
   and filter locally. It never receives a workspace's subscriptions, universe, or queries, because
   the working universe is strategy (HLD "Where data lives").
10. **Tagging is deterministic.** Entity tags come from structured vendor fields (ticker lists,
    filer CIK through a versioned table), resolved to `asset_id` as of knowledge time. No model runs
    in the shared plane, and a tag never makes an instrument eligible.
11. **Fetch only from allowlisted endpoints.** The endpoint list is compiled in or signed
    configuration, checked before any byte is sent, as ADR-0001 ES-23 does for broker hosts.
12. **Capture at use.** What a decision, gate check, or thesis used is captured into the journal
    by digest when it is used, so the bulk store's retention and a vendor's terms never break a
    record.
13. **Late corporate actions.** A split or dividend first seen after its 19:45 ET preparation
    time blocks openings in the instrument until it is prepared, applied, and posted. Exits and
    protection continue; the owner is alerted. Whether trading spec §8.5 should state this itself is
    an open question there.
14. **Syndicated copies are one source.** Copies of one story, grouped by vendor id, original
    source attribution, or normalized-body digest, count as one source for the corroboration check
    (mandate spec §8.5 check 15).
15. **Shared exchange market data** (**Proposed**; licensing and spend). Recommendation: do not
    license redistribution in v1; revisit with a licensing review and a vendor quote when managed
    cells host enough workspaces to pay for it. Until decided: item 3.
16. **News and fundamentals vendors** (**Proposed**; spend and licensing). Recommendation: SEC
    EDGAR filings and XBRL facts first, in the shared plane; Alpaca news per workspace; no paid
    vendor before the DEC-99 evaluation passes. Until decided: exactly that, since it spends nothing.
17. **One high print sets H or E₀ for good** (**Proposed**; it changes the mandate spec's
    safety-critical risk-state fold, and in one direction it delays a rise in H). Today the
    high-water mark is the maximum E ever seen and E₀ is the last regular-session mark, each from a
    single sane quote, so one wrong high print can make later real prices confirm a drawdown or a
    daily loss. Recommendation: a quote that would raise H, or become E₀, counts only when a second
    sane quote at least min(`breach_confirm_s`, 10 s) later confirms it, as the hard trigger already
    requires (mandate spec §5.6). Until decided: the mandate spec is unchanged and the spec's DP-6
    tests for H and E₀ stay pending.
18. **Paper on `iex` without a status channel** (**Proposed**; it decides whether paper trading
    works at all if the IEX feed carries no status or LULD channel). Recommendation: allow paper
    openings on a fresh IEX quote plus an asset read no older than 60 s showing `tradable`; live stays
    SIP-only (DEC-35). Until decided: once E2-6 ships, item 6 and DP-5 hold and such a profile opens
    nothing; until then the code reads a presumed halt from the latest quote only, as the backlog
    records.
19. **Live theses that cite a revoked source** (**Proposed**; it changes the mandate spec's thesis
    lifetime). Recommendation: revoking a source invalidates every live thesis that cites it, so its
    instrument is removed (exits only, protection stays). Until decided: the operator per-thesis halt
    (E17-6), and mandate spec §8.5 check 14 refuses new admissions.
20. **Keeping licensed news text six years** (**Proposed**; legal). Trading spec §13 keeps what
    informed a decision for six years; a vendor license may forbid that. Recommendation: keep only
    what a thesis cited, as journal artifacts, and have counsel confirm against each vendor's terms.
    Until decided: cited items are kept as artifacts; no other licensed text is kept beyond its
    license.

## Rationale

The data plane had no spec, and its rules were scattered: freshness in the trading and mandate
specs, observations in the journal spec, sources in DEC-101, licensing in HLD §12. Items 1 to 14
close gaps where code would otherwise pick a reading silently. Each picks the reading that blocks
an opening or adds a record, never one that allows more. Item 9 follows from HLD's rule that
strategy never leaves the workspace deployment: a per-workspace subscription to a shared feed would
send the universe out. Item 14 follows from DEC-101's purpose: one planted story must not admit an
instrument, and syndication would otherwise let it corroborate itself.

Items 15, 16, and 20 involve spend, licensing, or legal terms, which DEC-79 reserves. Item 17 changes
a safety-critical fold in a way that is not purely tightening. Items 18 and 19 change what the
platform does in a way an owner would notice, so the founder decides.

## Alternatives considered

- **A new epic for the data plane.** Rejected: E2 is the market data epic, and new epic numbers
  were being taken in parallel.
- **Age from the vendor time alone.** Rejected: a vendor clock ahead of ours would make old quotes
  look fresh.
- **Per-workspace subscriptions to the shared plane.** Cheaper bandwidth, but it sends each
  workspace's universe to us; rejected (item 9).
- **Model-based entity tagging.** Better recall, but it puts a model on untrusted text in the shared
  plane, where one injected item reaches every workspace; rejected (item 10).
- **Backfilled quotes as marks after a reconnect.** Faster recovery, but a backfilled quote is
  history, not a price one can trade at now; rejected (item 7).
