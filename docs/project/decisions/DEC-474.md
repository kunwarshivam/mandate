## Decision

This amends [DEC-473](DEC-473.md) items 1, 4 and 6, and, for setting up a new agent only, the
brief's PX-2 (per-section confirmation). The model still only points (DEC-473 items 2, 3 and 5 are
unchanged), and A6 still governs every later version.

1. **The conversation reads like any chat.** The platform answers in plain sentences: what it
   understood ("Got it: $3,000.00 to use and the goal “Grow it steadily”."), what it refused and
   why, then the next question. There are no "Noted from your words" cards, no section cards, and
   no "The model said" label: the model's reply is the first sentence of the platform's reply. It
   still never carries a button (PX-18), and a reply that reads as advice is still withheld
   (DEC-52). The only buttons in the conversation are deterministic: the model choices, Try again
   and "Continue without the model" when the model fails, and Create agent.
2. **A value written in words is read, and said back once.** "About three grand" becomes $3,000.00
   at once, and the reply says so: "I read “about three grand” as $3,000.00." There is no Yes or No
   card. The owner corrects it by saying so. Nothing is active until the owner creates the agent
   from a summary that shows the value.
3. **One confirmation, of the whole agent.** Once nothing is missing, the conversation shows the
   agent once, as one card: the agent in plain sentences, the losses in dollars, what could buy
   without asking, every setting in compact form, the Not enforced notes, and the version. Every
   value the platform drafted is marked *proposed* or *default*; nothing is collapsed (PX-1 (b)
   stands). One button, Create agent, confirms it with a passkey (G3). There is no per-section
   confirmation. Saying a change shows a new card; the earlier one can no longer be created.
4. **The card is the A5 record.** It replaces the separate confirmation screen. The journal keeps
   every line it shows with `MandateConfirmed`, as before (`summaryLines` in
   `web/src/components/new-agent/summary.tsx`), and the confirmation binds the version hash it
   shows. What happens after the passkey appears under the card, apart from the record.
5. **The account's rules are checked again when the card is shown and created.** If another agent
   took the money (V-002) or a symbol (V-006) after the owner said it, the card says why it cannot
   be created, and offers no button until the owner changes it. The runtime repeats both at
   application (DEC-472 item 3).

## Rationale

- **The founder found the section confirmations, the cards, and the labels confusing.** Owners
  expect a chat to answer in sentences. Five Confirm section buttons and a second record screen
  made a short task feel like a form.
- **Showing every value once, before one confirmation, keeps what the per-section rule protected.**
  PX-2 (b) was there so a platform proposal is never accepted unseen. The card shows every value
  uncollapsed, with each drafted one marked, and nothing is active until the passkey.
- **Saying a reading back keeps the interpretability DEC-473 wanted**, without stopping the
  conversation for a Yes or No. Figures are still read exactly as written.

## Risks, flagged to the founder with this change

- **Compliance question 23**, recorded for PX-2: "a proposal accepted in bulk is the platform
  choosing limits in all but name." One Create agent accepts every proposed limit at once. What
  remains: each is marked *proposed* on the card the owner confirms, and the record keeps that
  marking. Counsel's answer to question 23 may bring back a per-proposal step.
- **The owner cannot yet change a proposed limit by saying so** (DEC-473's Not covered). To
  change one, the owner edits the mandate after creating it (A6).

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep per-section confirmation, restyled as chat replies.** Rejected: the founder named the
  confirmations themselves as the friction.
- **Keep the Yes or No card for amounts in words.** Rejected: it stops the chat for a value the
  summary shows anyway, and the reply already says how it read the words.
- **No summary: create once nothing is missing.** Rejected: the owner would confirm values never
  shown together, and PX-1 requires the confirmation to show them.
