## Context

Trading-domain §6.2 accrues each equity regulatory fee at full precision and charges the day's
total once, `round(total, 2, ceiling)`. Robinhood rounds each fee of a trade to the cent
separately (SEC up; TAF and CAT to the nearest cent). For a sell, its total can exceed the
platform's one rounded figure by under two cents a fill: 30 shares sold at 85 cost 0.07 at
Robinhood and 0.06 on the platform (DEC-958 item 4). The platform then books less than the
account is charged, which reconciliation reports as a fee mismatch and which understates cost.

## Decision

1. **A fee configuration says how its fees round.** `fee_rounding` in the equities fee
   configuration takes `daily_total` (§6.2 as before; the value when absent) or
   `per_fee_per_fill`.
2. **`per_fee_per_fill`.** Each regulatory fee of a fill (SEC Section 31, FINRA TAF, CAT) is
   computed at full precision and accrued as `round(fee, 2, ceiling)`, separately, before the
   fill's fees are summed. A zero fee stays zero. The 20:00 ET `FeesCharged` still charges
   `round(daily total, 2, ceiling)`, which then equals the accrual.
3. **Up, not nearest, for every fee.** Robinhood rounds TAF and CAT to the nearest cent. Rounding
   them up is at least the broker's figure for every input, so the platform never books less;
   it can book up to a cent more per TAF or CAT. Nearest would match more often but would need
   to reproduce the broker's tie rule exactly to never understate, and a mismatch there books
   less, which rule 3 refuses.
4. **The TAF cap applies before rounding.** The rounded TAF is `round(min(uncapped, room), 2,
   ceiling)`. Every cap in force (9.79) is whole cents, so the rounded TAF never exceeds it. A
   sub-cent cap would round above itself, which overstates and is accepted. Under `per_order`
   the room counts the full-precision TAF already charged on the order, as today, so each fill's
   TAF is at least what it is under `daily_total`.
5. **Who opts in.** Robinhood's live schedule (`robinhood_live_2026_10_16`, DEC-958) takes
   `per_fee_per_fill` in the code change that implements this. Alpaca's paper schedule and the
   paper-only overestimate stay `daily_total`, so no paper outcome changes. Robinhood's posted
   rule is already quoted in DEC-958's dated file (SEC "rounded up to the nearest penny", CAT
   "rounded to the nearest penny"), which DEC-958 item 5 keeps unedited; the code change sets the
   rounding on the executor's constant and pins it to that quoted rule.
6. **Spec wording.** §6.2's rates are "transcribed from the broker's fee schedule", Alpaca's for
   paper and Robinhood's for the live Robinhood account, closing DEC-958's wording note; the
   commission row reads "Zero on Alpaca and Robinhood".
7. **Reference case RC-26** pins the rule with test rates: a 500-share buy and three sells over
   two orders, with a whole-cent TAF cap that binds on one fill. Its values are recomputed from
   the rule with Python `decimal` (per fill: CAT 0.005 → 0.01; SEC 0.1503 → 0.16, TAF 0.02, CAT
   0.001 → 0.01, twice; SEC 0.4509 → 0.46, TAF min(0.06, 0.03) = 0.03, CAT 0.003 → 0.01; accrued
   0.01, 0.20, 0.39, 0.89; charged 0.89). Its variant `daily_total_unchanged` runs the same fills
   under the default and charges 0.84 (accrued 0.005, 0.1763, 0.3476, 0.8315), the old rule.
8. **Order.** The spec change is its own PR (ES-22); the fold, the harness's reading of
   `fee_rounding`, and RC-26's status row follow on `claude/fee-rounding-code`, tests first and
   failing (DEC-921).

## Not closed here

- **Fee reservations** (§2.1, §9.5) still price an order as one fill at its limit. Under
  `per_fee_per_fill` an order that fills in N parts can cost up to a cent per fee per extra fill
  more than it reserved. Once a part fills, its rounded fee is in the accrual and counted against
  buying power at once (`charges_due`), and the broker's own buying power check still applies;
  `daily_total` had the same reservation figure, so nothing here loosens. A follow-up may reserve
  per expected fill if reconciliation ever shows it.

## Rationale

Recorded cost must never be below what the broker charges (rule 3); a per-fee ceiling makes the
platform's figure for each fee at least the broker's, whatever the broker's sub-cent rule, while
leaving every Alpaca and paper outcome as it was.

## Alternatives considered

- **Round TAF and CAT to the nearest cent, as Robinhood does:** matches more often, but any
  difference in the tie rule or in the order of rounding books less than the broker.
- **Round the fill's summed fees up once:** still below Robinhood when two fees each round up
  (0.06 + 0.01 against ceil(0.05847) = 0.06).
- **Switch every schedule to per-fee:** changes Alpaca and paper outcomes and RC-01 and RC-13,
  for a broker that charges the daily total.
- **Add a margin to the daily charge:** an arbitrary figure, and still not tied to the broker's
  rule.
