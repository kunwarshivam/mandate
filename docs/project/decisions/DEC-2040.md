## Context

Rule 13 lets an owner exit sell equities outside the regular session once the owner confirms the
displayed bid. Trading spec §5.5 adds that the exit price ladder then prices from the confirmed
bid and never below the floor the owner confirmed. The core does neither yet.
- **An owner kill switch.** Its record, `KillSwitchActivated`, carries the floor, bid, and bid size,
  and `kill::floor_of` reads the floor. But DEC-260 (13) sends an owner exit in pre-market or
  after-hours as a regular-session limit. So a confirmed owner flatten waits for the session, and
  E7-4's pending test `an_owner_exit_outside_the_session_prices_from_the_confirmed_bid` (a
  behaviour-only row) records the gap.

- **An owner's exit of one instrument.** It reaches the core as a handoff. That handoff carries no
  confirmation, so the core treats every handed owner exit as unconfirmed, and it waits for the
  regular session ([DEC-2037](DEC-2037.md) item 6). DEC-2033 item 4 also prices an owner's exit at
  the market as a limit at the floor, because the core took only limits then. So the
  `IntentReceived` it writes differs from the `IntentProposed` it copies, which journal spec §9.1
  says it copies exactly.

## Decision

1. **The handoff carries the confirmation.** `IntentHandoff` gains `confirmation`: the bid,
   bid size, and floor the owner confirmed on the `OwnerExitRequested` the intent follows from,
   the instant it expires (item 7), and that record's event id (`HandedConfirmation`). It is handed
   only when that record's `step_up_status` is `valid`: mandate spec §6.1 grants the owner-exit
   privilege of selling outside the regular session only with valid step-up. It rides on the handoff rather than the
   order body, because it describes how the exit was asked for, not the order. The core refuses,
   as non-canonical and before anything is journaled:
   - a confirmation on any purpose but `owner_exit` (field `confirmation`);
   - a bid size that is not positive (the bid and floor are `Price`s, positive by type), or an
     expiry that is not (field `expires_at`);
   - a limit below the floor (field `limit_price`).
2. **The executor journals it beside the intent.** A confirmed handoff journals
   `OwnerConfirmationReceived` immediately after its `IntentReceived`, in the same batch. It is a
   new account-stream record, journal spec v0.67 §9.5, with members `intent_id`,
   `instrument_id`, `qty`, `bid`, `bid_size`, `floor`, `expires_at`, `copied_from`, and
   `risk_clock`. The confirmation stays out of
   `IntentReceived` for the reason the protective prices do (DEC-446 item 5): the copy of the
   proposal stays exact. Two rules bind the new record:
   - **Rule 247:** its quantity, three prices, and expiry are positive.
   - **Rule 248, a batch rule:** the record immediately before it is the `IntentReceived` of the
     same intent, instrument, and quantity, with purpose `owner_exit` and a limit that is null or
     not below the floor.

   The fold reads the confirmation back, so a restart, a re-gate, a later rung, or a resubmission
   after a confirmed absence sees the same floor. Its vectors are the `owner_confirmations`
   section. `mandate-journal` registers the record and enforces both rules in its own DEC-77 pair,
   before the core writes the record.
3. **The core sells a confirmed owner exit outside the regular session.** This applies to an
   owner's exit of one instrument with an `OwnerConfirmationReceived`, and to an owner kill
   switch's flatten whose `KillSwitchActivated` carries a confirmed bid.
   - The gate sees the confirmed bid as `owner_confirmed_bid`, so it does not defer the exit
     outside the regular session (`owner_confirmation_required`).
   - In pre-market and after-hours the exit goes as an extended-hours limit. This amends DEC-260
     (13), which sends every owner exit there as a regular-session limit. An unconfirmed owner exit
     is still sent that way.
   - There, the ladder prices from the confirmed bid rather than from a quote the owner did not see
     (§5.5), on the tick (DEC-260 (6)), and never below the floor. The floor is the higher of the
     intent's own floor and its switch's floor, where both exist. This completes E7-4's pending
     test above, so its behaviour-only row goes with the change that makes it pass.
   - Any remainder rests at the floor, then waits for the session, with the existing alert
     (§5.5).
   - In the regular session the floor binds too (item 6): a confirmed owner exit never goes at
     the market, and goes as a limit priced by the ladder from the market, never below the floor.

   An owner exit with no confirmation still waits for the regular session, unchanged.
4. **The executor process hands the proposal as proposed.** An owner's exit at the market whose
   `OwnerExitRequested` (on its causation chain, for the same instrument) confirmed the bid with
   valid step-up is handed with no limit, plus the confirmation, so `IntentReceived` copies the
   proposal exactly. One confirmed without valid step-up keeps DEC-2033 item 4's limit at the
   floor and carries no confirmation: the floor the owner chose still binds, and nothing lets it
   sell outside the session (mandate spec §6.1). One with no confirmed bid is handed with no limit
   and no confirmation.
5. **DEC-2037 item 6's gap closes.** Item 6's check stands: the gate paces an owner exit only by
   the participation caps. What changes is that a confirmed owner exit is no longer held to the
   session.

6. **Does a confirmed owner floor bind in the regular session? (Proposed for the founder.)**
   §5.5 says "the ladder never prices below the floor". Read narrowly, the floor binds only the
   exit price ladder, and in the regular session on a fresh quote the exit could go at the market
   (§5.1). Today the executor process hands such an exit as a limit at the floor in every session
   (DEC-2033 item 4), so the floor binds there too. The trade-off:
   - **Binding (the default until the founder rules):** the owner never sells below a price they
     confirmed, but in a falling market the position can stay unsold, resting at the floor.
   - **Ladder only:** the position closes at the market in the session, but can fill below the
     floor the owner confirmed.

   Until the founder decides, the floor binds in every session: a confirmed owner exit is never
   sent at the market.

7. **A confirmation is bound to one intent and expires five minutes after the step-up.** Neither
   is in the spec: `OwnerExitRequested` names no quantity, and no spec gives a confirmed bid a
   validity window. Both readings only tighten (DEC-176).
   - **Scope.** The confirmation covers only the one intent that follows from its
     `OwnerExitRequested`, that intent's instrument and quantity (rule 248). A rung or a
     resubmission sells only that intent's remainder; a new intent, or a larger one, needs a new
     confirmation.
   - **Expiry.** `expires_at` is the step-up's `authenticated_at` plus 300 s, the freshness window
     mandate spec §6.1 gives step-up. It is never later than the commit plus 300 s. The core judges
     it against its journaled event time (the batch's risk clock), never a wall-clock read, so
     replay decides it the same way; at exactly `expires_at` the confirmation has expired.
   - **After it expires**, nothing new is priced from the confirmed bid or sent extended-hours:
     the gate sees no confirmed bid, and a new rung or resubmission takes the unconfirmed path, a
     regular-session limit queued to the session (DEC-260 (13)). The floor still binds (item 6).
     Orders already working are never cancelled for expiry (`AGENTS.md` rule 13).
   - **The trade-off.** An off-hours ladder that has not sold within five minutes of the step-up
     falls back to waiting for the regular session. That is conservative, since a bid confirmed
     longer ago says little about the book; the founder can widen the window later.

## Rationale

Rule 13 and trading spec §5.5 let a confirmed owner exit sell outside the regular session.
DEC-260 (13) predates the confirmation reaching the executor. This decision gives a confirmed owner
exit, whether a switch's flatten or an exit of one instrument, one route and one floor, and
changes nothing for an unconfirmed exit. A separate record keeps `IntentReceived` the exact copy of the proposal and
gives replay the confirmation without reading the agent stream.

## Alternatives considered

- **`IntentReceived` version 3 with `floor`, `bid`, and `bid_size`.** Rejected: §9.5 keeps the
  intent record the exact copy of the proposal, and this would break that copy.
- **The executor process re-reads the agent stream on each restart.** Rejected: the core folds only
  its own stream, and a re-gate between restarts would see no confirmation.
- **Let the floor bind only the ladder, so a confirmed exit goes at the market in the regular
  session.** Not taken until the founder rules (item 6): it moves the code from today's stricter
  behaviour to a looser reading of §5.5.
