## Context

[DEC-2804](DEC-2804.md) item 2 opens each agent's account risk fold flat at its first deployment.
`mandate_spec`'s `RiskState::open` takes an `Opening` with an `avg_cost`, and uses it as the
position's starting mark. A flat opening has no cost, so R4b passed 1.

So an own fill that comes before the fold has read a counted sane mark of its instrument values
the position at 1. In the executor daemon's test, buying 10 at 150 that way gave
E = 10000 − 1500 + 10 × 1 = 8510. That is a false 14.9% loss: it latched the daily loss, both
drawdown rungs and the floor, and the ladder set the agent `paused`. `paused` holds exits, which
`AGENTS.md` rule 13 forbids for a state that is not real.

In production this ordering is rare but possible:
- the executor marks a deployed instrument at most every 15 seconds (DEC-2380 item 3);
- it marks only once the deployment names the instrument, so after the opening instant;
- for an equity, it marks only from a sane regular-session quote.

Mandate spec §5.2 says what a mark does, but not what a flat fold's mark is before its first one.
The reference cases have no flat opening.

## Invariants first

- E is never computed from a price nobody quoted. A position the fold holds is valued only at a
  counted sane mark (§5.2: sane, and in the regular session for equities).
- While E is unknown, no limit trips or lifts, no rung or mode changes, and no record is
  journaled. Openings are held. Exits, protective orders, risk exits and the kill switch are never
  held (rule 13).
- Nothing the journal holds is skipped. Every input the fold reads while E is unknown is stepped,
  in journal order, once E is known.
- The fold remains a function of the journal: a replay from any head reaches the same state, and
  owes the same records.

## Decision

1. **Unpriced.** A fold opened flat is *unpriced* until it reads a counted sane mark of its bound
   instrument, at or after the opening instant: a `MarkUpdated` at version 2, `sane`, and for an
   equity in the regular session (crypto counts at any time). Once priced, it stays priced. The
   flat opening's `avg_cost` is never read as a price.
2. **An own fill while unpriced is booked, and E is unknown.** The fold does not refuse the fill
   or lose it. It holds the fill, and every later row that steps the risk state (ticks, marks that
   do not price it, fills and fees), in journal order, without stepping any of them. Its other rows
   are read as before: whose an order is, a compensation's pairing, and the records owed.
   - The view is unreadable with the cause `unpriced`, which is the one cause the fold leaves
     again (DEC-2304 item 2 is unchanged for every other cause).
   - Its mode is that of any unreadable view (DEC-2304 item 3): at least `exits_only`, so openings
     are held and nothing else is.
   - Nothing pauses, trips or lifts, and the fold returns no records while unpriced.
3. **The first counted sane mark prices it.** When the fold reads its first counted sane mark of
   the bound instrument, the risk state steps in this order:
   - that mark's bid, at the instant of the first held input, with the mark's own session;
   - then every held input, in journal order;
   - then the mark itself.

   E is then computed as it always is, from that mark. Each record those steps return is owed
   after the mark's row, with the held input that caused it as its cause, as on any replay
   (DEC-2399).
4. **A held input the fold cannot read** makes the view unreadable for good when it is stepped, with
   that input's cause, as if it had been read in its place. While unpriced, a mark naming another
   instrument is refused at once, since that does not depend on a price (DEC-2308 item 4).
5. **Tests.**
   - The daemon's process test: a mark first, then an own fill booked by reconciliation. The view
     stays readable, and the gate reads the agent's risk.
   - The fill before any mark: the view is held with `unpriced`, the gate reads none, nothing is
     journaled, and the first counted sane mark makes it readable with E = A − cost + Q × bid.
   - A mark that is not sane, or an equity's mark outside the regular session, does not price.
   - A property test over random inputs with fills before any mark: no limit trips or lifts and no
     record is returned while unpriced. Once priced, the fold's state and its records are those of
     the oracle, a fold that read the pricing mark first at the same instant.
   - Restart and replay reach the same view.
6. **Mandate spec §5.2** says how a flat fold is priced (v0.15).

## Consequences

- R4b (#2675) carries the code, in `mandate_executor::risk_state`. The executor daemon's book
  publishes no risk for an unpriced agent, so its openings hold.
- Not changed: DEC-2304 item 2 for every other cause, and an opening with a position (not used
  today), which is priced at its cost.

## Alternatives considered

- **Refuse the fill (`refused_to_decide`).** It is safe, but unreadable for good, so one fast fill
  would hold the agent's openings until an operator recovers it.
- **Value an unpriced fill at its fill price.** That changes what E means. It is not taken; the
  founder would decide it.
- **Open with the instrument's last mark before the opening instant.** Most deployments have no
  such mark, because marks start after the deployment, so it would not close the gap.
