## Context

Journal spec v0.20 §9.8 (DEC-800) gives a connection's stream rules 66 to 68, and §11 the checks
`connection_lifecycle_mismatch` and `connection_cause_mismatch`. `reference/journal/connections.py`
generates and judges their vectors. E7-17's fold (`crates/mandate-journal/src/connection_fold.rs`,
DEC-687) follows the text where the reference was looser or folded differently, and its hand cases
in `crates/mandate-journal/tests/connection_fold.rs` pin three places; E7-17's tests-correction PR
left the reference's side owed.

## Decision

1. **Rule 66: a version-1 first establishment is never re-established.** The text says so ("it
   has no `account_ref`, so it is never re-established"). The reference compared the two absent
   `account_ref`s as equal and admitted a version 1 after a version 1; it now refuses any
   establishment whose first establishment is version 1.
2. **Each rule holds on its own stream.** §9.8 says so ("Each holds on its own stream"). The
   reference now keeps one fold per control stream and one per account stream. Another workspace's
   revocation no longer lets an id be established again here, and one account stream's
   `reauthorize` check no longer admits another stream's rotation; two connections' account streams
   interleaved, which the text admits, are no longer refused as one stream with two connections.
3. **§11: `reconnect` after an establishment of either version, on the same control stream.** A
   version-2 establishment of an id established before rests on a `reconnect` check whether the
   earlier one was version 1 or 2, and a rotation rests on an establishment on its own control
   stream, with the account stream taken from that control stream's workspace. §11's sentence now
   says "on the same control stream, after an earlier one of either version", and that a rotation's
   connection is established on its own control stream.
4. **Vectors.** The `connections` section gains four `sequences` and four `chains`, with the
   stream mutants `stream.66.version_1` and `stream.per_stream` and the chain mutants
   `chain.version_1` and `chain.per_stream`. Every earlier case keeps its answer, and the Rust fold
   agrees with every new one. The vectors stay version 3.

## Rationale

The spec's text already decides each case and the E7-17 fold already follows it, so the reference
is the one place out of line. Six of the eight new cases are refusals the reference used to admit.
The two it used to refuse and now admits (`two_account_streams_interleave`,
`reconnected_after_version_1`) are admitted by §9.8's and §11's text as written; refusing them was
the reference's defect, not a rule, and admitting them adds no risk: each binding and each
establishment still rests on a passing check of its own stream and connection.

## Alternatives considered

- **Leave the reference as it was and keep the Rust hand cases as the only pin.** The vectors are
  the oracle both implementations are judged against, so two implementations disagreeing on cases
  the vectors do not hold is the drift E7-17's PR recorded as owed.
- **Change the text to the reference's readings.** That would loosen rule 66 (a version 1
  re-established) and fold streams together against §9.8's own sentence; not an agent's to take.
