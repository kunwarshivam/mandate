## Context

The executor holds openings under a restriction `reconciliation:<subject>`, by an `AgentModeApplied`
that is not `normal`:

- an instrument's position mismatch;
- a cash or fee difference;
- external activity;
- a run, batch or broker input that could not be recorded whole (`incomplete`, DEC-2805,
  DEC-2821).

Trading spec §11 resumes a paused agent only on the owner's acknowledgment with step-up, and §7.1
holds external activity until the owner acknowledges it.

No journaled record can carry that acknowledgment:

- §9.28's closed `OwnerAcknowledged` names only a limit, or, since v0.102, an account restriction;
- the executor's old reader of a `subject` member read a shape `append` refuses, and DEC-2828
  removes it.

So every such hold stands until the stream is replaced. On a broker input the executor cannot apply
(DEC-2821), that strands every agent of the account. The demo of 2026-10-27 must not depend on that.

## Decision

1. **`subject` gains `reconciliation`** in version 2 of `OwnerAcknowledged` and
   `OwnerAcknowledgmentRequested`.
   - `event` names the `AgentModeApplied` that applied the hold.
   - `agent` is any agent of the account, as for a restriction.
2. **The executor judges the copy by §9.28's four checks**, in order, with the same refusals:
   1. **The hold exists.** The named record is an `AgentModeApplied` on the account stream, before
      the copy, that the executor originated, whose `to` is `exits_only` or `paused`, and whose
      `restriction` begins `reconciliation:`. Otherwise `trigger_unknown`, so a copied mode, a Stop,
      or a removal is never lifted this way.
   2. **The hold stands.** No later `AgentModeApplied` of the same `agent` and `restriction` comes
      between the hold and the copy. The executor's own lift counts. Otherwise
      `trigger_superseded`, so a hold applied again needs its own acknowledgment.
   3. **Step-up**, as for any acknowledgment.
   4. **Independence**, with the overlay read across every agent deployed on the account, as for a
      restriction (DEC-2824 item 1).
3. **An accepted acknowledgment lifts that hold and no other.**
   - The executor journals `AgentModeApplied` for the hold's `agent` to `normal` under the hold's
     `restriction`, `originated` `true`, caused by the copy, as the record right after the copy.
   - Each agent goes back to the strictest restriction it still holds.
   - A refused acknowledgment's next record is its refusal, as DEC-2828 item 2 says.
   - Each hold is its own `(agent, restriction)`, so one acknowledgment lifts one.
   - Lifting `reconciliation:<instrument>` also ends that instrument's recorded mismatch, so the
     next run compares it afresh.
   - An acknowledgment resumes; it does not clear the cause. A cause found again is held again,
     and needs a new acknowledgment.
4. **No new stream rule.** `append` does not judge the executor's mode records. The lift names its
   acknowledgment by `causation_id`, and the copy names the hold, so the account stream alone says
   why the hold ended.
5. **Slices, all before 2026-10-27 13:00 UTC:**
   - this spec with its vectors;
   - `mandate-journal` registers the version-2 acknowledgment records;
   - the executor's lift, in the copy (DEC-2828);
   - executord hands the control stream's acknowledgments;
   - the CLI writes one for a hold.

## Rationale

- The hold's own `AgentModeApplied` is the record the owner was alerted about. Naming it binds the
  acknowledgment to that one hold, and a hold applied again after it needs a new acknowledgment.
- Reusing §9.28's checks keeps one step-up, independence and reuse path for every owner lift.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the old reader for reconciliation holds.** Rejected. Its record shape cannot be appended,
  so the reader is reachable only from tests.
- **Name the hold by its restriction string alone.** Rejected. A hold applied, lifted and applied
  again would be lifted by the first acknowledgment.
