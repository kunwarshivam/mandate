# DEC-472: New agent deploys into the fixture workspace, and the journey runs end to end on fixtures

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (agent, reversible engineering, DEC-79; DEC-176 for item 5, which only refuses more) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-05 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | A Cursor agent, for the founder's request "creating an agent is missing right now. so can we do complete e2e journey using fixture data?" |
| **Story or claim** | W4 web redesign ([#297](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/297)); PR [#617](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/617) |

## Decision

This amends [DEC-467](DEC-467.md) item 10: the prototype leaves `/design/new-agent` and becomes
`/agents/new`, and what it confirms is now deployed into the fixture workspace instead of being
discarded.

1. **`/agents/new` is the flow, and `/design/new-agent` is removed.** Its steps are the brief's A0
   (three questions, or a description), A2 (review, section by section), and A5 (the confirmation,
   a record screen). The reading stays the fixed, deterministic one of DEC-467 item 10, with no
   model call. The coming-soon page for New agent is gone, and `agents-new` is marked built.
2. **The model is a fifth section the owner chooses and confirms.** The picker lists methodology
   only (brief A3, FR-8.4, DEC-52): the fixture's two models in alphabetical order, with no ranking,
   nothing recommended, and nothing preselected. Each of the chosen model's parameters starts empty
   and must be filled in. The model and its settings are envelope fields (`AGENTS.md` rule 11), so
   changing either one unconfirms the section. The sections are money, limits, model, autonomy, and
   universe, and Continue to confirm stays disabled until all five are confirmed.
3. **V-002 and V-006 are checked when a section is confirmed and again when the deployment is
   applied** (mandate spec §4.1). The allocation must not exceed the account equity less the
   allocations of the agents that are not stopped. A pinned symbol must not be claimed by an agent
   that is not stopped. Confirming the money or universe section shows the refusal in place, and
   the section stays unconfirmed. The runtime repeats both checks against its current state, in the
   same step that applies the deployment, so two deployments started together cannot both pass.
   One that fails at application is shown as "Not deployed. Nothing was confirmed, and version 1
   does not exist.", with the reason, and the owner may change the answers and confirm again.
4. **A5 shows the record in full and confirms it with a passkey.** Everything is expanded, and
   nothing is hidden behind a disclosure: the version (`sha256:` over the canonical JSON of the
   mandate), the environment (paper), the contract terms, the answers in the owner's words, the
   unasked dollars, the loss figures, and every field with its provenance. The record sent with the
   deployment lists exactly the lines on the screen. A canceled or failed passkey check sends
   nothing and says so. Progress after confirming sits apart, under "After you confirmed", so the
   record itself never changes once shown (brief §4.1, P8).
5. **Only the owner confirms a new mandate.** The new capability `agents.deploy` belongs to the
   owner. An operator and an approver see the record but not the button, with the line "Only the
   workspace owner confirms a new mandate." Rule 11 says envelope fields are confirmed by the user
   who owns the account; letting an operator deploy capital would be the looser reading, which is
   the founder's to choose.
6. **The fixture agent's first check, approval, fill, and protection follow the rules the real path
   uses.** Each step is a pure function in `web/src/lib/fixture-journey.ts`, applied only once the
   runtime "records" it:
   - **First check.** The chosen model scores the first instrument, in symbol order, that one share
     of which fits the smaller of the order and position limits. The score is a fixed fixture
     number for each symbol and model. Below the entry threshold, the check writes why to the
     timeline and asks nothing.
   - **Sizing.** At or above the threshold, it sizes one limit buy at the fixture price: whole
     shares within the smaller of the two limits. A new mandate asks before every buy, so the
     gate's allow becomes a request with the mandate's own deadline.
   - **Approval.** An approved request is submitted as a resting limit order.
   - **Fill.** It then fills in full at the fixture price, never above its limit.
   - **Protection.** A protective stop is placed at the mandate's stop distance from the fill.
   - **Kill switch.** The agent's kill switch, unchanged, cancels its orders, sells its positions,
     and stops it, which frees its allocation and instruments again.

   All amounts use the decimal module, never floating point. Every ID is a deterministic fixture
   ULID, so the same journey writes the same record.
7. **The journey is tested end to end.** `e2e/agent-journey.spec.ts` drives it in one page load,
   with clicks only, at 1440 × 900 in both colour schemes: three answers, a V-006 refusal, the model
   and its setting, five sections, the A5 record and its passkey, the new agent on Agents and Home,
   the approval, the fill and its stop on Positions, Orders, Activity, and Decisions, and the kill
   switch. Unit tests check the V-002 boundary to the cent, a stopped agent's freed allocation,
   sizing against an independent cents calculation, idempotent submit and fill, and the runtime's
   rejection at application.

## Rationale

The founder asked for the whole journey on fixtures. Until now an owner could watch, approve, and
stop agents in the web app, but never create one, so the product's first act was missing. Moving the
prototype to the real route rather than building a second flow keeps one paved path. Repeating
V-002 and V-006 at application, as the mandate spec requires of the real platform, also closed a
race found while building this: two deployments in the same tick both passed the checks against a
stale workspace. Owner-only deployment is the conservative reading of rule 11, and is easy to
widen later if the founder chooses.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the prototype at `/design/new-agent` and link it from `/agents/new`.** Rejected: two routes
  for one task, and the one the owner reaches first would deploy nothing.
- **Preselect a model, or show a sample score beside each.** Rejected: that ranks or recommends a
  strategy (FR-8.4, DEC-52).
- **Check V-002 and V-006 only when a section is confirmed.** Rejected: the workspace can change
  between confirming the section and confirming the record, and the spec requires the check again
  at application.
- **Let an operator deploy.** Deferred to the founder. The conservative option, owner only, is in
  force.

## Not covered

- V-006 in the fixture compares symbols. The spec compares instrument groups (trading spec §7.1),
  which the fixture does not model. The platform's own check is `mandate-spec`'s validator, not this
  fixture.
- The A4 warnings (W-codes, each with its own acknowledgment) are not shown yet.
- A5 is a step of `/agents/new`, not a record route of its own, so the status strip's record-screen
  rules do not reach it yet.
