| **Story or claim** | E7-26, first-live-trade row G1a's precondition ([DEC-854](DEC-854.md) consequence, [brief](../tasks/first-live-trade.md) row G1a); [DEC-531](DEC-531.md) items 5 and 6; [DEC-630](DEC-630.md) item 9; [DEC-838](DEC-838.md) item 3; LT-14 |

DEC-854's consequence says an Alpaca crypto mandate is refused until `mandate-alpaca`'s profile
declares trading spec §5.2's crypto `limit` cells, before the G1a runner's deployment calls
`mandate_builder::deployable` or the cycle calls `propose_on`. §5.2's crypto row names its order
types (`market, limit, stop-limit; simple orders only`) and its times in force (`gtc, ioc`) for the
row as a whole, but not what each `limit` cell declares; the equities rows split their cells by
quantity form and crypto's does not. These are the readings the cells' pins take.

## Decision

1. **Times in force: exactly `gtc` and `ioc`, in both cells.** §5.2's crypto row names `gtc, ioc`
   and nothing else, so neither cell lists `day` or any other time in force. The row's
   parenthetical "(Mandate uses stop-limit as GTC only)" narrows the `stop_limit` cells alone
   (DEC-838 item 3), and §5.4's crypto adds are limit IOC orders on the same row, so the `limit`
   cells take the full set: an opening that carries either of §5.2's crypto times in force finds
   a form (DEC-854 items 2 and 6).
2. **Quantity forms: whole and fractional, neither more nor fewer.** The row's own precedent is
   DEC-838 item 3's reading, whose `stop_limit` cells the crypto row already declares in both
   forms. No notional cell is added: §5.2's equities rows name notional and crypto's row does
   not, the tighter set is taken (DEC-176), and the builder never opens notional anyway
   (DEC-854 item 1). A cell a row does not declare offers nothing (DEC-630 item 9).
3. **No protection form in a `limit` cell.** §5.2's crypto row is "simple orders only", and
   protection on crypto is the one resting stop-limit (DEC-36): a crypto limit order is never a
   bracket's entry or an OCO's parent, in either quantity form.
4. **Scope: the `limit` cells alone, beside B2a's.** The precondition that DEC-854 names is the
   `limit` cells, because §5.1 opens crypto with a limit order; `market` and `stop` cells wait
   for a story that reads them (DEC-531 item 6), and the row keeps exactly `stop_limit` (B2a) and
   `limit`. §5.2's "maximum 200,000 USD notional per order" line is a broker-side cap the mandate
   caps first (`max_order_usd`) and the broker enforces at submission; it is not a capability
   cell, and no reading of this slice adds it to the profile.
5. **Where the cells are pinned: at the declaration seam, in `mandate-alpaca`'s tests.** B3's
   reader (`opening_form`, `propose_on`, `deployable`) is implemented and pinned over generated
   and hand-built profiles in `crates/mandate-builder/tests/opening.rs`, and a connector's tests
   do not reach `mandate-builder` (layer 7 takes no builder dependency; a tests-only change adds
   none). What this slice pins is the missing half alone: Alpaca's declaration — each cell's
   existence, its exact times in force, its quantity forms and its protection forms — which is
   the entire contract the builder intersects. The wiring of `deployable` and `propose_on` to
   Alpaca's profile is G1a's implementation's, and #1297's precondition note holds it to these
   cells.

## Rationale

Each item declares less than a broker might accept, so the intersection is never wider than
§5.2's crypto row; none loosens a safety rule or changes an Alpaca outcome (LT-14 — the existing
B2a `stop_limit` pins and B1's equities pins are untouched, and adding cells can only end a
refusal that today's absence causes, never begin one). The readings follow the same route DEC-838
took for the stop-limit cells: §5.2's row is the recorded contract, and where it is silent the
tighter set is taken.

## Alternatives considered

- **`gtc` only, as DEC-838 narrowed the `stop_limit` cells.** Rejected: §5.2's "as GTC only"
  parenthetical names the stop-limit alone, and §5.4's crypto adds are limit IOC orders the same
  row must carry.
- **A notional cell, since Alpaca's crypto API accepts dollar amounts.** Rejected: §5.2's crypto
  row names no notional form, and refusing more is DEC-176's route; the builder never opens
  notional (DEC-854 item 1).
- **`market` cells beside the `limit` cells, since §5.2's row lists `market`.** Rejected: DEC-531
  item 6 declares only what a story reads, and the precondition names the `limit` cells.
- **Pinning the intersection through `mandate-builder` in the connector's tests.** Rejected: the
  connector may not take a builder dependency for a tests-only change, and B3's semantics are
  already pinned in `mandate-builder`'s own tests; item 5 records the seam.
