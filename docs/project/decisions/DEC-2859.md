| **Story or claim** | The chat's `order.buy` (agent harness spec §15.13, workspace API §4.6.2); mandate spec §6.2 step 5d; journal spec §9.1 rule 5. Amends [DEC-2159](DEC-2159.md), [DEC-2858](DEC-2858.md) item 10, and [DEC-2293](DEC-2293.md) item 5 and its chat `order.buy` |

## Context

DEC-2159 made an owner's own request `auto`: the owner's Send is the confirmation. DEC-2858 item 10
wrote that into mandate spec §6.2 as step 5d, which turns every `ask` of a request
`requested_by` `owner` into `auto`, the ask of the review ceiling (step 5b, DEC-188), of the
admission ceiling (step 5) and of the policy overlay (step 5c) included. DEC-2293 item 5 then made
the owner's explicit instruction in the chat an owner request, sent by the chat's `order.buy` as
soon as the model called it.

Two things follow that the founder did not want. In the chat, the model's reading of the owner's
words became an order with no look from the owner at what would be placed. And a review date that
has passed, a first order in an instrument the research agent admitted, or an order above the
mandate's two-approver threshold no longer reached a reviewer when the owner asked for it: in a team
workspace, one member's chat message bought above the threshold that exists so a second person
looks.

## Decision

1. **The agent's own ideas are unchanged.** An order the order builder proposes, or one the agent
   puts to the owner as an `order.propose` card, follows the mandate's autonomy rules (`auto`, `ask`,
   `deny`) as set.
2. **The owner's chat buy is confirmed by a tap, not by the message** (the founder). The chat's
   `order.buy`, after its checks (the owner's own words name the symbol and the quantity, the
   instrument is pinned, the environment is paper, the agent is not stopped, the session is still
   admitted), commits nothing. It posts a `confirm_order` card to the owner showing what would be
   placed, and only the owner's tap on that card submits the owner request. The request it submits
   gets no "approve your own idea" ask: step 5d still turns an `ask` from the autonomy rules or the
   policy overlay into `auto` (item 5).
3. **Risk reduction never waits on a card** (`AGENTS.md` rules 2 and 13). The chat's `order.sell`
   (an owner exit), `agent.pause`, `agent.stop` and `agent.kill` stay immediate.
4. **Review ceilings and approver thresholds still apply to the owner's own buys** (the founder).
   The review ceiling (`autonomy.review_by`, DEC-188, step 5b), the admission ceiling (step 5) and
   the approver threshold (`autonomy.approval.two_approver_above_usd`, §6.4's `approvers.required`)
   are reviews by someone other than the owner's Send. An owner request one of them holds stays
   `ask` and goes to the approval flow (§6.4), so in a team a different approver must approve. The
   risk gate runs on every order, as before.
5. **Mandate spec §6.2 step 5d becomes:** if the order was requested by the owner and the result so
   far is `ask`, it becomes `auto`, labelled `owner_request`, unless a reviewer holds the request.
   A reviewer holds it when, for this order:
   - the admission ceiling applies: `new_instrument` is true and `autonomy.admission` is `ask`;
   - the review ceiling applies: `autonomy.review_by` is set and the risk day is after it, or there
     is no risk clock to judge by (`AGENTS.md` rule 3); or
   - the ask needs two approvers: `two_approver_above_usd` is set and `order_usd` exceeds it.

   A held request stays `ask` with its own `decided_by` label, as any other ask keeps it (steps 5
   and 5b name themselves only when they changed the decision). Step 5d still applies last, after
   the policy overlay, and only to an `ask`: a `deny` stays a `deny`, and an `auto` stays `auto`.
   The agent's reading (DEC-176): "an ask that comes from a ceiling" is read as "an ask on an order a
   ceiling applies to", so a rule's `ask` that the review or admission ceiling would also have
   raised is held too; and the threshold holds an ask whatever raised it, the policy overlay's
   included, because the threshold judges the order's size, not the source of the ask. Both only
   tighten.
6. **Journal spec §9.1 rule 5 drops "an owner's own opening is never `ask`".** An owner's own
   `DecisionMade` may be `ask`; `owner_request` stays the label only of an owner's own `auto`
   opening or increase. A new invariant, MI-35, states item 5: an owner request a reviewer holds is
   never `auto`, and one no reviewer holds is never `ask`.
7. **The card, until a preview exists.** No route sizes an order without submitting it: the order
   builder runs in the agent runtime, on the journaled facts, after the request arrives. Until a
   preview route exists, the card shows the instrument, the quantity the owner asked for, the last
   trusted daily close as the reference price, the estimated cost (quantity × close), and says that
   the builder may clip the quantity to the mandate's caps and sets the limit price when it places
   the order. The decision the request comes to, with any clip and its reason, reaches the owner as
   today.
8. **The tap is its own route.** `POST /agents/{id}/messages/{message_id}/confirm` (workspace API
   §4.6.2) reads the card the platform wrote from the journal, re-runs the owner-request route's
   checks (§3.11 item 13) under the member's session and a fresh authorization of the *Make an owner
   request* row, and commits `OwnerRequestSubmitted` with `causation_id` the card's message and an
   event id derived from the card, so a second tap replays the first and submits nothing more. Only
   the member whose thread holds the card can confirm it; it needs no step-up, as the owner-request
   route needs none on paper (DEC-2158). Reusing `POST /agents/{id}/requests` was considered: it
   cannot name the card as the cause or bind the tap to the card's own content without a new body
   member.

## Rationale

The founder's proposal keeps the one-tap simplicity of DEC-2159 for the owner, but moves the tap to
the moment the owner can see what will be placed, so a model that misread the message cannot place
an order the owner did not look at. The ceilings and the threshold exist precisely so that someone
other than the person asking looks: a passed review date says nobody has re-confirmed the mandate,
an admission is a new instrument, and the threshold says an order this size needs two people. An
owner's own request is the owner's approval of their own idea, not a second person's review.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep step 5d as it was and add only the card.** Refused by the founder (item 4): it leaves a
  team's two-approver threshold and a lapsed review date unenforced for the owner's own buys.
- **Ask every owner request again.** Refused: DEC-2159's ruling stands for the ask the autonomy
  rules or the overlay raise; the card is the confirmation.
- **Read "comes from" by the `decided_by` label.** Not taken: the label names a ceiling only when
  the ceiling changed the decision, so a rule's `ask` past a review date would have been made `auto`
  although the review ceiling applies to it.
