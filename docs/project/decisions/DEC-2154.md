## Context

[DEC-2126](DEC-2126.md) item 6 ends DEC-2125's interim `cli_confirm` for a connect, member by
member, once the member holds a passkey "past its cool-off", judged as the step-up verifier counts
one. Since then, [DEC-2146](DEC-2146.md) lets a test cell waive a first passkey's 24-hour cool-off,
and DEC-2150 does the same on staging until 2026-11-05. The waiver is the API server's start-time
setting, which the verifier applies when it judges a step-up. The founder wants a customer's
experience on staging: passkey is the path, and `cli_confirm` is only a fallback. So the interim
should end as soon as the passkey can step up, with no second 24-hour wait. The approval's grant is
still served under `cli_confirm` alone ([DEC-2129](DEC-2129.md)), and its passkey half is deferred
to its own change (DEC-2129 item 6).

## Decision

1. **"Past its cool-off" is the verifier's rule, the waiver included.** DEC-2126 item 6 reads a
   member's passkey as past its cool-off exactly when the step-up verifier would judge it so at that
   instant: a `CredentialEnrolled` version 2 passkey whose cool-off has ended, has none, or is waived
   by the server's waiver as DEC-2146 and DEC-2150 set it. The check calls the verifier's one rule
   rather than restating it. A version 1 enrolment still holds nothing (DEC-2122 item 3).
   - DEC-2146 item 3 lets a waived *step-up* count only on a route that journals its assertion, so
     each waiver is on the record. This reading does not widen that: here the waiver never makes a
     step-up count. It only refuses `cli_confirm` sooner, and the member's passkey step-up that then
     follows journals its own waiver as DEC-2146 says.
2. **The approval's interim ends the same way.** Once the grant is served under passkey step-up
   (DEC-2129 item 6), DEC-2104's `cli_confirm` for a paper `approve` ends for each member who holds
   a passkey past its cool-off as item 1 reads it, exactly as DEC-2126 item 6 ends it for a connect.
   For a member who holds no such passkey, `cli_confirm` for `approve` stays as DEC-2104 says.

## Rationale

- Both items only tighten. A member who could use `cli_confirm` either keeps it, or holds a passkey
  that the verifier accepts at that instant, so no member is left with no way to step up.
- One rule, read in one place, keeps the interim's end and the verifier from drifting apart, for
  example under a waiver's expiry (DEC-2150).

## Alternatives considered

- **Read only the enrolment's own `cool_off_ends`, ignoring the waiver.** Rejected: on a waived
  staging deployment the passkey path works at once, but `cli_confirm` would linger 24 hours as a
  second path, which is the opposite of what the founder asked for.
- **End `cli_confirm` for every member at once.** Rejected: a member who has not enrolled a passkey
  would be left with no way to step up (DEC-2126 item 6).
