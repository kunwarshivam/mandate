# DEC-1140: A refused thesis record wins a receive-time tie, and a resolved thesis's exit stays DEC-281's

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (agent: both readings only tighten or restate, DEC-176) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-17 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | The lane I4 builder (Claude Code), under DEC-79 and DEC-176 |
| **Story or claim** | E4-4, E17-8; DEC-1000 item 5, DEC-1002 item 3, DEC-281 item 3; claim [#1463](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/1463) |

## Context

The coordinator's delta review of #1472 left two minors. DEC-1000 item 5 breaks a tie between thesis
records received at one instant by thesis id, which says nothing about which verdict wins. And
DEC-1002 item 3 says a thesis resolves once every series holds a close at or after its horizon end,
which a reader could take to mean that close is the exit.

## Decision

1. **A refused record wins a tie.** Among the thesis records for the instrument received at or
   before `t`, the latest is the one with the greatest `(received_at, refused, thesis_id)`: at one
   receive time a refused record outranks an admitted one, and the thesis id breaks what remains.
   An admission therefore never survives a refusal journaled at the same instant, whatever the ids.
   This only keeps the instrument out of the universe in more cases (DEC-176).
2. **The exit is DEC-281's.** A thesis's exit is the last close at or before its horizon end
   (`mandate_research::score::exit_close`, DEC-281 item 3), however many closes follow it. A close
   at or after the horizon end only shows that the series has passed the horizon, so the exit can no
   longer move (DEC-1002 item 3). It is never the exit. Taking the first close after the horizon would
   read a price the thesis never claimed and would contradict the scorer. So this item restates
   DEC-281 and changes no code.

## Tests

- Item 1: `version::thesis_visibility_holds_at_its_edges` (an admitted `th_02` and a refused `th_01`
  received at one instant: no opening); the property oracle `openings_only_on_an_active_thesis`
  ranks records the same way.
- Item 2: `evaluation::a_closed_thesis_is_scored_net_of_its_round_trip` (exit 110 at the Wednesday
  close before the 21:00Z horizon, with a later Thursday close of 111 in the series).

## Alternatives considered

- **The admitted record wins a tie.** Rejected: it would let an admission outlive a refusal that
  arrived at the same instant, which adds risk.
- **The first close at or after the horizon is the exit.** Rejected: see item 2.
