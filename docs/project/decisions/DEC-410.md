# DEC-410: The gate counts a ladder's remainder between rungs; a risk-reducing sell is sized to what is left, or waits

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-10-02 |
| **Decided by** | The stream K builder (`claude-code`) under DEC-79 and DEC-176, on the coordinator's ruling on #468 (2(i)): the Σ never exceeds the position, and no risk exit is refused for a remainder |
| **Story or claim** | E7-4, issue [#485](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/485) |

## Decision

1. **`available` counts the remainders** (§5.3 rule 4). The gate's `available` subtracts, beside
   the live non-protective sells, what every ladder between rungs in the instrument may still
   send: a sequence or lone ladder whose current rung's step cancel is confirmed with part
   unsold, still stepping or parked. After DEC-409, that is a parked ladder (DEC-260 (18)), or a
   lone one waiting for its agent to resume.
2. **Sized, or waits, never refused for a remainder.** Take a risk-reducing sell (any exit
   purpose) that fits beside the live sells but not beside the remainders as well:
   - **Something is left:** it is allowed, sized to what is left. `GateDecided` records the size,
     and the fold carries it to the order and to the sequence's sums.
   - **Nothing is left:** it is held `exit_between_rungs` and re-checked like every held intent.
     It is released, sized, as soon as room opens. An over-sell against the live sells alone is
     still denied `sell_exceeds_available`, as §5.3 rule 4 has always said.
3. **`next_rung` is unchanged.** Every remainder it can send is counted by item 1 from the
   moment it exists, so no exit can take its room. A defensive clip there could not be reached
   by any test, so it would leave mutants alive. The Σ oracle (DEC-408) checks every rung it
   sends.
4. **Every state, walked:**
   - **Paused, or stopped, at the step:** a sequence's ladder ends there (DEC-409) and counts
     nothing. A lone ladder sends its next rung in the same step unless it is parked; parked, it
     counts until the open and its agent's resumption.
   - **Restart between rungs:** the stepping and parked flags and the cancelled rung are folded,
     so the count is the same after a restart (named test).
   - **A refused rung:** a rung the broker refuses is terminal, and the ladder holds nothing
     more. Its remainder no longer counts, and the next exit has the room (named test).
   - **The kill switch:** E7-3's agent-scoped kill switch must end the agent's ladders, parked
     ones included, before or as it flattens. The Σ must hold through the open (pending test,
     `pending E7-3`).
   - **The interval's bound before the step:** the sequence's ladder ends at the confirmation
     (DEC-409).
   - **The open after a parked night:** exits held `session_closed` are released in `settle`
     before the parked ladder resumes, and are sized beside its remainder (named test: 8 sized
     to 6 beside 4).

## Rationale

The parked route over-sells with no pause at all. A ladder parked at 20:00 resumes at the next
open, and every exit held overnight is released at that same open, before the ladder's rung,
against the whole position. Counting the remainder in the gate keeps the Σ at every input,
whatever order the open's work runs in. Sizing a risk exit to what is left sells as much as can
be sold without crossing zero, so it is never a refusal of risk reduction (rule 13).

## Alternatives considered

- **The ladder yields: `next_rung` clips its rung to the room at resubmission, and the gate counts
  nothing.** Rejected: between the two, the Σ with the remainder counted exceeds the position,
  which is what the ruling forbids. The oracle could verify the clip only by trusting it.
- **Deny the new exit `sell_exceeds_available`, as for live sells.** Rejected by the ruling: a risk
  exit is not refused for a remainder that may never be sent.
- **The newer exit takes the parked ladder over and ends it.** Rejected: where the two overlap
  only in part, it sells less than sizing does.
