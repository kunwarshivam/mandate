# DEC-410: A ladder's remainder is a plan: it counts only while something will send it, and it gives way to the sell

## Decision

1. **A remainder counts only while something will send it** (§5.6 step 5). A ladder is between rungs from the confirmation of a step's cancel that leaves part of its rung unsold. Its remainder is that part, less what sells have trimmed off it (item 2).
   - **A sequence's remainder counts while its ladder climbs:** its agent is neither paused nor stopped, and its unprotected interval has not reached its bound. A ladder that did not climb at the confirmation has ended (DEC-409).
   - **A parked sequence past its bound ends.** If a parked sequence's interval reaches its bound, its ladder ends. When protection returns, it is not carried on as a lone ladder.
   - **A lone ladder's remainder counts unless it is parked while its agent is paused or stopped.** It counts again when the agent resumes and it still has something to send.
   - **It ends** at any of these:
     - the next rung is sent;
     - the rung is refused;
     - the exit is abandoned;
     - protection returns for a sequence that was not parked;
     - the interval's bound ends the sequence's climb;
     - a trim takes all of it.
   - **The gate's `available` is unchanged.** The gate reads the remainders beside it (`between_rungs`).
2. **The plan gives way, never the sell.** Take a sell allowed by the gate, of any exit purpose but discretionary: a risk exit, an owner exit or a flatten (protective placements are not gated, and are sized by §5.4). If it fits beside the live sells but not beside the remainders as well, the remainders are trimmed by the shortfall:
   - in their exits' id order;
   - each journaled as `ProtectionChanged remainder_trimmed`, with what it took;
   - folded, so a restart trims the same;
   - with nothing sent to the broker.

   The sell goes at its full size. No new hold reason exists. Rule 13's four holds and the live property `the_only_holds_on_an_exit_are_the_four_the_rule_names` stand.
3. **A discretionary exit is paced, not prioritised.** Beside remainders it trims nothing. It is sized to what is left (`GateDecided sized_qty`, folded into the intent's quantity), which rule 13 allows as pacing for a discretionary exit. With nothing left it is denied `sell_exceeds_available`, the verdict and reason an exit that does not fit beside live sells already gets (§5.3 rule 4), so there is no wait to bound.
4. **The plan also gives way to the position.** At every step, before anything is released or resumed, the live sells plus the remainders still counted may exceed the position. That happens when a paused agent resumes, or when the position shrank while a ladder was parked. Then the remainders are trimmed by the excess, the same way. So no rung ever sends more than is left.
5. **The kill switch.** E7-3's agent-scoped flatten trims the agent's remainders as it goes, like any flatten (§5.5). A remainder that would outlast it is a plan, so ending or trimming it sends nothing to the broker and touches no other agent's orders.
6. **Every state, walked:**
   - **How a remainder starts:** at a step's confirmed cancel with part unsold, if the ladder still climbs, or for a lone ladder always.
   - **What it blocks:** only a discretionary exit's size.
   - **How it ends:** item 1's list.
   - **Session close:** it parks for the next open, and counts unless its agent is paused or stopped.
   - **The open:** exits held overnight are released after the step's trim (item 4), and trim it again if they need room (item 2). A lone ladder resumes after both.
   - **Restart:** the stepping and parked flags, the cancelled rung, the trims and the modes are all folded, so it counts and trims the same.
   - **Mode change:**
     - a pause or stop at the confirmation ends a sequence's ladder;
     - a pause or stop later stops it counting;
     - a lone parked ladder counts again on resumption, trimmed to what is left.
   - **Two agents in one instrument:** a stopped agent's parked ladder counts nothing, so another agent's risk exit goes whole (the review's script, pinned).
7. **Attacked:**
   - **Can a remainder stay counted that nothing sends?** No.
     - A sequence counts only while it climbs, and `steps` sends its next rung then.
     - A parked one waits only for the open, or for its agent; a paused or stopped agent stops it counting.
     - The bound ends it, and the old ladder may send no rung after (the oracle checks).
   - **Can the trimmed ladder and the new sell together sell less than the exits asked for?** No.
     - A trim takes exactly the shortfall, or the overhang, and no more (the oracle checks both).
     - So the two together sell what the position allows. The plan's remainder was never more than that.

## Rationale

Round 1's review measured what the first version cost:
- it made a fifth hold on an exit;
- it sized a risk exit down beside a stopped agent's ladder that would never send (`main` sells all 10 on that route, the first version 6, or holds everything);
- its hold had no end, since the held exit was itself among the `waiting` that kept the interval open.

Adding a hold to rule 13 or shrinking a risk exit weakens a safety rule, which DEC-79 reserves for the founder. A remainder is a plan, not an order, so trimming it costs nothing at the broker and keeps the Σ at every input. Counting only what will be sent removes the phantom remainders.

## Alternatives considered

- **The first version (#494 at its first head): size the newer sell, or hold it `exit_between_rungs`.** Not taken.
  - **The review's script:** one account holds 10 AAPL. Agent A's lone ladder parks at 20:00:05 with 4 unsold, and A is stopped. Agent B's risk exit for 10 is sized to 6; with A's remainder at 10, it is held with no end.
  - **The reasons:** it needs a fifth hold on an exit, it shrinks a risk exit, and its wait is unbounded.
  - **What it would take:** an amendment to rule 13, MI-1 and the four-holds property, and a bound on the hold. Those are the founder's to make (recorded on #494 for them).
- **The ladder yields only at its next rung (`next_rung` clips to the room).** Not taken alone. Between the two, the Σ with the remainder counted exceeds the position. Item 4's step-time trim does the same work while keeping the Σ.
- **The newer exit ends the parked ladder outright.** Not taken: where they overlap only in part, the trim sells more.
