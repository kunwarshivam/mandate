## Decision (proposed)

1. **The question.** Even with the redacted projection and the numeric scrub, an owner or explain
   run's prompt holds the agent's symbols, sides, decision and order times and states, reason codes,
   rule and limit names, holdings by symbol, a thesis's conviction and confidence, and the member's
   question (with its numbers scrubbed). `AGENTS.md` says not to log
   order details, positions, or mandate content in anything that leaves the workspace deployment.
   May these reach a hosted provider under DEC-997's zero-retention routing, or only a model served
   inside the deployment?
2. **In force meanwhile, the conservative option:** owner and explain runs call a model only in the
   platform's own demo and development workspaces, whose records are paper or simulated, on the
   platform's key (DEC-2242), and CI uses recorded fakes. A customer workspace's desk serves the
   fallback (the agent's latest 10 decisions and its current mode, no model call).

## Rationale

The research loop already sends held instruments to the provider (agent harness spec HI-11), but a
history of the agent's decisions and orders is more than that, and the rule is the founder's.

## Alternatives considered

- **Treat it as covered by HI-11.** HI-11 lists held instruments, not decisions or orders.
- **Send nothing but refs.** The model could not explain anything.
