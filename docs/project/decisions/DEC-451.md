## Decision

The E7-4 implementation slice is the last executor slice the E7-2/E7-3 pending properties were
pinned behind. Their random scripts (`properties::scripted()`) draw `Step::KillSwitch` steps, and
on `main` every one of those steps answers `later_slice()`, whose `Unimplemented { story: "E7-3" }`
report is what made the pending gate accept them as failing at a stub — whatever each property's
subject. Implementing the kill switch removes that stub, and 20 of the pending properties then
evaluate against the complete executor and pass:
`a_paused_agents_exit_is_held_not_denied`;
`every_submit_effect_follows_the_order_submitted_draft_that_names_it`;
`no_crash_point_makes_the_broker_see_two_orders_for_one_intent`;
`no_recovery_submits_without_a_confirmed_absence`;
`filled_quantity_equals_the_sum_of_unique_fills`;
`no_terminal_order_leaves_its_terminal_state`;
`every_terminal_state_releases_its_reservation`;
`a_replay_emits_no_draft_and_no_broker_effect`;
`two_runs_of_the_same_inputs_give_equal_effects`;
`every_order_difference_adopts_the_broker_with_a_compensating_event`;
`no_position_cash_or_fee_difference_is_ever_adopted`;
`a_reconciliation_leaves_nothing_unexplained_and_unpaused`;
`the_reconciliation_order_is_orders_then_fills_then_positions_then_cash_then_fees`;
`no_reconciliation_run_is_appended_after_a_submission_it_did_not_cover`;
`every_copied_draft_cites_its_origin`;
`a_derived_event_id_is_a_function_of_epoch_head_and_ordinal`;
`no_draft_payload_holds_a_credential_or_an_account_number`;
`no_alert_payload_holds_an_instrument_a_price_or_a_quantity`; and
`no_submission_carries_an_intent_older_than_its_maximum_age` (E7-2/E7-3).

1. **The pending lines are not this slice's to delete.** Each carries another story's tag
   (E7-2 or E7-3), whose claim is open; DEC-77 gives the implementation PR only its own story's
   `#[ignore]` deletions. The gate's red on this branch is therefore the honest outcome, in two
   shapes, both named in `xtask`'s `BEHAVIOUR_ONLY_TESTS`: nineteen of the list below **pass** on
   the completed executor, and six more (the id-citation pair, the reservation and fold-replay
   pairs, the random-script Σ quantity, and the risk-clock walk) **fail away from their stub** on
   the kill switch's own orders — the kill switch's sells are not intents (they are exempt from
   the agent's mode, §5.5), so the oracles that read only intent-placed orders reject or
   miscount the runs the switch appears in. Both shapes are released by a tests correction under
   their own story: the passing ones by deleting their lines, the failing ones by teaching their
   oracles the kill flow.
2. **Three properties stay pending on this branch** on E7-4 oracle-gap rows, and six E7-2
   properties join them: the kill switch's own orders are not intents (they are exempt from the
   agent's mode, §5.5), so the E7-2 oracles that read only intent-placed orders — the id-citation
   pair, the reservation and fold-replay pairs, the random-script Σ quantity, and the risk-clock
   walk — reject or miscount the runs the switch appears in. Both shapes are released by a tests
   correction under their own story: the E7-4 gaps by teaching the oracles the kill flow's
   intervals and cancels; the E7-2 ones by teaching their oracles the kill flow's orders.
3. **The slice's stub boundary is exactly the 26 tests it makes live.** The kill switch, the
   confirmed cancel, the crypto add and stop-limit sequences, the fractional disclosure, the
   corporate-action preparation, and #195's all-endpoints dual test land implemented;
   `trading_domain_rc_04` stays pending on the applied-action fold, whose multiplication is E7-3's
   (`a_split_multiplies_the_position_and_ends_the_pending_action` pins it pending E7-3, so the
   slice must not take it).

## Rationale

The alternative — leaving the kill switch a stub so the gate stays green — lands no protective
sequence at all, which is the story; the other — deleting the 20 pins' pending lines — takes
another story's tag. The gate's own message names the state: "a pending test must fail until its
story is implemented". These tests' stories' executor behavior is implemented.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep a loud E7-4 stub on the kill-switch path** (DEC-160 (2)'s mechanism): rejected — the
  mechanism is for a pending test that "would then fail on a later slice's missing behaviour";
  these pass on complete behaviour, so no stub can be both loud and reachable only by them.
- **Add rows for the 20**: rejected — the rows exist to let a pending test fail away from its
  stub without stopping the gate; a test that passes is not covered by the mechanism.
- **Delete the 20 pending lines in this PR**: rejected — not this story's tests (DEC-77), and the
  claims are open.

## What it does not decide

Whether the 20 lines move to their stories' claims as a batch tests correction (a coordinator
call), and the four rows' tests corrections themselves, which remain backlog rows under E7-4.
