## Decision

1. #822, the E7-11 tests PR for the connection record and the account fingerprint, merged at
   about 1,470 added lines. That is over ES-13's 400 non-generated lines for a safety-critical
   crate ([ADR-0001](../../adr/0001-engineering-setup.md) ES-13). The coordinator accepted it at
   approval, with this note as the record.
2. From now on, a tests PR stays within 400 lines, or reserves a size waiver up front, before it
   grows past the limit.

## Rationale

#822 grew across several review rounds, and most of its lines are tests asked for by those
reviews. Splitting it at approval would have thrown away reviewed work. The limit stays: item 2
makes any later exception a decision taken in advance, not one recorded afterwards.
