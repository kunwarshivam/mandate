# DEC-2850: Until a crypto asset fee names its buy, the executor takes it from the owner's residual, which only tightens DEC-2849 (lane RK)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted (agent, under DEC-79 and DEC-176: a reading that only tightens DEC-2849, adds no risk, and holds no exit, protective order, risk exit or kill switch) |
| **Date** | 2026-10-27 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | Lane RK (Claude Code) |
| **Story or claim** | E6-15, [DEC-2849](DEC-2849.md) item 5 and its definition of Lᵢ; trading domain spec §6.3, §8.1; journal spec §9 (`FeesCharged`); backlog row E6-15u |

## Context

DEC-2849 defines agent i's lots Lᵢ for crypto as net of the asset fee taken on a buy (§6.3: received
= gross − `fee_qty`). The executor's fold takes that fee off the position when it folds the
`FeesCharged` row with `family: crypto_asset`. That row carries family, day, accrued and charged, and
names no order or fill. The fold does not hold the fee schedule, so it cannot net the fee at the
buy's fill either. The executor cannot tell from the journal whose buy the fee was taken on.

The journal's `FeesCharged` schema admits only `family: equities` today. So no crypto asset fee
reaches the fold yet, and the question is what the code does until it can.

## Invariants first

- N, the position, still falls by the fee, as the broker's quantity does once the fee posts (§11).
- No reading may raise any Sᵢ or Pᵢ above DEC-2849's, and none may hold a sell rule 13 allows.

## Decision

1. **Until the fee names its buy, it comes out of R.** The fold lowers N by the asset fee and
   leaves every Lᵢ as it was, so R = N − Σ Lᵢ falls by the fee.
2. **Why it only tightens.** Against DEC-2849's figures, where the buying agent's Lᵢ is lower by
   the fee:
   - D is higher by at most the fee, so every holder's Sᵢ = max(0, Lᵢ − D) is the same or lower.
     The buying agent's is equal; any other holder's is lower by up to the fee.
   - Σ Pᵢ = min(N, Σ Lᵢ) is the same or lower, so U is the same or higher; protection still covers
     N once (I9).
   - While D > 0, openings in the instrument hold (I6, `attribution_deficit`) until the owner
     releases or buys the gap. Exits, protective orders, risk exits and the kill switch are not
     held: I4 sizes them to min(Lᵢ, N), clipped to what is left to sell.
3. **Before crypto goes live** (backlog row E6-15u), the fee comes off the buying agent's lots, with
   a test: either the fee's record names its buy, or the fold nets the fee at the buy's fill. That
   change ends this decision.

## Consequences

- `mandate_executor::holders` and the fold's `fees_charged` read the fee as item 1 says, and the
  holders fuzz asserts it.
- A crypto agent's own fee would hold its next opening in the instrument, at the size of the fee,
  until the owner releases it. That is why item 3 must land before crypto goes live.

## Alternatives considered

- **Spread the fee over the holders pro rata.** Refused: it is not DEC-2849's reading either, and it
  can lower a holder's lots for a fee another agent's buy paid.
- **Take it from the largest holder.** Refused: it can charge an agent for a fee it did not pay,
  which lowers its sell authority and coverage on another agent's trade.
