# DEC-423: A trim of the whole position is never withheld for the minimum order size

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-10-02 (UTC) |
| **Decided by** | the coordinator's rulings on [#504](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/504) and on [#520](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/pull/520) (round 1, which narrowed the first to the whole position), recorded by the stream G builder (claim [#123](https://github.com/kunwarshivam/mandate/issues/123)) under DEC-79 and DEC-176. It only lets a risk exit go that is held today, and reducing risk needs no approval (`AGENTS.md` rule 2) |
| **Story or claim** | E6-4, the full-close exemption to the trim's minimum (#504 review, M1; #520 review, round 1); this tests PR is DEC-77 stage 1 |

## Decision

1. **The exemption.** A `trim_to_target` trim whose quantity is the whole position held is
   proposed even below the instrument's `min_order_size`. Since the trim is capped at what the
   agent's own non-protective resting sells leave unsold (DEC-399 item 7), it is the whole position
   only when none of them rests beside it. A protective stop is not one of them, so a position
   under its stop is still closed whole.
   - Trading spec §5.3 rule 2 exempts a sell closing the full position by its exact quantity, so
     the broker does not apply the minimum to this order.
   - Nothing further down the gate refuses it. `account_rules` reaches rule 2's minimum only for
     an opening, and a risk exit is paced by nothing (`conduct::pacing`).
   - Withholding it would hold a risk exit for a minimum the broker does not apply, which
     `AGENTS.md` rule 13 forbids.
2. **What stays.** Any other trim below `min_order_size` is still withheld (DEC-399 item 5). That
   includes the remainder beside one of the agent's own resting sells: the order is not the
   position's quantity, so rule 2's exception does not reach it, and the exit path never emits an
   order the broker would refuse (DEC-163 item 4). Nothing is lost by that. If the resting sell
   fills, the next evaluation's trim of what is left is the whole position and is proposed. If it
   ends unfilled, the next trim is sized on the whole position again.
3. **The tests PR (DEC-77 stage 1).** `trim::proposals` calls `exempt_full_close` for a trim that
   is below the minimum and equals the quantity held. Until the implementation PR, the stub
   refuses the whole call as `GateError::Unimplemented("the full-close trim", "E6-4")`.
   - On `main` that trim was withheld silently.
   - No production caller reaches `trim_proposals` yet, so the stricter refusal reaches no order.
   - The family-B harness's cases never reach it: every trim there is 3 shares of 10.

   The pending test `hand::a_trim_of_the_whole_position_is_never_withheld_for_the_minimum` runs
   every row before one assertion, so under the stub each row's outcome is reported. Proposed:
   - an equity full close of 1 share at a 2-share minimum, which is also the withheld row below
     once its resting sell fills;
   - the same full close under a resting protective stop for the share;
   - a crypto full close of 0.0002 at a 0.001 minimum, at a zero target;
   - the same crypto full close at a target above zero (factor 1e-10).

   Withheld:
   - a 1-share trim of 2 held beside a 1-share resting sell, at a 2-share minimum;
   - a 1-share trim of 2 held, one increment short of the position, at a 2-share minimum;
   - a 3-share trim of 10 at a 4-share minimum.
4. **The implementation PR** deletes the `#[ignore]` line and makes the exemption infallible, so
   the stub's refusal of the whole call does not outlive it. Any later refusal on this path is per
   instrument: one instrument's trim never withholds another's (`AGENTS.md` rule 13).
5. **Then the spec and the reference (ES-22).**
   - §5.5's sentence on "a trim that would close the whole position" gains the exemption as
     written: the whole position, not what is left to sell.
   - `reference/mandate/ref.py`'s `below_minimum_order` guard skips a trim equal to the position
     held.
   - A new family-B case states the full-close trim.
   - The harness's `trim_guards` moves in step, as #498 did.

## Rationale

The #504 review's case is 0.0002 BTC at a 600,000 bid (120 dollars), with a cap of 100, a
confirmed factor of 0.5 and a 0.001 `min_order_size`. The exemption does not release it. The gate
puts a fractionable instrument on a 1e-9 grid, so the trim is `ceil(70 × 0.0002 ÷ 120)` =
0.000116667. That is below the minimum and is not the whole position, so it stays withheld. It is
released only once the instrument's real quantity grid is a gate input, which is the backlog's
[E6-4 "real quantity grid"](../06-backlog-v1.md) row. On the 1e-9 grid the exemption fires only
when the target is worth less than one increment, which is why both crypto rows sit at or near a
zero target.

The exemption is rule 2's own, so the gate sends nothing the broker refuses.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep withholding and document it.** That holds a risk exit for a minimum the broker does not
  apply to a full close, which rule 13 forbids.
- **Exempt every sub-minimum trim.** A partial sell below the venue minimum is refused by the
  broker, so the order would only fail later. Withholding it until the excess grows is the same
  answer, and it is visible.
- **Compare with what is left to sell (the quantity held less the agent's own resting sells).**
  This was #504's ruling. Beside a resting sell, that trim is not a sell of the position's exact
  quantity, so the broker's minimum still applies and the order would be refused. Rule 2 does not
  cover it, and the next evaluation releases the whole position anyway (item 2).
