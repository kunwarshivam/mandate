## Context

An agent's kill switch raises one flatten per instrument, `k-<switch>-<n>`, sized at the agent's
sub-ledger when it is raised (DEC-2354 item 2). The flatten then waits: on the cancel of the
protection resting in the instrument (§5.4), and between the rungs of §5.6's ladder. Its first
send and every later rung send that size, less what it already sold.

The owner may release the position (mandate spec §3.1) in that wait. The released shares are the
owner's from then on, but the flatten still offers them: with 10 shares, a switch and then a release
of 4, the executor sends a sell of 10 (`a_release_while_the_flatten_waits_shrinks_its_sell`). That
breaks DEC-2354 invariant 1, that the switch never sells shares the agent released.

## Invariants first

1. A kill switch's flatten never sends a sell for more than the agent's sub-ledger in the
   instrument at the moment it sends, whatever was released since it was raised.
2. A flatten never sells less than the sub-ledger because of a release: a release takes only the
   released quantity off it.
3. Every quantity a flatten does not send for a release is journaled, so a replay reads the same
   sends.
4. Nothing changes for a flatten with no release since it was raised, nor for any other exit.

## Decision

1. **Each send is capped at the sub-ledger.** A switch's flatten (an intent `is_flatten` names)
   sends at most the agent's sub-ledger in the instrument at that send, `kill.rs`'s `sub_ledger`,
   which already nets the agent's sells filled so far and its releases. That holds for its first
   send and for each rung of §5.6's ladder. A shorter send is journaled `ProtectionChanged
   rung_short` with the intent, the quantity it does not send and the quantity it sends, as DEC-410
   item 4 already journals a rung the position cuts short.
2. **Nothing left abandons it.** A first send with a sub-ledger of zero is not sent: the flatten is
   journaled `OrderAbandoned` with the reason `released_to_owner`. A later rung with nothing left
   ends the ladder through `rung_short`, as DEC-410 item 4 does.
3. **A sell already at the broker** is not resized by the executor. Mandate spec §3.1's release
   cancels every working order of the agent's, the flatten's rung among them, and the confirmed
   cancel's next rung is then capped by item 1.

4. **Not covered here: protection after the flatten ends.** A flatten abandoned or finished ends
   §5.4's sequence, which re-places protection sized at the long position, released shares
   included: after a release of all 10, the agent's OCO of 10 is placed on the owner's shares.
   That sizing ignores releases everywhere protection is placed, not only after a flatten, so it
   is its own backlog row (E6-4, protection sizing after a release), with its own pair.

## Alternatives

- **Cancel the part a release takes from a sell already sent.** Rejected for this decision: the
  release already cancels every working order of the agent's (§3.1), and item 1 caps what follows.
- **Abandon the flatten and raise a new one at the new size.** Rejected: it needs a second intent
  id per instrument and switch, which DEC-485 item 2's `k-<switch>-<n>` does not have, for the same
  sells item 1 sends.

## Consequences

- `mandate-executor`: `intent.rs`'s `submit` and `protection.rs`'s rung send cap a flatten's
  quantity at `sub_ledger`.
- No journal change: `rung_short` and `OrderAbandoned` already carry what items 1 and 2 record.
- Tests in `mandate-executor/tests/hand.rs`, pending E6-4 until the implementation:
  `a_release_while_the_flatten_waits_shrinks_its_sell`,
  `a_release_of_everything_while_the_flatten_waits_abandons_it` and
  `a_release_between_rungs_shrinks_the_next_rung`.
