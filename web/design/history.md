# Owlhead design history

The dated record of founder feedback and the decisions it led to, moved out of
[DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) under DEC-511. New entries are one line each: the date, what was asked,
and the DEC. Detail belongs in the decision file.

- **2026-09-28, first look rejected.** PR #253's first screenshots (a generic card dashboard) were rejected by the founder as too plain.
- **2026-09-28, three directions.** Impeccable's `shape` flow produced three concepts over the same fixtures. The founder picked a transit-signage direction and asked for tighter gutters. The product was named Owlhead the same day (DEC-201).
- **2026-09-28, Kumo and a dashboard shell.** shadcn/ui, Radix and lucide gave way to Kumo and Phosphor, with a collapsible sidebar, breadcrumbs, ⌘K and agent-scoped navigation.
- **2026-09-28, a first palette and the logo** (DEC-202, DEC-203), both since superseded or amended by DEC-204.
- **2026-09-28, the calm redesign in Ink and Gold (DEC-204).** The founder asked for a consumer-grade product at the level of Robinhood, Public or Wealthfront, without gamification. The signage structure and type were superseded: three families gave way to Mona Sans in one tight scale; square fields and 2px rules to soft corners, hairlines and space; the dashboard to one hero number over a scrubbable equity chart; one density to two. The palette became Ink and Gold with a dark theme, and the logo ink on light and off-white on dark. Every safety behaviour stayed.
- **2026-09-29, glass on the frame only** ([DEC-208](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). From a mockup, the founder approved frosted glass for the sticky header and the phone tab bar, and turned it down anywhere else; the opacity went from the mockup's 58% to 72% so the header's text keeps AA over whatever scrolls under it.
- **2026-09-29, hero-first number type** ([DEC-208](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). From a mockup, the founder approved a larger equity figure with muted, raised half-size cents, and the day's change on a soft pill toned by its sign. The figure got its own display size, scaled by its container rather than the viewport so long values fit on phones; the approval's action keeps the hero size.
- **2026-09-29, a dock, a ticker and a command bar** ([DEC-208](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The founder approved three changes to the frame: on desktop a floating glass dock replaces the sidebar, the lockup moves to the header and the content takes the full width; a ticker tape of held instruments' prices and day changes replaces the status strip under the header while every feed answers; and the ⌘K trigger becomes a wide command bar in the middle of the header.
- **2026-09-29, an agent wire instead of a ticker** ([DEC-208](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The founder turned the price ticker down as too much like a brokerage: the strip under the header became a wire of what the agents are doing, in ink and muted text, from 64rem (it was first drawn from 40rem; the phone below 64rem has no wire). The same day, the current page's crumb stopped truncating beside the command bar, and the bar's prompt became "Jump to an agent or screen…", because the palette does not search orders.
- **2026-09-29, Ink and Ultramarine ([DEC-205](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)).** The founder compared seven accents (gold, iris, ultramarine, petrol, jade, plum, graphite) and page colours in a live preview and chose ultramarine on a cool white page. Only DEC-204's gold accent and warm paper changed: the ramp became ultramarine (hue 266) and paper and ink moved to a cool hue (255), with every token name and meaning kept. Colour-blind friendly moved the gain from blue to teal, so it stays apart from the new accent, deepened the light loss one step, and added tritanopia to the simulation.
- **2026-09-29, a quiet Stop ([DEC-206](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)).** The founder found the always-filled 44px Stop pill too big and prominent for a calm screen. Stop became an ink outline, 40px on desktop (the command bar's height), that fills in ink only while something needs the owner. It stays in the same place, the same size, never disabled and never hidden. This amends DEC-204 item 2 ("the header keeps Stop as its dominant control").
- **2026-09-29, a labelled dock** ([DEC-208](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The founder asked for a more prominent desktop dock so people are not lost: every item gained its name under the icon, the current section a tinted pill, and the dock a denser glass and a firmer edge; the tooltips went, and "More screens" became "More". The header's folded menu became "Alerts and account", so only the dock says More on desktop.
- **2026-09-29, the phone is a remote control ([DEC-207](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)).** Squeezing the desktop onto a phone had made it crowded: two navigation systems, a status strip on every screen and one request shown three times on Home. The founder approved a simpler phone: three things in the header, four tabs with More for the rest, a banner only when a feed fails, Needs you first on Home with headroom instead of P&L on the agent rows, an agent page of state, equity and headroom with its sections as links, and one request per screen with its choices pinned. Tablet and desktop did not change.
- **2026-09-29, Public Sans ([DEC-209](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)).** The founder called Inter "an AI smell tell" and asked for the face to be chosen with Impeccable. Mona Sans is on the same reflex list, so it gave way to Public Sans after Archivo, Golos Text and Public Sans were rendered in the built app; the hero's tracking went from -0.035em to -0.03em. A serif figure face for the hero, echoing the wordmark, was tried and turned down.
- **2026-09-29, the performance disclosure behind a symbol** ([DEC-210](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The dashed `[[DISCLOSURE-PERFORMANCE]]` tag beside every P&L cluttered every screen; the founder asked for it behind a symbol that opens on click and hover. It stays beside every P&L, inline in print and on record screens, pending counsel's confirmation.
- **2026-09-29, one system from eight branches** ([DEC-208](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The frame, the dock, the wire, the hero number and the one current section tab were recorded together as DEC-208. Where the branches met: the display figure took Public Sans's -0.03em and proportional lining figures, with every other figure tabular; Stop's desktop pill and the command bar share 40px; the phone's agent rows carry headroom and so no disclosure symbol, while every P&L keeps one; the phone's Stop turns quiet and loud as on desktop; the phone meter's post became the deep accent to hold 3:1 on its track; and the wire starts at 64rem, with the dock.
- **2026-09-29, sign-in and the public pages' frame** ([DEC-211](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The founder chose Supabase Auth, with Google for sign-up and the first sign-in and a passkey after. The pages before sign-in got a frame of their own, the app's glass header with only the brand and "Sign in", so the welcome and sign-in pages carry no Stop, dock or wire.
- **2026-09-29, the landing page** ([DEC-212](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The founder asked for a public page at owlhead.ai that looks like a serious financial product and like the app, not a brokerage and not an AI template. It is built from the app's own screens and system: hairlines instead of cards, real screenshots instead of illustrations, a ledger of what the agent can't do and what the owner can always do, and compliance text only as placeholders.
- **2026-09-29, a retro text page and a private beta** ([DEC-213](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The founder turned the screenshot page down for one page of text that explains what Owlhead is, why and how, and gathers interest: in the manner of a 1990s home page, with a guestbook that records requests for a place in the private beta. The placeholders gave way to plain wording at the founder's direction, and the screenshot pipeline was removed.
- **2026-09-29, Ink and Volt** ([DEC-214](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The founder moved the accent from ultramarine to volt (hue 120): olive volt as light mode's text and focus ring, pale volt in dark, and a neon highlight that is always a fill under ink type. Every token kept its name and meaning, and every pair was measured again.
- **2026-09-29, no agent wire** ([DEC-215](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). On the live dashboard the founder asked for the strip above it to go. The wire was removed, and from 64rem the frame follows the phone: nothing under the header while every feed answers, the full status strip while one is stale or failing and on record screens, and the "Fixture data" tag at the foot of the page.
- **2026-09-30, the landing page's period behaviour.** Asked to improve the landing page, within DEC-213's page: the guestbook became a button in the hero and after "Who it's for", and the form's button took the same name; the contents selects the section being read and the status bar shows where a link goes; problems with the form sit in a message box; and on a phone the guide buttons sit two by two and the record fits without scrolling sideways.
- **2026-09-30, a livelier dashboard** ([DEC-216](../../docs/project/04-decision-log.md#decisions)). The founder passed on feedback that the dashboard "looks dull and not as good looking and fun as robinhood" and asked for more life in it. Dollar figures roll digit by digit, the range pill springs between ranges in the neon highlight, the section tab's underline slides, a new range fades the line up, and a current hero line's last point pulses. Approve, Skip and Stop are untouched, and nothing moves under reduced motion.
- **2026-10-05, cleanups after sign-in** ([DEC-452](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-452.md)). The founder asked for Minesweeper to go from the landing desktop ("just noise"), for the logo to become an agent owl in a colour picked at random each time, for the pixel owls to look better in the same style, for some buttons after sign-in to borrow the landing page's look, and for Stop to leave the top header ("that's not a right place"). Minesweeper was removed. The owls were redrawn on the same 16-pixel grid: a dark outline, round eyes with a highlight, shaded wings, a pale belly and a two-tone beak. The brand owl replaced the traced mark on screen. Stop moved to the end of the dock and of the phone tab bar, still on every screen, with one Stop sheet for both. A few buttons took the landing page's bevel and Pixelify label.
- **2026-10-05, a design pass from screenshots** ([DEC-467](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-467.md)). The founder asked for the landing page and the product to look "beautiful and premium and easy on the eyes", functional and extremely aesthetic, with the retro desktop and its wallpaper kept. From screenshots of every screen: Home leads with a timeline of the agents' decisions beside a rail of Needs you and a compact account figure; news moves to the agent it concerns; one paper P&L note per list; one page grid for Home, an agent and the inbox, whose rail says what sends a request; one approvals count, on the dock and the tab bar; Approve and Skip become one solid key in Public Sans; on the landing page the record, the questions and the guestbook open as windows, the hero gains "See the record", and the wordmark is set upright. A mandate-writing prototype runs at `/design/new-agent` on fixture rules.
- **2026-10-05, one key, a logon window and an honest browser** ([DEC-469](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-469.md)). On a screenshot of the landing page's browser the founder found "a lot of things here are not actionable, either disable them visually or add actions", then asked for the sign-in page to match the landing page and for every button in the dashboard to match Approve and Skip ("I like accept/decline buttons. lets bring consistency"). The menu bar became real menus, Home and Reload act, and what cannot act is etched grey; sign-in became a logon window on the landing page's wallpaper and the site header went; and the six button styles in the app became one key, with Stop, the kill switch and navigation keeping their own.
- **2026-10-05, the money first** ([DEC-468](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-468.md)). On a screenshot of the decisions-first Home the founder asked "why would people care about decisions after it is taken? they will care about the money". The account figure and the agents took back Home's main column, with the chart at 260px on today; the latest four decisions moved to the rail under Needs you on desktop, and three to the foot of a phone's Home, in a timeline narrowed to fit.
- **2026-10-05, a copy audit of the landing page.** The founder asked why the record was on the landing page ("what is unique about it? it is just explaining hashing?") and then for an audit of the whole page's copy. The record now answers "Why did it buy that?" step by step, with the tamper check second and the hero's button "See why it traded". Claims that did not match the product were corrected: Stop also sells what the agents hold and ends them; a position's stop is the protection the owner may turn off, and a take-profit is optional; how many tries an idea gets is the owner's setting, not a fixed three; the path to live is per agent. The desk, cloud and MCP features say they are coming, agents that bring their own ideas join the list of what is coming, an unanswered request is said to be skipped, and Questions gains "What if I miss a request?". The footer disclaimer and the investment-advice answer are unchanged, for counsel.
- **2026-10-06, the agent journey on fixtures** ([DEC-472](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-472.md)). The founder pointed out that "creating an agent is missing right now" and asked for the complete journey on fixture data. The prototype moved to `/agents/new` and deploys: a fifth section picks the model, listed alphabetically with nothing preselected and each setting empty; confirming money or the universe refuses in place what V-002 or V-006 would refuse; and A5 shows the whole record, open, above a Confirm and deploy button that asks for a passkey, with what happened next set apart under "After you confirmed". The new agent's owl, name and first request appear there and on Agents and Home; the request, the fill and the protective stop use the screens that already existed.
- **2026-10-06, setting up an agent as a conversation** ([DEC-476](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-476.md)). The founder asked for the mandate's values to "come from LLMs for better interpretability" and for every input to be answered in a conversation, "like Claude Code, open ChatGPT", because forms confuse. `/agents/new` became one chat: the platform's questions sit on the page as plain text, the owner's messages sit in bubbles on the right, and what was read is quoted back on the mandate surface under "Noted from your words". The model's own replies are set apart as a quotation under "The model said", with no buttons; every action (Yes and No for an amount written in words, the two models, Confirm section, Review and confirm) is the app's one key on a deterministic card. The draft and then one section at a time appear in the conversation, and "Reading your words…" shows while the model reads. A fixture stands in for the model, and the composer says so.
- **2026-10-06, a plain chat** ([DEC-477](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-477.md)). The founder asked for it to be "as simple as chatgpt and claude chat ui" ("what is the confirm thingy? and why so much friction?") and asked what "In your words" meant. The cards went: no "Noted from your words", no "The model said", no Yes and No for an amount written in words ("I read “about three grand” as $3,000.00." says it once), no Confirm section and no separate confirmation screen. Replies are plain sentences, the owner's messages are bubbles, and the composer is one rounded box with a round send key. Once nothing is missing the conversation shows the agent once, as a summary card with every value, tagged where Owlhead proposed it, and one Create agent key that asks for a passkey; saying a change shows a new card and marks the old one replaced.
- **2026-10-06, pixel icons** ([DEC-478](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-478.md)). The founder asked for an icon set "not famous for ai slop", called Lucide slop too, and chose Pixelarticons from a comparison sheet beside the owls. Every app icon moved from Phosphor to it at 24px; the dock's and tab bar's current item lost its filled icon (the pill and the label carry it), and Stop keeps its octagon, redrawn on the pixel grid.
- **2026-10-06, Messages and the desk** ([DEC-479](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-479.md), [DEC-480](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-480.md)). The founder asked for a copilot to "do everything" from, and for agents that reach out in a chat, then approved the v2 mockup with "needs you" on the side. Messages gives each agent a thread made of its journal, a desk that lays out how its latest request was made, and a composer whose answers come from the record. The phone's Approvals tab became Messages, carrying the count. Approve and Skip in a chat card, replies in the agent's voice, and setup inside the copilot wait for the founder (DEC-480); until then the card links to the request.
- **2026-10-06, the Owlhead copilot** ([DEC-479](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-479.md) item 8). The same record answers from anywhere, with ⌘J. It docks beside the page only from 110rem: below that the page keeps its breakpoint's layout in less width and overflowed (Home's chart ran under Needs you at 1440px), so the panel floats instead, clear of the paper badge and the dock.
- **2026-10-06, Messages edge to edge, and Markdown** ([DEC-481](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-481.md)). The founder asked for "the gutter on the messages" to be zero, to "use the full real estate", and for the chat to "handle any formatting". Messages dropped the page frame: three panes from edge to edge, each to the foot of the window, the thread's title and tabs in one row over the log, and the composer docked at its foot with no note under it. The founder turned the hedging copy down ("Own the work ... No serious product would say things like that"): answers lost their "From the record" mark, the composers their "Owlhead answers from your record" note, and the desk its line on who wrote it. Model output stays quoted and named; setup's composer keeps its note on the fixture model. The log follows the latest entry until the reader scrolls up, then offers "Jump to latest", and sending always brings it back. Messages render GitHub-flavoured Markdown, with nothing fetched, no raw HTML and only safe links; the model's links are shown, never opened; and record answers stay plain sentences, with the limits answer as a real table.
- **2026-10-06, an open thread gets the phone's screen** ([DEC-482](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-482.md)). The founder found a phone thread too cramped "to do anything". The app header, the thread's header, the Chat and Desk tabs and a two-line pinned request left the log 414px of an 844px screen and 192px of a 667px one. The thread's one-row bar now replaces the header and carries the paper badge, the agent's name opens the agent, Desk is one link in the bar, and the pinned request is 44px. The log has 553px and 376px. Only the reader's own scroll stops the log following, not a resize. The same day, the founder asked the same of the agent's page and of Needs you on Home, "not very noticeable" but not stacked "one by one". The agent's page drops its second paper badge, Stop this agent becomes a quiet Stop on the name's row instead of a row of its own, and the chart is 200px. Needs you, on Home and on the agent's page, is one sideways row of cards.
- **2026-10-09, the brand owl out of the agents' colours** ([DEC-739](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-739.md) item 2). From critique C-15, the founder ruled that the brand owl never wears an agent's colour, since an owl is an agent here. It takes the logo's colour, ink in light and paper in dark, on every load; DEC-452's random pick among the agents' colours is withdrawn.
- **2026-10-10, the app live in the landing page's browser** ([DEC-906](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-906.md)). The founder liked the page as it was but asked to "break the mould": the browser now opens on the app itself, live in its tab, driven by the browser's own Back, Forward, Home, Reload and address. The founder saw that "all owls on the web page act together"; each now blinks, turns and glances on its own. Of the screenshot tab's captions ("Home puts your money first…", "These are the app's own screens, not drawings of it") the founder said "stop writing explanations in the landing page copy", which DESIGN.md now holds.
- **2026-10-10, wallpapers of moonlight and snow** ([DEC-906](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-906.md) item 7). Asked whether the wallpapers should match the product, the founder said yes: the Great Wave and Van Gogh's wheat field said nothing about an owl. The set is now moonlit nights and snow from the Met (Hiroshige, Friedrich, Dahl, Homer), one at random per visit, with the palest, Hiroshige's autumn moon at Ishiyama, as the light theme's default and the tour video's desktop.
- **2026-10-10, Sign in and Sign up in words** ([DEC-906](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-906.md) item 8). The founder asked to "make signing in/up much more obvious, like posthog does and not just icon". The desktop's bar now names both at every width, on the taskbar or the Mac's menu bar, Sign up filled; the key icon went, and on a phone the clocks and the Mac's greyed Edit menu make room.
- **2026-10-10, a long page under the desktop** ([DEC-907](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-907.md)). The founder asked for the landing page to become "a long scrollable landing page" with the desktop kept as its first screen, in Manrope and Gascogne Serial, and said "dont repeat": the first draft restated the desktop's lines. The page now rises over the desktop with what the desktop does not say, shown in the app's own screens. Gascogne waits on the founder buying its licence; Sorts Mill Goudy stands in.
- **2026-10-10, tide, the owl and springs** ([DEC-907](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-907.md) items 4 to 7). The founder asked to show that the page scrolls, for a third colour by colour theory beside black and yellow, and for "a 3d something that moves around the page" with physics, then chose tide and the voxel owl from a proposal with "yes, use it sparingly". A "Scroll" pill sits on the desktop, tide fills one section, the brand owl flies down the page in voxels on springs, and the page's pieces settle on springs.
- **2026-10-10, an owl, a night and a thread** ([DEC-907](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-907.md) items 5, 8 and 9). The founder said the flying owl "looks like cat than an owl", asked for something interesting instead of the lock screen's blank space, liked the movements, and asked for something "pixelated (or threaded)" in contrasting teal. The owl got its own taller sprite with a domed head, a facial disc, a beak and wings that flap; the lock screen became a pixel night with the brand owl awake in it; and a tide thread is sewn down the page, knotted wherever the owl lands.
- **2026-10-10, the long page sells** ([DEC-908](../../docs/project/decisions/DEC-908.md)). The founder said the long page is marketing material and its parts read like decisions ("limit order, notification nothing more, these are not how you sell"). Each part now sells one thing the visitor gets: your rules hold, the big calls come to you, it can tell you why, the stakes are in dollars. The desktop and the app keep the explaining.
