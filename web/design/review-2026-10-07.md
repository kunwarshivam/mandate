# UI review, 7 October 2026

A working list from a screenshot review of every screen on fixture data (1440px and 390px, light and
dark). Each item is a candidate change under `web/`; none is decided. Items that change [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md)
rules (owl poses, the badge system, where Needs you sits) need a DEC before they land.

## Findings and proposals

1. **Owls show agent state.** One pose per mode: awake (normal), asleep (paused), eyes shut or greyed
   (stopped), alert (waiting on the owner). Drawn on the existing 16-pixel grid in `web/brand/`. The
   pose changes only when the mode changes, so it answers a change and never moves on its own.
2. **Needs you stands out on desktop.** The phone leads with a pale volt card; desktop shows plain
   text at the top of the right rail with the same weight as the decision feed. Use the card on
   desktop too, with the deadline as a fixed time, never a countdown.
3. **One status badge system in the decision feed.** "Allowed" is filled, "Not allowed" is outlined,
   some wrap to their own line, and "Allowed" beside "Asked you for approval" reads as a
   contradiction. One set of badges in a fixed column, and a distinct "Waiting for you" state so
   "Allowed" only ever means the gate passed it.
4. **Less fine print.** Three lines of small grey text under the Home chart, several info symbols
   and three "Fixture data" tags per page. Fold the as-of time, the simulated note and the fixture
   tag into one status line per screen. Required disclosures stay as counsel set them.
5. **Agent-row sparklines.** A near-flat line over a dashed limit far below it looks broken. Scale
   each sparkline to its own range and draw the daily loss limit as a shaded band, so the row shows
   how close the agent is to its limit.
6. **Headroom as the agent page's centrepiece.** The mandate field's bars are blue on pale blue and
   squeezed into the sidebar. A headroom strip (position, holdings, loss today, distance to the next
   drawdown rung) under the equity figure, in the series colours with higher-contrast bars.
7. **Fill the Set up an agent page.** Today one paragraph and a blank box. Add example prompts, show
   the mandate filling in beside the text as the owner types, and let the owl hatch when the mandate
   is confirmed (answers the owner's action). `/design/new-agent` already prototypes most of this.
8. **Smaller fixes.**
   - Breadcrumb shows "… > Agents" on top-level screens; drop the ellipsis there.
   - The "Normal" mode chip's icon looks like a ticked checkbox, so it reads as a control. Use a
     status dot or the owl pose instead.
   - The top bar and the dock both navigate; the dock holds nine items plus Stop. Move Audit and
     Connections into More on desktop as the phone does.
   - The Agents list has a wide empty middle at 1440px; cap the width or use a two-column grid.
   - Carry the logon window's look onto the first screen after sign-in, or show the owl lineup on
     the empty Home, so the landing page and the product read as one.

## Round two: scenarios, overlays, phone

Functional first, then aesthetic.

9. **Drawdown scenario, equity chart.** The account line stays at the normal scenario's values and
   only the last point drops, so the chart shows a vertical cliff at the right edge (`sc-drawdown`).
   `buildWorkspace("drawdown")` (`web/src/fixtures/workspace.ts:587`) changes the agent's equity but
   not the series. A real drawdown would show a slope. Fix the fixture so the series ends where the
   state says, and add a fixture test that the last point of every series equals the live equity.
10. **Drawdown scenario, the agent row.** Two "Your mandate" pills with full sentences push the row
    to three times its height, and the sparkline becomes a single tick. Restrictions belong in the
    mode chip ("Exits only", with the restrictions on hover or on the agent page), not inline.
11. **Stale scenario, Needs you.** Five items with the same weight: an approval with a deadline,
    "Market data stale", "Push relay down", and two per-instrument stale prices. Only the approval
    needs the owner; the rest are information. Keep requests in Needs you and move degraded feeds to
    the degraded bar, which already names them; one line per agent, not per instrument.
12. **Deployment unreachable.** The page is right to hide agent data, and the copy is good. But the
    dock still offers every screen, the approvals count disappears without saying so, and the Stop
    control opens the same sheet. Grey the screens that need the deployment, and have Stop open
    straight onto the "reach the broker directly" text, since that is all it can do.
13. **Stop sheet.** Three big filled blocks (black, red, outlined red) for Pause, Stop and Close all
    stack directly under three agent rows with no space. The most drastic action is the widest and
    the most text-heavy, so it draws the eye first, the opposite of "pausing comes first". Give the
    account section a heading rule and space, make the three the same visual weight, and put the
    bullets of Close everything behind its confirmation step.
14. **Approvals list.** The rows say "(8 min left)", a countdown on an approval. DESIGN.md says
    deadlines never move; confirm the text is static per render and does not tick. The resolved
    rows' badges ("Approved and submitted") are wider than their column and collide with the
    scenario panel; give the badge column a fixed width.
15. **Messages.** Three columns of near-equal weight and the chat column is the narrowest thing on
    the page at 1440px. The thread lists a card that repeats the approval page. The Headroom rail
    duplicates the mandate field. Make the thread the widest column, and show the rail only above
    about 80rem.
16. **Positions.** Agent 2's Mark column wraps "as of 14:05:17, 5 s ago" onto two lines with a
    stray comma; Agent 1's does not. Two "Fixture data" tags at the foot of the page. One
    "Unrealized paper P&L" footnote per table where one per page would do.
17. **Audit and Connections.** Four of six Audit rows and the whole Connections page say "Next
    slice" or "Coming in the next slice" with an hourglass. Hide what is not built from the dock and
    the palette, or mark it once in the list, not as its own page.
18. **Command palette.** Its "Stop…" row is the first item and reads as the default action when the
    owner has typed nothing. Put Go to first and Safety after, or put Stop in its own row below the
    list; it stays one keystroke away either way.
19. **Keyboard focus.** After three Tabs no focus ring is visible on the Home screen (`focus`). Check
    the order (brand, workspace switcher, search?) and that every stop paints the volt ring the
    design names.
20. **Phone agent page.** The y-axis labels take a third of the width, the level labels
    ("High-water mark", "Daily loss limit") overlap the line, and Levels collapse below. Hide the
    axis on phones, draw the levels as hairlines with the label at the right edge, as the desktop
    chart does.
21. **Phone More sheet.** Account, Workspace, Search, then Approvals and Alerts at the same size as
    the groups below. The top of the sheet should be the two things the dock does not hold and the
    owner opens most (Approvals, Alerts), then Search, then the rest; account and workspace belong at
    the bottom with the theme.
22. **Mandate page.** The strongest screen in the product: the ladder and the headroom bars say
    exactly what the agent may do. Three things weaken it: "Equity now" is a black bar that reads as
    a button; the ladder's top rail of ticks duplicates the list; the "Proposed by the platform",
    "You said", "From template", "Platform default" tags use four different styles for one idea
    (provenance). One tag style, and the dotted ones kept for what the platform proposed.
23. **Sign-in.** The logon window sits on the Great Wave with no owl in sight, and in this build
    the single button is "Open Owlhead". Fine for a fixture build; for the beta the window should
    carry the brand owl in its title bar (it does, tiny) and the five-owl lineup under the heading
    so the two pages share a face.
24. **Dark mode.** Clean; the chart's pale green on black reads well. The "PAPER" hatched chip,
    the pale volt mandate field and the "Normal" chip were tuned for light and sit a little hot in
    dark. Worth a pass with `contrast-pairs.ts` once the badge system (item 3) lands.

## Round three: the set-up conversation and the landing windows

25. **Item 7 corrected.** `new-agent.test.tsx` forbids example amounts, placeholders and "e.g." in
    the set-up flow, so the platform never suggests a figure (DEC-476, DEC-477). Example prompts
    are out. What stays from item 7: show the mandate taking shape beside the chat as fields are
    understood, and let the owl hatch on Create.
26. **The parser gives up without help.** "Put $5,000 on it, buy dips in AAPL and MSFT, I can lose
    $500 at most" set only the loss limit; the symbols and the $5,000 were dropped and the reply
    asked for "a smaller amount" without saying what it read. A wrong answer at any step repeats the
    same sentence word for word, four times in a row. The fixture stands in for a model, but the
    replies are the product's: say what was read ("I read $5,000 and a $500 loss limit; I didn't
    catch which symbols"), and on a second miss say what shape of answer would work.
27. **Lookback and z-score are asked of a retail owner.** After choosing a model the conversation
    asks "How many bars should the model read back over?" and "How far below its average, in
    standard deviations?". Those are envelope fields (AGENTS.md rule 11), but a defaulted answer
    marked `default` in the summary, changeable by saying so, would fit the rest of the flow; today
    the owner must invent a number with no range given.
28. **The summary is too long to confirm.** The "Your agent" field runs about 2,500px: six sentences
    in prose, then thirty rows in six groups, then a hash. Most rows are `proposed` or `default`.
    Lead with the six sentences and the three dollar losses, fold the thirty rows under "Everything
    it will hold to", and let the hash sit in a footnote. Create agent should sit at the end of the
    short form, not after the long one.
29. **`/design/new-agent` does not exist.** DESIGN.md names it as the mandate-writing prototype; the
    route renders "No such page". Either the page moved into `/agents/new` and the doc is stale, or
    the route was lost. One of the two needs fixing.
30. **The landing windows.** The record, Questions and the guestbook open as cascading windows and
    each is well written; the record's eight-step table is the best explanation in the product.
    Three things: the windows open on top of one another and the stack hides the home page, so
    "Sign in" and the guestbook button vanish behind them; the record's "Try it." has no affordance
    (nothing to click); Questions stops mid-sentence at the window's edge with no scrollbar in view.
    Open each window beside the last, cascading by 24px, give "Try it" a button, and show the
    scrollbar the chrome already draws.

## Suggested order

1 and 2 first (agent health and the owner's one job become readable at a glance), then 5 and 6
(limits visible), then 3, 4, 7, 8.
