# Agent-stream payload schemas (E7-9)

- **Spec:** `docs/specs/journal.md` §9.1 (the eight closed schemas, consistency rules 1 to 13,
  subject rules 14 and 15, copy rule 16, rule 10's batch clause) and §11 (`intent_action_mismatch`,
  `mode_event_mismatch`); DEC-168, DEC-177, DEC-252; v0.85's `DecisionMade` version 3 and rules 269
  and 270 (DEC-2388 item 10); v0.79's §9.34 `RetirementHeld`, rule 265, and stream rule 266
  (DEC-2352, E8-17).
- **Code:** `crates/mandate-journal/src/agent.rs` (`Draft::parse` takes a §9.1 schema by the stream
  type, then the rules; `verify_agent_stream` runs the two range checks after `verify_events`;
  `held_anchor` folds a range's hold anchor from a `VerifiedPrefix`, DEC-892, E12-3), and
  `src/retirements.rs` (`RetirementHeld`'s schema and rule 265 on the draft; `retirement_failure`,
  stream rule 266 over the agent's records in commit order; `retirement_held_silent`, the verifier's
  finding, DEC-2835).
- **Tests:** `crates/mandate-refcases/tests/agent_stream.rs` (one test per §9.1 family, the batches,
  the range checks, and that every expectation key is read), `crates/mandate-journal/tests/append.rs`
  (`an_agent_stream_opens_with_an_agent_subject_only`), `crates/mandate-journal/tests/catalogue.rs`
  (`a_closed_agent_stream_schema_refuses_an_unlisted_member`), `tests/held_anchor.rs` (E12-3), and
  `tests/deferred_to_binding.rs` (the `deferred_to_binding` vectors: every valid draft parses at
  version 3, every invalid one is refused with its reason at its path; `agent.rs`'s unit test pins
  version 3 as version 1's members then its two), and `tests/approval_deferrals.rs` (journal spec v0.92, DEC-2515:
  `ApprovalRequested` version 3's base drafts, rule 291's invalid drafts, and rule 292's batches,
  the cell's pre-version-3 batch of a deferring decision and a version 2 request refused), and
  `tests/retirements.rs` (the `retirements` vectors: the drafts through `Draft::parse`, and each of
  the 28 streams judged by `retirement_failure` and an independent oracle, both shown refusing a
  record broken out of every valid stream; and the 11 `silence` cases judged by
  `retirement_held_silent` and an oracle of its own, with its edges and seeded changes).
- **Reference cases:** `journal::agent_stream::*` (the vectors' `agent_stream` section of
  `fixtures/refcases/journal.json`, generated by `reference/journal/generate.py`).
- **Run:** `cargo nextest run -p mandate-journal -p mandate-refcases --run-ignored all -E
  'binary(agent_stream) | test(/agent_stream/)'`.
